\section{Experimental setup}

The experiment evaluates LensRAG on the BragsLabs HR corpus at three cumulative sizes and against the 89 gold questions of the benchmark (report Section~3.3). Retrieval-level metrics score whether the gold evidence documents reach the context (document-level recall, precision and F1); answer-level metrics score the answers themselves (exact match with a 1\% numeric tolerance, set-level F1, and, for sampled estimates, whether the gold value lies inside the 95\% interval).

\section{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item The benchmark has 89 questions (17 local, all from tier S), so the local-versus-global comparison on tiers M and L compares different kinds of documents.
\item The rule-based planner and the relevance-judge thresholds were developed on the same benchmark; only the BGE reranker threshold was chosen on a held-out split.
\item Retrieval-level metrics are a precondition for correct answers, not answer accuracy.
\item Exact match uses a 1\% tolerance, so sampled estimates can miss it while their interval still covers the gold value.
\item Local models were Qwen2.5 3B/14B/32B in place of the 72B models in Table~3.1, which do not fit in 36\,GB of memory.
\end{itemize}
