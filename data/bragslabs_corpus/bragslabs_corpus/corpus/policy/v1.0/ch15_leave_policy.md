## Chapter 15. Leave policy

Employees in every work mode receive the same leave entitlements; working from home is never a substitute for sick leave, and leave days are full days off work.

### 15.1 Leave year and entitlements

The leave year runs **1 January to 31 December**. Entitlements for confirmed, full-time employees are:

| Leave type | Days per year | Accrual | Carry forward | Encashment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Earned / privilege leave (EL) | 15 | 1.25 days per month | Up to 45 days | At separation; and up to 10 days a year on request |
| Casual leave (CL) | 8 | Credited on 1 January (pro rata for joiners) | No | No |
| Sick leave (SL) | 7 | Credited on 1 January (pro rata for joiners) | Up to 24 days | No |
| Floating holidays | 2 | Credited on 1 January | No | No |

Where State law provides a higher entitlement than this table, the State entitlement applies.

Other leave types (maternity, paternity, adoption, bereavement, marriage, caregiving and others) are in Chapter 16.

### 15.2 Earned leave

- Used for vacations, rest and planned personal needs.
- Applied at least **7 days in advance** for 3 or more days, and at least 30 days in advance for 10 or more days.
- May be combined with casual leave or holidays, but not taken for less than half a day.
- Employees are encouraged to take at least one break of 5 consecutive working days each year.
- Earned leave accumulated above 45 days on 31 December lapses, except where law provides otherwise; HR alerts employees by 30 September.
- Earned leave encashment is calculated on the basic wage plus dearness allowance.

### 15.3 Casual leave

- For unplanned or short personal needs.
- Maximum **3 consecutive days**; cannot be combined directly with earned leave without manager approval.
- Inform the manager before the start of the working day; apply in the HRMS on the day or the next working day.

### 15.4 Sick leave

- For the employee's own illness, injury, medical procedure or medical appointments.
- A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for absence of **3 or more consecutive days**, or for sick leave adjoining a holiday or weekend when a pattern is noticed.
- Employees who are unwell should rest, not work, even if they are remote. Working while on approved sick leave is not expected.
- Extended illness beyond the sick leave balance may be covered by earned leave, then by extended medical leave (15.8).

### 15.5 Applying for leave

1. Apply in the HRMS with dates and leave type.
2. Update calendar and chat status, set an out-of-office reply and hand over urgent work.
3. The manager approves or rejects within **2 working days**; leave not acted on within that time is escalated to the skip-level manager.
4. Leave may be refused or rescheduled only for genuine business reasons, with the reason recorded. Managers should approve planned leave fairly across the team, with priority given to school holiday periods for employees with children on a rotating basis.

### 15.6 Leave during notice period

Leave during the notice period needs manager approval and is generally limited to sick leave and pre-approved planned leave. Earned leave balance may be set off against the notice period only with the Company's approval (Chapter 34).

### 15.7 Leave for new joiners and leavers

Earned, casual and sick leave are credited pro rata from the date of joining, rounded to the nearest half day. At separation, earned leave availed in excess of accrual is recovered from the final settlement.

### 15.8 Extended medical leave

An employee with a serious illness or injury who has exhausted sick and earned leave may receive up to **60 days of paid extended medical leave** once in every three years, on the recommendation of a medical practitioner and approval of the CHRO. Further unpaid leave of up to 6 months may be granted. Medical details are kept confidential (Chapter 31).

### 15.9 Leave without pay (LWP)

LWP may be granted for up to 3 months for genuine reasons, such as higher studies, family emergencies or personal circumstances, after all paid leave is exhausted. LWP over 30 days needs CHRO approval. During LWP, the employee does not accrue earned leave, and group insurance continues only if the employee pays the premium for the period.

### 15.10 Sabbatical

Confirmed employees with at least **5 years of continuous service** may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of 1 to 6 months, for study, social service, caregiving or rest. The Company will try to hold the employee's role or offer an equivalent role on return. Sabbaticals are approved by the business unit head and CHRO.

### 15.11 Leave for public duty

- **Voting:** paid time off to vote in national, state and local elections, as required by law.
- **Jury, witness or court summons:** paid leave for the days the employee must attend, on production of the summons.
- **Blood donation:** half a day of paid leave on the day of donation.
- **Disaster relief and civil defence:** up to 5 days of paid leave a year for registered volunteers.

### 15.12 Misuse of leave

False medical certificates, applying sick leave for a holiday, or taking other employment during leave is misconduct and handled under Chapter 33.
