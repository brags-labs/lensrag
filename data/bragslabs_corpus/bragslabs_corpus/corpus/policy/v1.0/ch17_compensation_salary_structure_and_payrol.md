## Chapter 17. Compensation, salary structure and payroll

The Company pays competitively within published salary bands, pays the same for the same role and level in every work mode, and pays salaries by the **last working day of each month**.

### 17.1 Compensation philosophy

- Pay is set by role, level, skills, performance and market benchmarks.
- Salary bands are reviewed annually against market surveys for the IT industry and published to employees by level.
- Work mode does not change the salary band. Location may affect pay through a location factor attached to the base location (10.3).
- Pay decisions are free of bias; an annual pay-equity review checks gender and other gaps (Chapter 3).

### 17.2 Levels and bands

| Level | Typical titles | Band review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Trainee, Associate Engineer | Annual |
| L2 | Software Engineer, Analyst | Annual |
| L3 | Senior Software Engineer, Senior Analyst | Annual |
| L4 | Lead Engineer, Team Lead, Consultant | Annual |
| L5 | Engineering Manager, Senior Consultant, Architect | Annual |
| L6 | Senior Manager, Principal Engineer | Annual |
| L7 | Director, Senior Principal | Annual |
| L8 | Senior Director, Vice President | Annual |
| L9 | Senior Vice President and above | Board-approved |

The band ranges (minimum, midpoint, maximum) for each level are in the compensation guide on the intranet.

### 17.3 Salary structure

| Component | Description | Typical share of fixed pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic pay | Core salary; base for statutory calculations | 50% |
| Dearness allowance | Where applicable | Included in wages |
| House rent allowance (HRA) | Tax benefit as per Income-tax rules | Up to 50% of basic (metro) / 40% (non-metro) |
| Special allowance | Balancing component | Remainder |
| Leave travel allowance | Tax benefit as per rules | As per flexible benefits plan |
| Flexible benefits | Meal cards, telephone and internet, books, fuel, as chosen by the employee | As per plan |
| Employer PF contribution | 12% of wages | Part of CTC |
| Gratuity provision | Statutory gratuity | Part of CTC |
| Group insurance premium | Health, life and accident cover | Part of CTC |
| Variable pay | Annual performance bonus | Separate from fixed pay |

**Wage rule under the Labour Codes.** Basic pay, dearness allowance and retaining allowance ("wages") make up at least **50% of total remuneration**. If excluded components exceed 50%, the excess is added back to wages for computing PF, gratuity and other statutory benefits.

### 17.4 Minimum wages

No employee is paid less than the applicable minimum wage or the floor wage notified under the Code on Wages for their location and category. HR checks this every April and October.

### 17.5 Payroll process

| Step | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance and leave cut-off | 20th of the month |
| Variable inputs (allowances, overtime, reimbursements) | By 22nd |
| Payroll processing and checks | 23rd – 26th |
| Salary credit | On or before the last working day of the month |
| Payslips published in the HRMS | Within 2 days of salary credit |

In no case is salary paid later than the **7th of the following month**, as required by law.

### 17.6 Deductions

Only deductions permitted by law are made: income tax (TDS), employee PF and ESI contributions, professional tax, labour welfare fund, recovery of advances and loans, recovery for loss of Company property as permitted, deductions for absence, and any amounts authorised in writing by the employee. Total deductions in a month do not exceed **50%** of wages, as provided by law.

### 17.7 Income tax

- Employees choose the old or new tax regime and submit investment declarations in the HRMS by 30 April, with proofs by 31 January.
- TDS is deducted monthly; Form 16 is issued by **15 June** each year.
- Perquisites, such as Company-provided accommodation on assignments, are taxed as per rules.

### 17.8 Variable pay

Eligible employees receive an annual variable pay linked to Company performance and individual rating:

| Rating | Individual multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| 5 Exceptional | 150% |
| 4 Exceeds expectations | 120% |
| 3 Meets expectations | 100% |
| 2 Partly meets | 50% |
| 1 Does not meet | 0% |

The Company performance multiplier (0–120%) is set by the Board. Variable pay is paid with the June salary, pro rata for employees who joined during the year. Employees who resign or are terminated before the payout date are not eligible unless the plan states otherwise or law requires.

### 17.9 Annual salary review

Increments are effective **1 April** and communicated by 30 June, based on rating, position in band, market movement and budget. Employees who joined after 1 January are reviewed in the next cycle. Promotions carry a separate increase (Chapter 23).

### 17.10 Salary advance and loans

- **Salary advance:** confirmed employees may take one interest-free advance of up to one month's net salary a year, recovered over up to 6 months.
- **Emergency loan:** for medical emergencies, natural disasters or a family death, up to 3 months' net salary, interest-free, recovered over up to 12 months, with CHRO approval.

### 17.11 Confidentiality of pay

Employees may discuss their own pay if they choose. HR, payroll and managers must keep individual salary information confidential and access it only for their duties.

### 17.12 Payroll queries

Payroll queries are raised through the HR helpdesk. Errors in the Company's favour are corrected in the next payroll; underpayments are paid within 7 working days of confirmation.
