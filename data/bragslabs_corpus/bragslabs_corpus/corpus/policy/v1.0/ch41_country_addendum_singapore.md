## Chapter 41. Country addendum: Singapore

This addendum applies to employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd.; the Employment Act covers all employees, with extra protections on hours and overtime for lower-salaried staff, and tripartite guidelines set expected standards on fair employment and flexible work.

### 41.1 Key laws and guidelines

Employment Act 1968; Child Development Co-Savings Act; Central Provident Fund Act; Retirement and Re-employment Act; Employment of Foreign Manpower Act; Work Injury Compensation Act 2019; Workplace Safety and Health Act; Protection from Harassment Act; Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA); Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices; Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests; Tripartite Advisory on Managing Excess Manpower and Responsible Retrenchment.

### 41.2 Employment terms and work passes

- Key employment terms are given in writing within 14 days of starting; itemised payslips are issued with each salary payment.
- Foreign employees must hold a valid work pass (Employment Pass, S Pass or other) before starting work. Employment Pass applications follow the COMPASS framework, and roles are advertised on MyCareersFuture where required.

### 41.3 Working hours and overtime

- Standard week of 40 hours, Monday to Friday.
- Employees covered by Part IV of the Employment Act (workmen earning up to the statutory threshold and non-workmen earning up to the statutory threshold) work no more than 44 hours a week, are paid overtime at 1.5× the hourly basic rate, and may not work more than 72 hours of overtime a month.
- Other employees' hours and overtime follow their contract.

### 41.4 Leave (Company policy)

| Leave | Statutory | Company policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual leave | 7 days, rising by 1 day a year to 14 | 14 days, rising to 17 days after 5 years |
| Outpatient sick leave | 14 days after 3 months' service | 14 days from day one |
| Hospitalisation leave | Up to 60 days (including outpatient days) | As statutory, from day one |
| Childcare leave | 6 days a year (child under 7, Singapore citizen) | As statutory, extended to all employees with children under 7 |
| Extended childcare leave | 2 days a year (child aged 7–12) | As statutory |
| Unpaid infant care leave | 12 days a year (child under 2) | As statutory |
| Public holidays | 11 gazetted public holidays | As statutory; a day off in lieu when a holiday falls on a Saturday |

### 41.5 Parental leave

- **Maternity:** 16 weeks of Government-Paid Maternity Leave for eligible mothers of Singapore citizen children (12 weeks under the Employment Act otherwise); the Company tops up to full pay for 16 weeks for all mothers.
- **Adoption:** 12 weeks of Government-Paid Adoption Leave for eligible mothers.

### 41.6 Flexible work arrangement requests

In line with the Tripartite Guidelines, employees who have completed probation may make formal written requests for flexible work arrangements (flexi-place, flexi-time or flexi-load). The Company responds in writing within **2 months**, considers the request properly, and gives reasons if it rejects it.

### 41.7 Pay, CPF and benefits

- Salary is paid at least monthly, within 7 days after the end of the salary period; overtime pay within 14 days.
- **CPF:** contributions for Singapore citizens and permanent residents at the rates for their age band (for employees aged 55 and below, 17% employer and 20% employee of wages up to the CPF wage ceiling).
- Skills Development Levy paid for all employees.
- Group hospitalisation and surgical insurance, outpatient medical and dental benefits, group term life at 3× annual salary, and EAP.
- Work injury compensation insurance for all employees, as required.

### 41.8 Retirement and re-employment

The Company does not dismiss employees because of age before the statutory retirement age, and offers re-employment to eligible employees up to the statutory re-employment age, as set under the Retirement and Re-employment Act.

### 41.9 Termination and retrenchment

| Service | Statutory minimum notice (if contract is silent) |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 26 weeks | 1 day |
| 26 weeks to less than 2 years | 1 week |
| 2 years to less than 5 years | 2 weeks |
| 5 years or more | 4 weeks |

Contractual notice: 1 month during and after probation for L1–L3; 2 months for L4+. Retrenchment follows the Tripartite Advisory, with retrenchment benefits of 1 month of salary per year of service for employees with 2 or more years' service, and Mandatory Retrenchment Notification to MOM where required. Wrongful dismissal claims may be brought at the Tribunal for Employment Claims.

### 41.10 Fair employment and harassment

The Company follows the Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices and the Workplace Fairness Act once in force, including a grievance-handling process and protection against retaliation. Harassment complaints are handled by the Singapore Respect at Work Committee; serious harassment may also be an offence under the Protection from Harassment Act.

### 41.11 Data protection

Employee data is handled under the PDPA. The Data Protection Officer for the Singapore entity, whose business contact information is published as required, is Tan Wei Ling (dpo.sg@bragslabs.com).
