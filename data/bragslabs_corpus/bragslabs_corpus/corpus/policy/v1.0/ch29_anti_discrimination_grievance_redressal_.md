## Chapter 29. Anti-discrimination, grievance redressal and whistleblowing

Every employee has a safe, confidential and time-bound way to raise a workplace grievance or report wrongdoing, from any work mode, without fear of retaliation.

### 29.1 Which channel to use

| Concern | Channel |
| --- | --- |
| Sexual harassment | Internal Committee (Chapter 28) |
| Pay, leave, workload, work mode, assignments, treatment by manager or colleagues, discrimination | Grievance redressal (29.2–29.5) |
| Fraud, bribery, data misuse, falsified records, safety risks, legal violations, serious policy breaches | Whistleblower channel (29.6–29.10) |
| Disability-related issues | Liaison Officer and grievance redressal |
| Data protection concerns about your own data | Grievance Officer for data protection (Chapter 31) |
| Immediate danger to life or safety | Emergency helpline, then manager and HR (Chapter 32) |

### 29.2 Grievance redressal: principles

- Grievances are resolved at the lowest appropriate level, quickly and fairly.
- Both sides are heard before any decision.
- Information is shared only with those who need it to resolve the grievance.
- Raising a genuine grievance never leads to adverse treatment.

### 29.3 Grievance procedure

| Stage | Raised with | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Informal | Immediate manager (or skip-level manager if the grievance is about the manager) | 7 working days |
| 2. Formal | HRBP, in writing through the HRMS grievance form | 7 working days for acknowledgement and meeting; 15 working days for resolution |
| 3. Committee | Grievance Redressal Committee | 30 days from the reference to the Committee |
| 4. Appeal | CHRO (for decisions of the Committee where permitted by law) | 15 working days |

Grievance meetings may be held in person or by video. The employee may be accompanied by a colleague of their choice.

### 29.4 Grievance Redressal Committee

In line with the Industrial Relations Code, each establishment with 20 or more workers has a Grievance Redressal Committee:

- with equal numbers of members from the employer and the workers, up to a maximum of 10;
- with a chairperson selected alternately from the employer side and the worker side each year;
- with adequate representation of women, in proportion to the women workers in the establishment;
- which completes its proceedings within 30 days of receiving an application.

A worker who is not satisfied with the Committee's decision may pursue the remedies available under the Industrial Relations Code.

### 29.5 Group grievances

Where several employees share a grievance (for example, about a team's working hours or transport), they may raise it together through a representative. HR responds to the group and communicates the outcome to all concerned.

### 29.6 Whistleblower policy: purpose

The Company encourages employees, contractors, vendors, clients and others to report suspected wrongdoing ("protected disclosures") so it can be investigated and stopped. The policy also serves as the vigil mechanism required under the Companies Act, 2013, where applicable.

### 29.7 What to report

- Fraud, theft, embezzlement or misuse of Company funds or assets.
- Bribery, kickbacks or improper payments.
- Falsification of financial records, timesheets, billing or client reports.
- Breaches of data protection, information security or client confidentiality.
- Violation of law, including labour, tax, environmental and competition law.
- Unsafe practices that endanger health or safety.
- Discrimination, harassment or abuse of authority not covered by the IC process.
- Retaliation against anyone who raised a concern.
- Deliberate concealment of any of the above.

The whistleblower channel is not for routine personal grievances, which go through 29.3.

### 29.8 How to report

- Email the Ethics Committee at **ethics@bragslabs.com**.
- Use the 24x7 independent ethics hotline (phone and web) at **+1 800 555 0199 (US and Canada), 000 800 919 1234 (India) or ethicsline.bragslabs.com**, which allows anonymous reports.
- Write to the Chief Ethics Officer by post at BragsLabs, Ethics Office, 200 Summit Avenue, Suite 1400, Boston, MA 02110, USA.
- In exceptional cases, or if the concern involves senior management, write directly to the Chairperson of the Audit Committee at audit.chair@bragslabs.com.

Reports should include as much detail as possible: what happened, when, where, who was involved, and any evidence. Anonymous reports are investigated if they contain enough information to act on.

### 29.9 Investigation

1. The Ethics Committee acknowledges the report within **3 working days** (where the reporter can be contacted).
2. A preliminary review within **15 days** decides whether a full investigation is needed.
3. Investigations are led by an independent investigator from Internal Audit, Legal, HR or an external firm, depending on the matter, and are completed within **60 days** where possible.
4. Those being investigated are informed of the allegations and given a chance to respond, unless this would prejudice the investigation.
5. The Committee decides on actions and informs the reporter of the outcome, as far as confidentiality permits.
6. Serious matters and summaries are reported to the Audit Committee every quarter.

### 29.10 Protection of whistleblowers

- The identity of the whistleblower is kept confidential and disclosed only where required by law or with their consent.
- No one may retaliate against a whistleblower, through dismissal, demotion, transfer, denial of increments or promotions, harassment or any other adverse action. Retaliation is gross misconduct.
- A whistleblower who reports in good faith is protected even if the concern turns out to be unfounded.
- Knowingly making false or malicious allegations is misconduct.
- A whistleblower who was involved in the wrongdoing is not protected from action for their own part, but voluntary reporting is considered as a mitigating factor.

### 29.11 Records

All grievances and disclosures, investigation reports and decisions are recorded confidentially and kept for at least 8 years.
