## Chapter 24. Learning and development

Every employee is expected to complete at least **40 hours of learning a year** and has an individual learning budget, with programmes delivered online, in classrooms and on the job so that all work modes have equal access.

### 24.1 Learning entitlements

| Level | Annual learning budget | Minimum learning hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1–L3 | ₹20,000 | 40 hours |
| L4–L6 | ₹30,000 | 40 hours |
| L7 and above | ₹60,000 | 30 hours, plus leadership programmes |

The budget may be used for courses, certifications, conferences and books related to the employee's current or next role. Unused budget does not carry forward.

### 24.2 Types of learning

- **Mandatory compliance training:** code of conduct, POSH, information security, data protection, anti-bribery and health and safety, completed at joining and refreshed annually.
- **Technical training:** cloud, data, AI, security, programming languages, testing, architecture and domain skills.
- **Professional skills:** communication, client management, project management, writing and presentation.
- **Leadership development:** first-time manager programme (mandatory within 6 months of becoming a manager), mid-level and senior leadership programmes.
- **Learning on the job:** stretch projects, rotations, shadowing and mentoring.
- **Self-paced learning:** licences to online learning platforms for all employees.

### 24.3 Access across work modes

- All mandatory and core programmes are available online.
- In-person programmes are offered at multiple locations; remote employees who are nominated receive travel support.
- Live virtual sessions are recorded where possible and scheduled in core hours.
- Learning time is working time: employees may block up to **4 hours a week** for learning, agreed with the manager.

### 24.4 Certifications

The Company sponsors approved certifications relevant to the role (for example, cloud provider, security, project management and agile certifications):

1. The employee applies through the learning portal with manager approval.
2. The Company pays the exam fee directly or reimburses it on passing.
3. Each certification may be sponsored for up to two attempts.
4. Paid leave of up to **2 days** is given for each exam.

### 24.5 Higher education support

Confirmed employees with at least 2 years' service and a rating of 3 or above may apply for support for part-time or online degree programmes relevant to their career path:

- up to 50% of fees, to a maximum of ₹5,00,000 over the programme;
- examination leave of up to 5 days a year;
- flexible hours where possible.

### 24.6 Training agreements and repayment

Where the Company spends more than ₹50,000 on a single programme or certification for an employee, the employee signs a training agreement. If they resign within 12 months of completing the programme, they repay a pro rata share of the cost, reducing monthly. The repayment is reasonable and proportionate to the actual cost and is waived if the Company ends the employment for reasons other than misconduct.

### 24.7 Mentoring and coaching

- Every employee may request a mentor from outside their reporting line through the mentoring platform.
- New managers and high-potential employees receive structured mentoring.
- Senior leaders may receive external executive coaching, approved by the CHRO.
- Reverse mentoring pairs senior leaders with early-career employees on technology and culture topics.

### 24.8 Conferences and knowledge sharing

Employees may attend external conferences with approval and present at them with the Company's permission. After an external programme, the employee shares key learnings with the team. Internal communities of practice, tech talks and hackathons are open to all, with hybrid participation.

### 24.9 Individual development plan (IDP)

Each employee agrees an IDP with the manager during goal setting, listing 2–3 development goals, the learning activities to reach them, and target dates. Progress is reviewed at the mid-year and annual reviews.

### 24.10 Responsibilities

- **Employees** own their development, complete mandatory learning on time, and apply what they learn.
- **Managers** release time for learning, discuss development in one-to-ones, and create on-the-job learning opportunities.
- **The L&D team** designs programmes, manages vendors and budgets, and reports learning hours and effectiveness quarterly.
