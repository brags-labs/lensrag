## Chapter 25. Reward and recognition

The Company recognises contributions promptly and visibly through peer recognition, spot awards, quarterly and annual awards, and long-service awards, delivered in ways that reach remote and off-site employees as well as those in offices.

### 25.1 Principles

- Recognise specific behaviours and results, soon after they happen.
- Recognise fairly across teams, levels, locations and work modes.
- Combine public appreciation with meaningful rewards.
- Make it easy for anyone to recognise anyone.

### 25.2 Recognition programmes

| Programme | Who nominates | Frequency | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Peer recognition ("Kudos") | Any employee | Anytime | Points redeemable on the rewards platform; each employee has 500 points a quarter to give |
| Spot award | Manager | Anytime | ₹2,500–10,000 in points or vouchers |
| Team award | Manager or skip-level | Quarterly | Team celebration budget of ₹1,500 per person |
| Client hero award | Delivery manager, based on client appreciation | Quarterly | ₹25,000 and certificate |
| Innovation award | Any employee; judged by a panel | Half-yearly | ₹1,00,000; idea fast-tracked for funding |
| Annual excellence awards | Business unit leadership | Annual | Trophy, ₹2,00,000, CEO recognition |
| Long-service awards | Automatic | On milestones | See 25.4 |

### 25.3 Delivering recognition in distributed teams

- Awards are announced in all-hands meetings and on the intranet, never only in office events.
- Physical awards and gifts are shipped to remote and off-site employees.
- Team celebrations offer equivalent options: an in-office lunch, a meal voucher for remote members, or a virtual activity.
- Recognition data is reviewed by work mode each quarter to check fairness.

### 25.4 Long-service awards

| Years of service | Award |
| --- | --- |
| 5 years | Certificate, ₹10,000 and one extra day of earned leave |
| 10 years | Certificate, ₹25,000 and two extra days of earned leave |
| 15 years | Certificate, ₹50,000 and a gift |
| 20 years | Certificate, ₹75,000 and a family holiday voucher |
| 25 years | Certificate, ₹1,00,000 and recognition by the CEO |

### 25.5 Tax

Cash awards and vouchers above the tax-exempt limit are taxable as perquisites and are processed through payroll.

### 25.6 Recognition and performance

Recognition received during the year is visible to managers during performance reviews as supporting evidence, but recognition counts are not used as a performance metric.

### 25.7 Misuse

Trading recognition points, nominating oneself through others, or recognising in exchange for favours is misconduct. Points obtained through misuse are cancelled.
