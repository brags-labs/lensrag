## Chapter 32. Health, safety and emergencies

The Company provides a safe workplace in its offices, sets safety standards for home workplaces, checks client sites before deployment, and supports travelling employees 24x7, in line with the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020.

### 32.1 Responsibilities

| Who | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Company | Safe premises and equipment, risk assessments, training, emergency plans, first aid, incident investigation |
| Facilities and Safety team | Fire safety, building safety, drills, inspections, safety committee |
| Managers | Ensure team members know safety procedures, report hazards, check on remote and travelling staff in emergencies |
| Employees | Follow safety rules, use equipment properly, report hazards and incidents, keep the home workplace safe |

### 32.2 Office safety

- Fire detection, alarms, extinguishers, sprinklers and emergency exits are maintained and inspected as required by law.
- Fire drills are conducted every **6 months**; all employees present must participate.
- Floor fire wardens and first-aiders are trained on every floor and shift, including night shifts.
- First-aid kits and automated external defibrillators (AEDs) are available at marked points.
- Electrical, lift and HVAC systems are maintained by certified vendors.
- A **Safety Committee** with employer and employee representatives meets quarterly where required by law.

### 32.3 Home workplace safety

Hybrid and remote employees complete a home workplace self-assessment at joining and annually, confirming:

- safe electrical wiring and sockets, no overloaded extension boards, and surge protection;
- a clear, tidy workspace without trip hazards from cables;
- adequate lighting and ventilation;
- an ergonomic setup (Chapter 14);
- a working smoke detector where possible, and knowledge of the home's exits;
- access to a first-aid kit.

The Company may arrange a virtual ergonomic or safety review on request. Injuries that occur at the home workplace while performing work during working hours must be reported like any workplace injury (32.7).

### 32.4 Client-site safety

Before deploying employees, the delivery manager confirms that the client site has basic safety arrangements, emergency procedures and POSH compliance. Employees follow the client's safety induction and rules, and report any unsafe condition to the client contact and their delivery manager.

### 32.5 Travel and field safety

- All travel is registered in the travel safety system, which tracks advisories and allows the Company to locate travellers in an emergency.
- Employees receive alerts about severe weather, unrest or health risks at their destination.
- The 24x7 assistance provider gives medical advice, security help and evacuation support.
- Field employees follow check-in procedures (Chapter 11) and must not continue a visit where they feel unsafe.

### 32.6 Emergency response

| Emergency | First action | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Raise the alarm, evacuate by the nearest exit, do not use lifts | Assemble at the assembly point; report to the fire warden |
| Medical emergency | Call the first-aider or emergency number (112), keep the person safe | Inform Facilities and HR |
| Earthquake | Drop, cover and hold; move away from windows | Evacuate when shaking stops |
| Flood or severe weather | Follow HR and local authority advisories; do not travel unnecessarily | Work from home or take special leave as declared |
| Security threat | Follow security staff instructions; lock down or evacuate | Report to Security and the manager |
| Emergency while remote or travelling | Get to safety, call local emergency services | Contact the Company emergency helpline and manager |

**Company emergency helpline:** +1 617 555 0100 (global) or 1800 425 7788 (India toll-free), available 24x7.

### 32.7 Reporting incidents and injuries

1. Report any injury, near miss, dangerous occurrence or occupational illness to the manager and the Safety team within **24 hours**, using the incident form in the HRMS.
2. Get medical attention as needed; the Company's medical tie-ups and insurance support are available.
3. The Safety team investigates serious incidents and recommends corrective actions.
4. Reportable accidents are notified to authorities as required by law.
5. Employee's compensation is processed as per Chapter 18.

### 32.8 Business continuity and crisis management

The Company has a business continuity plan covering office outages, pandemics, natural disasters and cyber incidents. A Crisis Management Team led by the COO coordinates response, communication and employee safety. During a crisis, HR runs a **safety check** through the HRMS app, SMS and calls, and every employee in the affected area must respond to confirm their status.

### 32.9 Public health

During outbreaks of infectious disease, the Company follows government guidance on hygiene, vaccination, testing, isolation and office occupancy. Employees with infectious illness should not come to the office or client site and should take sick leave or work from home if well enough.

### 32.10 Fitness for work

Employees must not work when unfit because of illness, fatigue, medication or substances where this could endanger themselves or others. Employees in safety-critical roles may be asked for a medical fitness certificate.
