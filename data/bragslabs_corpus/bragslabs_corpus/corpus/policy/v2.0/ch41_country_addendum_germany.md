## Chapter 41. Country addendum: Germany

This addendum applies to employees of BragsLabs GmbH; German employment law is highly protective, and works council co-determination rights mean several global policies (working time, monitoring, hybrid work rules) are implemented in Germany through works agreements where a works council exists.

### 41.1 Key laws

Civil Code (BGB); Working Time Act (Arbeitszeitgesetz); Federal Leave Act (Bundesurlaubsgesetz); Continued Remuneration Act (Entgeltfortzahlungsgesetz); Maternity Protection Act (Mutterschutzgesetz); Federal Parental Allowance and Parental Leave Act (BEEG); Protection Against Dismissal Act (Kündigungsschutzgesetz); Works Constitution Act (Betriebsverfassungsgesetz); General Equal Treatment Act (AGG); Part-Time and Fixed-Term Employment Act; Minimum Wage Act; Pay Transparency Act; EU GDPR and Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG); Whistleblower Protection Act (HinSchG); Evidence Act (Nachweisgesetz).

### 41.2 Works council

Where a works council (Betriebsrat) exists, it has co-determination rights over, among other things, working time arrangements, overtime, mobile and remote work rules, technical systems capable of monitoring performance or behaviour, leave scheduling principles and pay structures. Global policies in these areas take effect in Germany only through a works agreement (Betriebsvereinbarung) where required. The works council must be consulted before every dismissal; a dismissal without consultation is invalid.

### 41.3 Working time and time recording

- Standard week of 40 hours, Monday to Friday.
- Maximum 8 hours a day, extendable to 10 hours if the average over 6 months does not exceed 8 hours a day.
- At least 11 hours' rest between working days; breaks of 30 minutes after 6 hours and 45 minutes after 9 hours.
- Sunday and public holiday work is generally prohibited.
- Working time is recorded for all employees, including remote employees, as required by German case law, using the Company time-recording system.
- Overtime is compensated by time off or pay as set out in the employment contract or works agreement.

### 41.4 Holidays (Company policy)

**27 working days' paid holiday** a year on a 5-day week, above the statutory 20 days, plus the public holidays of the federal state (Bundesland) where the employee works. Holiday should be taken in the calendar year; statutory holiday carried over must be used by 31 March of the following year, and the Company reminds employees in good time so that holiday does not lapse unknowingly.

### 41.5 Sickness

Employees notify their manager on the first day of illness. A medical certificate (electronic eAU retrieved by the Company from the health insurer) is required from the fourth day. The Company continues full pay for up to **6 weeks** per illness; afterwards, statutory health insurance pays sick pay (Krankengeld), and the Company may top up for a further 6 weeks, paying the difference between Krankengeld and net salary.

### 41.6 Family leave

- **Maternity protection:** 6 weeks before and 8 weeks after birth (12 weeks after premature or multiple births), with maternity pay from the health insurer and a Company top-up to full net pay. Dismissal protection applies from pregnancy until 4 months after birth.
- **Parental leave (Elternzeit):** up to 3 years per parent per child, with state parental allowance (Elterngeld); dismissal protection during parental leave.
- **Company paid partner leave:** 2 weeks at full pay (Chapter 37).
- **Care leave:** short-term leave of up to 10 days for acute care situations, and longer care leave under the Care Leave and Family Care Leave Acts.

### 41.7 Pay and benefits

- Paid monthly in arrears; wage tax, solidarity surcharge where applicable, church tax where applicable, and social insurance contributions (pension, health, long-term care, unemployment) are deducted.
- Statutory minimum wage always met.
- Company pension (betriebliche Altersversorgung) with an employer subsidy of 15% on deferred compensation, or as agreed.
- Benefits such as a public transport ticket subsidy, job bike and meal allowance, as permitted under tax rules.

### 41.8 Remote and mobile work

Mobile work and home office are provided under a works agreement or individual agreement covering working time recording, availability, equipment, costs, data protection and occupational safety. The employer's occupational health and safety duties continue for home-based work, including a risk assessment of the home workplace.

### 41.9 Termination

- Dismissals must be in writing with a wet-ink signature; email or electronic signature is not sufficient.
- **Statutory notice by employer:** 4 weeks to the 15th or end of a calendar month, rising with length of service to 7 months after 20 years.
- After 6 months' service, in establishments with more than 10 employees, a dismissal must be socially justified (for conduct, personal or operational reasons) under the Protection Against Dismissal Act. Conduct-related dismissals generally require a prior written warning (Abmahnung).
- Special protection applies to pregnant employees, employees on parental leave, severely disabled employees (approval of the Integration Office needed) and works council members.
- Mass dismissals require notification to the Federal Employment Agency and consultation with the works council.
- Post-employment non-competes are only valid if the Company pays compensation of at least 50% of the last remuneration for the restricted period (maximum 2 years).

### 41.10 Equal treatment and complaints

Under the General Equal Treatment Act, the Company has a complaints office (Beschwerdestelle) for discrimination and harassment complaints: Katrin Weber, HR Berlin (beschwerdestelle@bragslabs.com). Complaints are investigated and employees protected against victimisation. Whistleblowing reports may be made through the internal reporting channel under the Whistleblower Protection Act, which also allows reports to external government channels.

### 41.11 Data protection

Employee data is processed under the GDPR and section 26 BDSG. Monitoring and system introductions are agreed with the works council where it exists. The data protection officer is Dr. Elena Fischer (datenschutz@bragslabs.com).
