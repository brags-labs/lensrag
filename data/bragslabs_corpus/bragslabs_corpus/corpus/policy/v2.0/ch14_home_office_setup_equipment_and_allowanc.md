## Chapter 14. Home office setup, equipment and allowances

The Company equips hybrid and remote employees to work safely and productively from home through issued equipment, a one-time setup allowance and a monthly connectivity allowance.

### 14.1 Entitlements by work mode

| Item | On-site | Hybrid | Remote | Off-site |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Laptop, charger, bag | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Headset with noise-cancelling microphone | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| External monitor (24 inch) | Office desk | Office desk; one for home on request | Yes, shipped home | On request |
| Keyboard, mouse, laptop stand | Office desk | Yes, for home | Yes | On request |
| One-time home office setup allowance | No | ₹8,000 | ₹20,000 | No |
| Monthly internet and phone allowance | No | ₹800 | ₹1,200 | ₹1,000 if client site requires hotspot |
| Co-working space | No | No | Up to 5 days a month on request | No |
| Power backup support | No | No | One-time ₹5,000 for UPS | No |

Allowances are paid through payroll and are subject to tax as per the Income-tax rules. Employees in their notice period continue to receive monthly allowances until the last working day.

### 14.2 Home office setup allowance

- Used for an ergonomic chair, desk, lighting and similar items for the home workplace.
- Claimed once every **3 years**, with bills, within 90 days of becoming eligible.
- Items bought with the allowance belong to the employee.
- If the employee leaves within 12 months of claiming, a pro rata amount may be recovered from the final settlement.

### 14.3 Ergonomic standards

To prevent strain and injury, the home workplace should have:

- an adjustable chair with lumbar support, with feet flat on the floor or a footrest;
- the top of the screen at or slightly below eye level, about an arm's length away;
- keyboard and mouse at elbow height, with wrists straight;
- good lighting without glare on the screen;
- room to move, and a routine of standing or stretching at least once an hour.

The Company provides an online ergonomic self-assessment and, on request, a video consultation with an ergonomics specialist. Employees with medical conditions may request specific equipment under Chapter 3.

### 14.4 Connectivity requirements

| Requirement | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Primary connection | Wired or Wi-Fi broadband of at least 50 Mbps download |
| Backup | Mobile hotspot with at least 4G service |
| Home Wi-Fi security | WPA2 or WPA3 encryption; default router password changed |
| Public Wi-Fi | Only with the Company VPN; never for restricted data |

If connectivity fails, the employee informs the manager immediately, switches to backup, or moves to the nearest office or co-working space.

### 14.5 Use and care of Company equipment

- Company equipment is for business use. Limited personal use, such as reading the news during a break, is acceptable if it does not breach Chapter 30.
- Family members and others must not use Company equipment.
- Equipment must be kept secure, out of sight when unattended, and away from food, liquids and children.
- Loss or theft is reported to IT and the manager within **2 hours**, and to the police where theft is involved, with a copy of the police report sent to IT.
- The employee may be asked to bear the repair or replacement cost (up to the depreciated value) if damage or loss is due to negligence.

### 14.6 Repairs and replacement

IT supports remote employees through the helpdesk, remote support sessions and courier pickup and drop of faulty equipment. A replacement laptop is shipped within **2 working days** for critical failures. If the employee lives near an office, they may also exchange equipment in person.

### 14.7 Printing and stationery

Printing at home is discouraged and is not permitted for confidential or restricted information. Where printing is essential, it is done in the office. Basic stationery is provided in offices; remote employees may claim up to ₹500 a quarter for stationery.

### 14.8 Utilities

The connectivity allowance is intended to cover internet and the additional phone and electricity costs of working from home. No separate reimbursement is made for electricity, rent or household expenses.

### 14.9 Return of equipment

On changing work mode, ending a deployment or leaving the Company, the employee returns all equipment issued for home use, either in person at an office or through the Company's courier pickup, by the last working day. Unreturned items are recovered at their depreciated value from the final settlement, as permitted by law.
