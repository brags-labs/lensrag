## Chapter 12. Working hours, shifts, flexi-time and overtime

The standard working week is **5 days, Monday to Friday, 9 hours a day including a 1-hour break**, in every work mode; no employee works more than 48 hours in a week except as permitted overtime.

### 12.1 Standard working hours

| Item | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Working days | Monday to Friday (rostered teams may have different weekly offs) |
| Daily hours | 9 hours including a 1-hour meal and rest break (8 hours of work) |
| Weekly hours | 40 hours of work; maximum 48 hours including permitted overtime |
| Rest interval | At least 30 minutes after 5 continuous hours of work |
| Weekly rest | At least one full day of rest in every 7 days |
| Spread-over | Working day, including breaks, not to exceed 10.5 hours (or the limit under State law) |

### 12.2 Shifts

| Shift | Hours (IST) | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | 9:30 am – 6:30 pm | Default for all work modes |
| Early | 7:00 am – 4:00 pm | APAC and early support teams |
| Afternoon | 12:00 noon – 9:00 pm | Teams overlapping with Europe |
| Late | 2:00 pm – 11:00 pm | Teams overlapping with the US East Coast |
| Night | 9:00 pm – 6:00 am | 24x7 support and operations |

Rosters are published at least **7 days in advance**. Shift changes need at least 48 hours' notice, except in emergencies. Employees on rotating shifts do not work night shifts for more than 2 consecutive weeks without a break week, and have at least 11 hours of rest between shifts.

### 12.3 Shift allowance

| Shift | Allowance per day worked |
| --- | --- |
| Afternoon | ₹200 |
| Late | ₹300 |
| Night | ₹500 |
| Weekend or holiday on-call (per day, on standby) | ₹1,000 |

Shift allowance is paid in every work mode, including remote, because it compensates for unsocial hours rather than travel.

### 12.4 Women employees on late and night shifts

Women employees may work night shifts (between 7:00 pm and 6:00 am, or the hours set by State law) only with their written consent. For on-site and hybrid work in these hours, the Company provides:

- door-to-door Company transport, with a security guard or escort where required, and GPS tracking;
- a verified driver; the woman employee is not the first to be picked up or the last to be dropped where avoidable;
- adequate lighting, security and safe rest areas at the office;
- a confirmation call or app check-in after drop-off.

Women employees may withdraw consent for night shifts with 15 days' notice, without any adverse consequence.

### 12.5 Flexi-time

For general-shift employees in any work mode:

- start time may vary between **8:30 am and 10:30 am**, with end time adjusted to complete the 9-hour day;
- employees must be available during core collaboration hours of **11:00 am – 4:00 pm**;
- the manager may require fixed hours for client-facing, support or time-critical roles.

Flexi-time arrangements are agreed in the team working agreement and shown on the employee's calendar.

### 12.6 Compressed and part-time work

- **Compressed week:** with HR approval, employees may work 4 days of 10 hours in roles where it suits the business, for a trial period of 3 months.
- **Part-time:** employees may request to work 50–80% of standard hours for a defined period (for example, after parental leave, for caregiving, study or health reasons). Pay, leave and benefits are pro rata, except insurance, which continues in full.

### 12.7 Right to disconnect

Work in hybrid and remote modes can blur the line between work and personal time. Therefore:

- Outside their working hours, weekends, leave and holidays, employees are not expected to read or reply to emails or messages, except when on-call or during a declared emergency.
- Managers should not send non-urgent messages outside working hours; if they do, they should use scheduled send or make clear no reply is needed.
- Meetings across time zones should rotate so that the same people are not always meeting late at night.
- No employee will face adverse treatment for not responding outside working hours.

### 12.8 On-call and standby

On-call schedules are published at least 2 weeks in advance and rotated fairly. On-call employees must be reachable within 15 minutes and able to log in within 30 minutes. Time spent actually working during on-call is counted as working time and compensated as overtime or compensatory off.

### 12.9 Overtime

- Work beyond 9 hours in a day or 48 hours in a week is overtime and must be pre-approved by the manager, except in emergencies (approval obtained the next working day).
- Employees covered by overtime provisions of law are paid at **twice their ordinary rate of wages**.
- Employees in managerial or supervisory roles who are excluded from statutory overtime receive compensatory off for significant extra work, as agreed with the manager.
- Total overtime must not exceed the quarterly limit in the applicable State law.
- Remote and hybrid employees record overtime in the HRMS in the same way as on-site employees.

### 12.10 Work on weekly offs and holidays

Work on a weekly off or public holiday must be pre-approved and earns, at the employee's choice where the law permits, either a compensatory day off (to be taken within 60 days) or overtime pay. Employees must still receive at least one weekly rest day in any 10-day period.

### 12.11 Breaks

In addition to the meal break, employees may take two short breaks of 15 minutes. Nursing mothers are entitled to nursing breaks as required by law, in addition to normal breaks, until the child is 15 months old. Remote and hybrid employees are encouraged to take regular screen breaks and to block lunch time in their calendars.
