## Chapter 13. Attendance and time recording

Every working day must have an attendance record in the HRMS — office, work-from-home, client site, on-duty or leave — and the method of recording depends on work mode.

### 13.1 How attendance is recorded

| Work mode | Primary record | Secondary record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-site | Access-card swipe at the office entrance | HRMS regularisation for exceptions |
| Hybrid (office day) | Access-card swipe | Desk booking check-in |
| Hybrid (home day) | Work-from-home status marked in the HRMS by 11:00 am | Login to Company VPN or systems |
| Remote | Daily attendance marked in the HRMS by 11:00 am | Login to Company VPN or systems |
| Off-site, client site | Client-site attendance marked in the HRMS; client timesheet where required | Company timesheet |
| Off-site, field or travel | On-duty marked in the HRMS with location | Travel request or service ticket |

The Company uses system login data only to confirm attendance and for security, not to measure minute-by-minute activity.

### 13.2 Attendance statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Present (P) | Worked from the office |
| WFH | Worked from the approved home workplace |
| Client site (CS) | Worked at a client site |
| On duty (OD) | Worked away from the base location for business: travel, field visit, training, another office |
| Half day (HD) | Worked less than 4.5 hours; half a day of leave is applied |
| Leave (L) | Approved leave of any type |
| Holiday (H) / Weekly off (WO) | Non-working day |
| Absent (A) | No work and no approved leave; treated as leave without pay |

### 13.3 Late arrival and early departure

For general-shift employees, arriving after the flexi window (10:30 am) or leaving before completing the working day, without approval, counts as a late mark. Three late marks in a calendar month result in half a day of leave being deducted. Late marks caused by Company transport delays, client meetings or approved reasons are waived through regularisation.

### 13.4 Short leave

Employees may take up to **2 short leaves of 2 hours each per month** for personal work, with manager approval in the HRMS. Short leave applies equally to home and office days.

### 13.5 Regularisation

Where a swipe is missing (card forgotten, system error) or an employee worked from another location, they submit a regularisation request in the HRMS with the reason within **5 working days**. The manager approves or rejects within 3 working days. Repeated regularisation without good reason (more than 4 times in a month) is reviewed by HR.

### 13.6 Timesheets

Employees on client billable projects and selected internal teams record time in the Company timesheet system:

- weekly, by Friday 6:00 pm (or as required by the client contract);
- accurately, by project and task, including overtime and on-call work;
- separately from client timesheets, which are filled where a client requires.

Managers approve timesheets by the following Monday. Falsifying a timesheet or attendance record is gross misconduct.

### 13.7 Unauthorised absence

- Absence without approved leave is recorded as absent and is unpaid.
- The employee must inform the manager as early as possible, and before the start of the shift, about any unplanned absence.
- Continuous unauthorised absence for **7 days or more** is dealt with under the absconding procedure in Chapter 33.
- For remote employees, being unreachable for a full working day without notice is treated as unauthorised absence.

### 13.8 Attendance during disruptions

During declared disruptions (heavy rain, floods, transport strikes, internet outages affecting an area, public-health orders), HR may declare a work-from-home day or special leave. Remote and hybrid employees who lose power or connectivity for extended periods must inform their manager and either move to a backup location (office, co-working space) or apply leave if work is impossible. Up to 2 days a year of special paid leave may be granted for verified outages beyond the employee's control.

### 13.9 Attendance and pay

The HRMS attendance record for the payroll cut-off period (normally the 21st of the previous month to the 20th of the current month) is final for salary processing. Corrections after cut-off are adjusted in the next month's salary.

### 13.10 Data and privacy

Attendance and access data are personal data processed for employment, payroll, security and statutory purposes under Chapter 31. They are accessible only to the employee, the manager, HR, payroll and security teams, and are retained as required by law.
