## Chapter 9. Hybrid working policy

Hybrid employees work from the office at least **2 days a week**, including their team's anchor days, and from an approved home workplace on the remaining days.

### 9.1 Objectives

Hybrid work combines in-person collaboration, mentoring and team building with the focus time and reduced commuting of working from home. It only works if teams plan their office days deliberately and treat remote colleagues as equal participants.

### 9.2 Office attendance requirement

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum office days | 2 days a week, averaged over each calendar month (8 days in a month with 20 working days) |
| Anchor days | Each team fixes 2 anchor days a week when the whole team is in the office |
| Flexible days | The remaining office day(s) are chosen by the employee in agreement with the manager |
| Weeks with holidays or leave | The requirement reduces pro rata; for example, 1 day in a week with one holiday |
| Senior leaders (L7+) | Expected in the office on all anchor days and for key client and team events |

Managers may require additional office days for specific events: quarterly planning, client visits, onboarding new team members, training, team offsites and project launches, with at least 3 working days' notice.

### 9.3 Team working agreement

Every hybrid team agrees and publishes a team working agreement, reviewed every 6 months. It covers:

1. anchor days and the activities reserved for them (planning, workshops, reviews, one-to-ones, team lunches);
2. core collaboration hours when everyone is available online (default 11:00 am – 4:00 pm);
3. communication channels and expected response times (9.6);
4. how meetings with remote participants are run;
5. where decisions and documentation are recorded;
6. how team members signal availability and focus time.

The template is Form C in Chapter 35.

### 9.4 Desk and room booking

- Hybrid employees book a desk in the desk-booking app at least 1 working day before an office day.
- Neighbourhoods are allocated to teams so that colleagues sit together on anchor days.
- A booked desk not checked into by 11:00 am is released.
- Lockers are available for personal items and equipment kept at the office.
- Bookings must be cancelled when plans change, so others can use the space.

### 9.5 Attendance tracking

Office days are recorded automatically through the access-card system. Home days are recorded as work-from-home in the HRMS. Employees who fall short of the monthly requirement without approval receive a reminder from the manager. Repeated shortfall (3 months in a 6-month period) may lead to review of the hybrid arrangement under 2.8 and is considered in the performance conversation.

### 9.6 Availability and communication

| Channel | Use for | Expected response during working hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phone call | Urgent issues, production incidents, client escalations | Immediately, or call back within 15 minutes |
| Team chat | Quick questions and coordination | Within 1 hour |
| Email | Formal communication, external parties, approvals | Within 1 working day |
| Project tracker or tickets | Work items, status, handoffs | As per the team's service levels |
| Calendar | Availability, meetings, focus time, leave and office days | Kept up to date daily |

On home days, hybrid employees must be reachable during their working hours and core collaboration hours, keep chat status accurate, and attend scheduled meetings with camera on unless bandwidth or a valid reason prevents it.

### 9.7 Meeting practices

- If anyone joins remotely, everyone joins on their own device or through a meeting room with proper video equipment.
- Meeting invitations include an agenda and a video link.
- Meeting notes, decisions and action items are shared in writing within 24 hours.
- Avoid scheduling meetings in the first and last 30 minutes of core hours across time zones, and avoid lunch hours (1:00 – 2:00 pm) where possible.
- Fridays after 3:00 pm are kept meeting-free as far as possible.

### 9.8 Home days: where you may work

On home days, hybrid employees work from their registered home workplace (Chapter 14). Working from another location in the country of employment, such as a family home, is allowed for up to 10 working days a year with manager approval, provided the location meets security and connectivity requirements and the employee can reach the office within one day if needed. Working from another country is not permitted for hybrid employees, except as approved under 10.6 and Chapter 36.

### 9.9 Commuting

Travel between home and the base office is the employee's own responsibility and cost, except for Company transport (Chapter 8) and late-shift cabs (Chapter 12). Hybrid employees do not receive a separate commuting allowance, but they receive the home office support set out in Chapter 14.

### 9.10 Hybrid exceptions

Temporary reductions of the office-day requirement may be approved for medical recovery, pregnancy, caregiving or disability under Chapter 3, or during city-wide disruptions. Permanent changes are requested as a work-mode change (2.5).

### 9.11 Manager responsibilities in hybrid teams

- Set clear outcomes and deadlines, rather than measuring activity or hours seen in the office.
- Hold one-to-ones at least every two weeks, alternating in person and online where practical.
- Rotate visible assignments and opportunities fairly between office-based and home-based time.
- Watch for signs of isolation, overwork or burnout, and encourage use of leave and wellbeing resources (Chapter 19).
- Review the team working agreement every 6 months and adjust based on team feedback.
