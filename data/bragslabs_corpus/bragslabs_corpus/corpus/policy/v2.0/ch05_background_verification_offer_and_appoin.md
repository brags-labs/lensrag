## Chapter 5. Background verification, offer and appointment

Every employee is verified and receives a written offer and appointment letter before or at joining; no verbal offer is binding.

### 5.1 Offer approval

1. The recruiter prepares an offer proposal with CTC, level, work mode, base location and joining date, benchmarked against the salary band.
2. Offers within band are approved by the HRBP and hiring manager. Offers above the band midpoint need business unit head approval; offers above the band maximum need CHRO and CFO approval.
3. Joining bonuses, relocation support and retention bonuses must be approved and stated in the offer.

### 5.2 The offer letter

The offer letter is issued by HR through the HRMS or by email from an official Company domain, and states:

- designation, level and department;
- annual CTC with a breakup of fixed pay, variable pay and benefits;
- work mode and base location;
- expected joining date and offer validity (normally 7 days to accept);
- probation period and notice period;
- conditions, such as satisfactory background verification and submission of documents.

A candidate accepts by signing electronically. The Company may withdraw an offer if conditions are not met, if false information is discovered, or if the candidate does not join on the agreed date without a mutually agreed extension.

### 5.3 Background verification (BGV)

The Company verifies all new employees through an empanelled agency, with the candidate's written consent obtained under Chapter 31.

| Check | Scope | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | PAN, Aadhaar (masked) or passport | All |
| Address | Current and permanent address, by digital or physical verification | All |
| Education | Highest qualification and professional certifications | All |
| Employment | Last two employers or last 7 years, whichever is shorter | Lateral hires |
| Criminal record | Police or court record check in places of residence over the last 7 years | All |
| Database checks | Global sanctions, regulatory and media checks | L5 and above, finance and security roles |
| Credit check | Only where legally permitted and relevant to the role | Finance roles handling funds |
| Client-specific checks | Drug tests, additional references or clearances where a client contract requires | Client-deployed employees |

BGV should be started before joining and completed within **30 days** of joining. If a check reveals a discrepancy, HR gives the employee 7 days to explain. Material falsification, such as a fake degree or fake employment record, results in withdrawal of the offer or termination without notice.

### 5.4 Joining documents

The new employee submits through the HRMS, before or on the first day:

- signed offer letter;
- identity and address proof;
- education and professional certificates (copies; originals shown for verification and returned the same day);
- relieving letter and last three payslips from the previous employer;
- PAN, UAN and previous PF details, bank account details;
- passport-size photograph;
- emergency contact details;
- nomination forms for PF, gratuity and group insurance;
- for remote and hybrid employees: home workplace address and self-declaration (Form B, Chapter 35).

The Company never retains original certificates.

### 5.5 The appointment letter

An appointment letter is issued on or before the joining date. It contains the terms in 5.2 plus:

- the date of joining and nature of employment (permanent, fixed-term, trainee);
- reporting manager;
- working hours, shift and weekly off;
- leave entitlement;
- confidentiality, intellectual property and non-solicitation obligations;
- notice period during and after probation;
- reference to this manual as part of the employment terms.

### 5.6 Fixed-term employment

Fixed-term employees receive the same hours, wages, allowances and benefits as permanent employees doing the same or similar work, pro rata to their service. They become eligible for gratuity on completing one year of service, as required by the Code on Social Security. A fixed-term contract states the end date or project. Renewal or conversion to permanent employment is at the Company's discretion and confirmed in writing.

### 5.7 Service agreements and bonds

The Company does not require service bonds as a condition of employment. Where the Company funds expensive training, certification or an overseas assignment, the employee may be asked to sign a training agreement with pro rata repayment if they leave within a defined period, as described in Chapter 24 and Chapter 11.

### 5.8 Change in terms

Any change to designation, level, CTC, work mode, base location or reporting manager is confirmed in a letter or HRMS notification. Changes to the appointment terms that need the employee's consent will be made only with that consent.
