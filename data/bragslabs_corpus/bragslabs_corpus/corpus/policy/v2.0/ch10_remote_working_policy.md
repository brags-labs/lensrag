## Chapter 10. Remote working policy

Remote employees work from an approved home workplace in their country of employment, are held to the same performance and conduct standards as everyone else, and attend the office when called in for defined purposes.

### 10.1 Eligibility

A role may be designated remote when all of the following hold:

- the work can be done fully online without loss of quality, security or client service;
- the client contract and data classification permit it;
- the team already works with distributed members and documented processes;
- for existing employees: confirmed, rated 3 or above, no active disciplinary action, and with a suitable home workplace.

Remote roles are capped at 15% of each business unit's headcount, unless the CHRO approves a higher limit.

### 10.2 Remote work agreement

Every remote employee signs a remote work agreement (Form D, Chapter 35) covering:

1. the registered home workplace address;
2. working hours, core hours and availability;
3. equipment issued and home office allowance;
4. information security and data protection obligations;
5. health and safety self-assessment of the home workplace;
6. office attendance obligations under 10.7;
7. the base location for payroll, statutory and travel purposes;
8. conditions for review or revocation.

### 10.3 Base location

Every remote employee is attached to the nearest Company office as their base location. The base location decides:

- the public holiday list (Chapter 16);
- professional tax and labour welfare fund registration, subject to the law of the State where the employee actually works;
- the city category for allowances, where applicable;
- the starting point for travel to office visits and client sites.

Pay is set by role, level and the location factor of the base location. Moving to a location with a different location factor may change future pay revisions but will not reduce current fixed pay.

### 10.4 Home workplace requirements

- A dedicated, quiet workspace where calls can be taken without disturbance and screens are not visible to others.
- Internet connection of at least **50 Mbps**, plus a mobile hotspot backup.
- Power backup (UPS or inverter) for at least 2 hours, or an alternative arrangement such as a nearby co-working space.
- Ergonomic setup as per Chapter 14.
- A lockable space for Company assets and any printed material.

Employees complete a home workplace self-assessment at joining and every 12 months.

### 10.5 Changing the remote location within the country of employment

Remote employees must request approval at least **30 days before** permanently moving to a new address. HR checks the new location for statutory, tax and connectivity implications and updates the base location if needed. Working temporarily from another address in the country of employment for up to 30 days a year needs only manager approval and an HRMS entry.

### 10.6 Working from another country

Working from another country can create immigration, tax, permanent establishment, data-transfer and social-security risks for both the employee and the Company. Therefore:

- Remote and hybrid employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up to **10 working days in a calendar year**, only with prior approval from the manager, HRBP, Legal and Information Security.
- The request is made at least 30 days in advance, stating the country, address and dates.
- Not permitted: countries under sanctions or the Company's restricted list, roles handling client data that must stay in a specific country or region, or work that requires access to restricted systems.
- The employee is responsible for having the correct visa; a visitor visa may not permit work in some countries.
- Unapproved work from another country is a serious breach and may lead to disciplinary action.

Long-term work abroad is handled only through an international assignment or a transfer to the local entity (Chapters 11 and 36).

### 10.7 Office attendance for remote employees

Remote employees attend the office or another location when called in for:

- onboarding week and team kick-offs;
- quarterly team or business unit meetings (up to 4 times a year);
- client meetings, workshops and audits that need in-person presence;
- mandatory training that cannot be delivered online;
- investigations, disciplinary hearings or performance reviews, where in-person attendance is reasonably required.

The Company gives at least **7 days' notice** and pays travel and stay as per Chapter 20 when the employee's home is in a different city from the meeting location.

### 10.8 Working hours and availability

Remote employees follow their assigned shift (Chapter 12), are online during core collaboration hours (11:00 am – 4:00 pm IST by default), and keep their calendar and chat status accurate. They must be reachable on the registered mobile number during working hours. Being unreachable for more than 2 hours during working hours without notice, more than twice in a month, is a conduct issue.

### 10.9 Productivity and performance

Remote employees are managed on outcomes: agreed goals, deliverables, quality and timeliness. The Company does not use keystroke logging, screen recording or webcam monitoring for productivity tracking. Security monitoring of Company devices and systems continues as described in Chapter 30.

### 10.10 Wellbeing and connection

Remote work can lead to isolation and blurred boundaries. The Company supports remote employees through:

- regular one-to-ones with the manager (at least every two weeks);
- virtual team rituals such as weekly stand-ups, demos and social sessions;
- an annual budget of ₹20,000 for co-working space days or team meet-ups;
- access to the Employee Assistance Programme (Chapter 19);
- the right to disconnect outside working hours (Chapter 12).

### 10.11 Costs

Remote employees receive equipment and the home office allowance in Chapter 14. Personal costs of the home, such as rent, furniture beyond the allowance, and family utilities, are not reimbursed.

### 10.12 Review and revocation

Remote arrangements are reviewed annually and may be revoked as described in 2.8. Where a remote role is converted to hybrid or on-site and the employee lives in a city without a Company office, the Company gives at least **60 days' notice**, discusses relocation support, and considers alternatives such as a transfer to a role that remains remote.
