## Chapter 21. Expense reimbursement

The Company reimburses reasonable, necessary and approved business expenses, claimed with valid bills within **30 days**, and paid with the next payroll.

### 21.1 Principles

- Spend Company money as carefully as your own.
- Only expenses incurred for Company business, within policy limits and with prior approval where required, are reimbursed.
- The person who benefits from an expense must not approve it; a manager cannot approve their own claims.
- Expenses billable to a client must follow the client's rules as well.

### 21.2 Reimbursable expenses

| Category | What is covered | Limit or rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travel | Air, rail, hotel, per diem as per Chapter 20 | As per entitlement |
| Local conveyance | Cab, auto, metro for business trips within the city, client visits, late-night travel | Actuals with receipts; personal vehicle at ₹12/km |
| Client entertainment | Meals with clients or prospects for business purposes | ₹2,500 per person per meal; names and purpose recorded; pre-approval above ₹10,000 |
| Team events | Approved team lunches, celebrations, offsites | As per team budget |
| Training and certifications | Pre-approved courses and exam fees | As per Chapter 24 |
| Professional memberships | Approved bodies relevant to the role | Up to ₹15,000 a year |
| Home office and connectivity | As per Chapter 14 | Through payroll allowances |
| Co-working space | Pre-approved days for remote employees | As per Chapter 10 |
| Courier and shipping | Business documents and equipment | Actuals |
| Visa, passport, vaccinations | For business travel | Actuals |
| Mobile and data roaming | During business travel abroad | Actuals within approved plan |

### 21.3 Not reimbursable

- Alcohol, except at client entertainment approved by a L7 or above leader.
- Traffic fines, parking fines and penalties.
- Personal entertainment, minibar, laundry for trips under 4 nights, spa and leisure.
- Upgrades beyond entitlement, unless at no cost.
- Gifts to colleagues, except approved recognition (Chapter 25).
- Expenses for family members, unless part of an approved relocation or assignment.
- Commuting between home and the base office.
- Late cancellation and no-show charges caused by the employee's personal reasons.

### 21.4 Claim process

1. Submit the claim in the expense tool within **30 days** of the expense (or of returning from a trip).
2. Attach itemised bills or GST invoices. Card slips alone are not accepted.
3. For GST, ensure invoices carry the Company's GSTIN where applicable.
4. The manager approves within **5 working days**; finance checks the claim against policy.
5. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll or within 15 days, whichever is earlier.

Claims submitted after 90 days are rejected unless approved by the CFO.

### 21.5 Missing bills

Where a bill is not available (for example, auto rickshaw fares or roadside meals), the employee may submit a self-declaration for up to ₹500 per day and ₹3,000 per trip.

### 21.6 Corporate cards

Employees who travel frequently may receive a corporate card. Corporate card expenses are reconciled monthly in the expense tool. Personal use of a corporate card is not permitted; any personal charge must be reported and repaid within 7 days.

### 21.7 Advances

Travel advances for domestic trips are discouraged. For international travel, a forex card is issued. Unused advances and forex must be settled within **15 days** of return; unsettled amounts may be recovered from salary.

### 21.8 Audit and misuse

Finance audits claims randomly and for high-value items. Inflating, duplicating or fabricating claims, or using fake bills, is gross misconduct leading to recovery and disciplinary action up to dismissal (Chapter 33).
