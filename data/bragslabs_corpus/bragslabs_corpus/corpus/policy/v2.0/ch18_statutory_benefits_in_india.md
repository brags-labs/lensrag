## Chapter 18. Statutory benefits in India

The Company provides every statutory benefit required by the Code on Social Security, 2020 and related laws to all eligible employees, regardless of work mode.

### 18.1 Summary

| Benefit | Who is covered | Contribution or entitlement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) | All employees | Employee 12% and employer 12% of wages |
| Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS) | Members eligible under EPS rules | 8.33% of wages up to the statutory ceiling, out of the employer's 12% |
| Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance (EDLI) | All EPF members | Employer contribution as notified; life cover as per scheme |
| Employees' State Insurance (ESI) | Employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month (₹25,000 for persons with disabilities) | Employee 0.75%, employer 3.25% of wages |
| Gratuity | Permanent employees after 5 years' continuous service; fixed-term employees after 1 year | 15 days' wages for each completed year of service |
| Statutory bonus | Employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month in wages | 8.33% to 20% of wages, subject to the statutory calculation ceiling |
| Maternity benefit | Women employees with 80 days' service in 12 months | As in Chapter 16 |
| Employee's compensation | All employees | Compensation for death or injury arising out of and in the course of employment |
| Labour welfare fund | As per State law | Small fixed contributions by employee and employer |
| Professional tax | As per State law | Deducted from salary as per slabs |

### 18.2 Provident Fund

- Every employee is enrolled from the date of joining and linked to their Universal Account Number (UAN).
- Employees earning above the statutory wage ceiling may contribute on actual wages if they wish; the employer matches on the same basis as per Company policy.
- **Voluntary PF:** employees may contribute more than 12%; the employer does not match the voluntary part.
- Employees can check their balance, update nominations, transfer accounts and apply for advances or withdrawals online through the EPFO portal.
- International workers on assignment in India and Indian employees on assignment to countries with a social security agreement are handled as per the agreement and EPFO rules.

### 18.3 ESI

Eligible employees and their dependants receive medical care, sickness, maternity and disablement benefits under the ESI scheme. Employees covered by ESI also remain covered by the group health insurance (Chapter 19) for benefits that ESI does not provide.

### 18.4 Gratuity

**Formula:** Gratuity = Last drawn monthly wages × 15 / 26 × completed years of service

- Service of 6 months or more in the final year counts as a full year.
- Gratuity is payable on resignation, retirement, termination, death or disablement. The 5-year condition does not apply on death or disablement, and applies as 1 year for fixed-term employees.
- The maximum payable is the ceiling notified under law; the Company may pay more at its discretion.
- Gratuity is paid within **30 days** of it becoming payable; delayed payment attracts interest as per law.
- Gratuity may be forfeited only in the cases permitted by law, such as termination for an act causing damage to Company property (to the extent of the damage), or for riotous, violent or seriously immoral conduct or an offence involving moral turpitude.
- Employees must keep nominations updated in the HRMS.

### 18.5 Statutory bonus

Eligible employees who have worked at least **30 days** in the financial year receive a bonus of at least 8.33% of wages (or ₹100, whichever is higher), up to 20%, based on allocable surplus. Bonus is paid within **8 months** of the close of the financial year. Where the Company's variable pay plan pays more, the statutory bonus is included in that payment.

### 18.6 Employee's compensation

If an employee dies or is injured due to an accident arising out of and in the course of employment, compensation is paid as per law. This includes accidents while commuting between home and the workplace, as provided under the Code on Social Security, and accidents at the home workplace during working hours while doing Company work, subject to the facts of each case. Employees must report any work-related injury within 24 hours (Chapter 32).

### 18.7 Professional tax and labour welfare fund

These are deducted according to the law of the State where the employee is registered. For remote employees working from a State other than their base location, HR determines the correct State registration as required by law.

### 18.8 Social security for gig, platform and contract workers

The Company pays contributions required under law for any gig or platform workers it engages, and ensures through its contracts that agencies supplying contract workers comply with PF, ESI and wage obligations. HR audits contractor compliance every quarter.

### 18.9 Records and returns

HR maintains statutory registers in electronic form, files monthly and annual returns on time, displays required notices in each office and on the intranet for remote employees, and makes records available to inspectors as required.
