## Chapter 7. Probation and confirmation

New permanent employees serve a **6-month probation**, at the end of which they are confirmed, have probation extended once, or are separated, based on documented performance and conduct.

### 7.1 Purpose

Probation lets the employee and the Company assess whether the role is a good fit. Probationers receive the same pay, benefits and support as confirmed employees, except where this manual states otherwise.

### 7.2 Probation periods

| Employee category | Probation |
| --- | --- |
| Lateral hires, all levels | 6 months |
| Graduate trainees | 12 months, including the training period |
| Rehires within 12 months of exit, same role | 3 months, or waived at CHRO discretion |
| Internal transfers and promotions | No new probation |
| Fixed-term employees under 12 months | 3 months |

### 7.3 Reviews during probation

1. **Goal setting (within 2 weeks):** the manager and employee agree written goals and success measures in the HRMS.
2. **Monthly check-ins:** short structured conversations on progress, recorded briefly in the HRMS.
3. **Mid-probation review (3 months):** formal review of performance, conduct and integration. Concerns are raised in writing, with specific improvement expectations and support offered.
4. **Final review (1 month before probation ends):** the manager recommends confirmation, extension or separation, with reasons, to the HRBP.

For remote and hybrid probationers, reviews take place over video with cameras on; the same standards and evidence apply as for on-site employees.

### 7.4 Assessment criteria

- Achievement of agreed probation goals
- Quality and timeliness of work
- Learning agility and acquisition of required skills
- Collaboration, communication and responsiveness (including in virtual settings)
- Attendance, availability and adherence to the agreed work mode
- Conduct and adherence to Company policies
- Completion of mandatory induction training

### 7.5 Outcomes

**Confirmation.** HR issues a confirmation letter through the HRMS on or before the probation end date. The notice period changes to the confirmed-employee notice period, and the employee becomes eligible for benefits limited to confirmed employees, such as salary advance, remote work requests and sponsored higher education.

**Extension.** Probation may be extended once, by up to **3 months**, if the employee needs more time to meet expectations or had extended leave during probation. The extension letter states the reasons, specific goals and support. Extension for maternity leave or long medical leave is not a negative outcome.

**Separation.** If performance or conduct is not satisfactory after documented feedback and a fair chance to improve, employment may end with notice as per 7.6. The decision is reviewed and approved by the HRBP and skip-level manager.

Probation is not deemed complete without written confirmation. However, if the Company does not communicate a decision within 30 days after the probation end date, the employee may write to HR, and HR must issue a decision within 7 working days.

### 7.6 Notice during probation

| Situation | Notice |
| --- | --- |
| Employee resigns during probation | 15 days, or salary in lieu at Company discretion |
| Company ends employment during probation | 15 days, or basic pay in lieu |
| Gross misconduct during probation | Without notice, after a fair hearing (Chapter 33) |

### 7.7 Leave during probation

Probationers accrue leave from the date of joining and may use it with approval (Chapter 15). Maternity, paternity, sick and bereavement leave are available during probation. Earned leave of more than 5 consecutive days in the first 3 months needs HRBP approval.

### 7.8 Work mode during probation

Probationers in hybrid roles follow the team's hybrid schedule. Remote work requests from probationers who were not hired as remote are considered only after confirmation, except for accommodation requests under Chapter 3.
