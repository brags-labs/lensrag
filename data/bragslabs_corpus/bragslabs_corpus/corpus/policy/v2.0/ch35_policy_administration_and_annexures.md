## Chapter 35. Policy administration and annexures

The CHRO owns this manual, reviews it every **12 months** or sooner when the law changes, and publishes changes at least 15 days before they take effect.

### 35.1 Ownership and review

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Policy owner | Chief Human Resources Officer |
| Approver | Chief Executive Officer (and the Board for policies requiring Board approval) |
| Review cycle | Every 12 months, or on a change in law, business model or work modes |
| Legal review | Employment counsel reviews each major revision |
| Version control | Each version is numbered and dated, with a change log on the intranet |
| Communication | Email, intranet, HRMS notification and team briefings; acknowledgement required for major changes |

### 35.2 Amendments

The Company may amend, add to or withdraw any policy in this manual, subject to applicable law and the appointment letter. Changes that reduce existing benefits are communicated with at least **30 days' notice** and, where the law requires, after consultation or with consent. Statutory changes take effect as required by law, even if this manual has not yet been updated.

### 35.3 Interpretation

If any part of this manual is unclear, the CHRO's interpretation applies, consistent with law. If any provision is held invalid, the rest of the manual remains in force. This manual is not a contract of employment and does not guarantee employment for any period.

### 35.4 Compliance monitoring

HR reports every quarter to the leadership team on key indicators: work-mode distribution, attendance compliance, leave usage, grievances and POSH complaints (anonymised), disciplinary cases, training completion, attrition and statutory compliance. Internal Audit reviews HR compliance annually.

### 35.6 Annexure: Form B — Home workplace self-declaration

I confirm that my home workplace at the address registered in the HRMS:

- [ ] has a private, quiet workspace where my screen is not visible to others and calls are not overheard;
- [ ] has internet of at least 50 Mbps and a mobile hotspot backup;
- [ ] has power backup for at least 2 hours, or I have an alternative location;
- [ ] has safe electrical wiring and no trip hazards;
- [ ] has an ergonomic chair, desk and screen setup;
- [ ] has a secure, lockable place to store Company equipment;
- [ ] has secured Wi-Fi (WPA2/WPA3) with a changed default router password.

I will inform HR within 7 days of any change to my home workplace.

### 35.8 Annexure: Form D — Remote work agreement

The remote work agreement between the Company and the employee records:

1. Effective date and review date.
2. Registered home workplace address and base location.
3. Working hours, shift, core hours and availability commitments.
4. Equipment issued and allowances (Chapter 14).
5. Information security obligations (Chapter 30) and data protection obligations (Chapter 31).
6. Health and safety self-assessment (Chapter 32).
7. Office attendance when called in (Chapter 10).
8. Rules on working from another location in the country of employment or abroad.
9. Conditions for review, change or revocation (Chapter 2).
10. Signatures of the employee, manager and HRBP.

### 35.9 Annexure: Client-site deployment checklist

- [ ] Deployment letter issued and acknowledged
- [ ] Client induction, NDA and security clearance completed
- [ ] Client site safety and POSH arrangements confirmed
- [ ] Employee informed of Company IC, HR and emergency helpline contacts
- [ ] Travel, stay and allowances approved (if outside base city)
- [ ] Timesheet and reporting arrangements set up
- [ ] Client devices and access issued and recorded
- [ ] Deployment review date set

### 35.10 Annexure: Exit checklist

- [ ] Resignation accepted in the HRMS and last working day confirmed
- [ ] Handover document approved by manager
- [ ] Exit interview completed
- [ ] All Company and client assets returned (or courier pickup completed)
- [ ] Company data removed from personal devices and confirmed
- [ ] Clearances from IT, Admin, Finance, Security and manager
- [ ] F&F calculated and paid within 2 working days
- [ ] Relieving and experience letters issued
