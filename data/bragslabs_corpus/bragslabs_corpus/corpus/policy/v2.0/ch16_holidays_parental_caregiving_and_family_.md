## Chapter 16. Holidays, parental, caregiving and family support

The Company observes 10 public holidays plus 2 floating holidays each year, and provides paid parental, family and life-event leave that applies equally in every work mode.

### 16.1 Public holidays

- The holiday list for each office is published by **15 December** for the next year and includes the three national holidays: Republic Day (26 January), Independence Day (15 August) and Gandhi Jayanti (2 October).
- The remaining holidays are chosen by each office in line with the State's festival holiday requirements.
- Remote employees follow the holiday list of their base location. Off-site employees follow their base location's list unless the client site is closed on a different day, in which case the employee may swap the holiday with manager approval.
- Employees working at client sites abroad follow the host country's public holidays, as set out in the assignment letter.
- Where a holiday falls on a weekly off, no substitute holiday is given unless State law requires it.

### 16.2 Floating holidays

Employees choose 2 days from the optional holiday list (festivals of various religions and regions, and personal days such as a birthday) and apply for them in the HRMS at least 7 days in advance.

### 16.3 Maternity leave

| Situation | Paid leave |
| --- | --- |
| Birth of first or second child | 26 weeks, of which up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date |
| Birth of third or subsequent child | 12 weeks, of which up to 6 weeks before delivery |
| Adoption of a child below 3 months | 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over |
| Commissioning mother (surrogacy) | 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over |
| Miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy | 6 weeks immediately following the day of miscarriage or termination |
| Tubectomy operation | 2 weeks |
| Illness arising from pregnancy, delivery, premature birth, miscarriage or tubectomy | Up to 1 additional month |

**Eligibility.** Women employees who have worked for at least 80 days in the 12 months before the expected delivery date, in any work mode.

**Additional support.**

- **Medical bonus** as prescribed by law if pre-natal and post-natal care is not provided free by the Company.
- **Return to work:** after maternity leave, the mother may work from home for up to 3 months where the nature of work allows, on terms agreed with the manager, as provided by law.
- **Nursing breaks:** two breaks a day until the child is 15 months old.
- **Crèche:** access to a crèche or crèche allowance as per 16.9.
- **Protection:** no termination or adverse change of terms during maternity leave, and the employee returns to the same or an equivalent role.
- **Pregnancy at work:** pregnant employees may request extra work-from-home days, lighter travel, no night shifts, and priority parking without loss of pay or rating.

### 16.4 Paternity and partner leave

- **7 working days** of paid leave for the birth or adoption of a child, to be taken within **6 months**, in one or two blocks.
- Available to all employees who are the non-birthing parent, including same-sex partners.
- Not limited to the first two children.

### 16.5 Adoption leave (all parents)

In addition to statutory maternity leave for adoptive mothers, the non-primary adoptive parent receives 10 working days of paid leave. Single adoptive fathers receive 12 weeks of paid leave as the primary caregiver.

### 16.6 Fertility and pregnancy loss support

- Up to **5 days** of paid leave a year for fertility treatment appointments, for the employee or to accompany a partner.
- Up to **5 days** of paid leave for an employee whose partner experiences a miscarriage or stillbirth.
- Counselling through the Employee Assistance Programme.

### 16.7 Bereavement leave

| Relationship | Paid leave |
| --- | --- |
| Spouse or partner, child, parent | 7 working days |
| Sibling, parent-in-law, grandparent, grandchild | 5 working days |
| Other relatives or close persons | 1 working day, with manager approval |

Bereavement leave may be taken in up to two blocks within 3 months of the death, to allow for funeral and ritual observances. Employees on off-site or international assignment receive emergency travel support to return home (Chapter 20).

### 16.8 Caregiver leave

Employees may take up to **5 days of paid caregiver leave** a year to care for a family member who is seriously ill, hospitalised or has a disability. Longer needs may be supported through flexible work, part-time work or leave without pay.

### 16.9 Childcare support

- **Crèche:** offices with 50 or more employees provide access to a crèche facility, either on-site or through a tie-up within a reasonable distance, as required by law. Mothers may visit the crèche four times a day, including rest intervals.
- **Crèche allowance:** remote and hybrid parents who cannot use the Company crèche may claim up to ₹8,000 a month for a registered day-care facility, for children up to 6 years.
- **School holidays:** managers should give priority to parents' leave requests during school holidays on a rotating basis.

### 16.10 Marriage leave

**5 working days** of paid leave for the employee's own marriage, once during employment, to be taken within 1 month of the marriage date.

### 16.11 Menstrual wellbeing

Employees who menstruate may work from home on up to 2 days a month, or take 1 day a month as sick leave without a medical certificate, for menstrual discomfort. No reason beyond "wellbeing day" needs to be given.

### 16.12 Domestic violence support

An employee affected by domestic violence may take up to **10 days of paid leave** a year for legal, medical, relocation or safety needs, request a change of work location or contact details, and access confidential support through HR and the Employee Assistance Programme. Information shared is kept strictly confidential.

### 16.13 Religious and personal observance

Employees may use floating holidays, earned leave or flexible hours for religious or personal observances, including pilgrimages. Requests are approved fairly across the team.
