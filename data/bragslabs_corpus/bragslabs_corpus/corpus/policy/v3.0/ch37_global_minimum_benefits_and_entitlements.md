## Chapter 37. Global minimum benefits and entitlements by country

No employee anywhere receives less than the Company's global minimums below, even where local law requires less; where local law or the country addendum gives more, the higher entitlement applies.

### 37.1 Company global minimums

| Benefit | Global minimum (all countries) |
| --- | --- |
| Paid annual leave (vacation, PTO or earned leave) | 18 working days a year, plus local public holidays (pro rata for part-time) |
| Paid sick leave | 10 working days a year, in addition to any statutory sick pay scheme |
| Paid birth-parent leave | 16 weeks at full pay (Company tops up any statutory payment) |
| Paid non-birth-parent leave (partner, adoptive, surrogacy) | 2 weeks at full pay, to be taken within 6 months |
| Paid bereavement leave | 5 working days for immediate family |
| Paid caregiver leave | 5 working days a year |
| Family and domestic violence leave | 10 working days a year, paid |
| Health cover | Private or supplementary medical insurance for employee and dependants, in addition to any public system |
| Life and accident cover | At least 2× annual base salary |
| Employee Assistance Programme | Free, confidential counselling worldwide |
| Learning budget | Equivalent of the Chapter 24 budget, set in local currency |
| Home office support | Equivalent of the Chapter 14 allowances, set in local currency |

### 37.2 Key statutory entitlements by country (summary)

This table summarises statutory minimums only. Company entitlements in each addendum are often higher.

| Country | Statutory paid annual leave | Statutory sick pay | Birth-parent leave | Partner leave | Employer notice (statutory minimum) | Retirement / social security |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United States | None federally | None federally; many states and cities require paid sick leave | FMLA: 12 weeks unpaid (employers with 50+ staff); state paid family leave in some states | FMLA: 12 weeks unpaid | None federally (at-will); WARN Act 60 days for mass layoffs | Social Security and Medicare (FICA); 401(k) voluntary |
| India | Set by State Shops and Establishments Acts | Set by State law | 26 weeks paid (first two children) | No statutory requirement for private employers | As per contract; 1 month for retrenchment of eligible workers | EPF, ESI, gratuity |
| United Kingdom | 5.6 weeks (28 days for a 5-day week, may include bank holidays) | SSP from the first day of sickness (from 6 April 2026), up to 28 weeks | 52 weeks leave, 39 weeks statutory pay | 2 weeks; day-one right to leave from 6 April 2026 | 1 week per year of service, up to 12 weeks | National Insurance; pension auto-enrolment |
| Germany | 20 days for a 5-day week | 6 weeks at full pay by employer, then health insurance | Maternity protection 6 weeks before and 8 weeks after birth, fully paid; parental leave up to 3 years with state parental allowance | Parental leave and state parental allowance | 4 weeks, rising to 7 months with long service | Statutory pension, health, long-term care and unemployment insurance |
| Netherlands | 4 × weekly working hours (20 days for 5 days a week) | At least 70% of salary for up to 104 weeks | At least 16 weeks pregnancy and maternity leave, paid through UWV | 1 week fully paid, plus up to 5 weeks supplementary leave paid by UWV; 9 weeks of paid parental leave | 1–4 months by length of service (dismissal needs UWV permit or court) | State pension (AOW); sector or company pension |
| Ireland | 4 working weeks; plus public holidays | Statutory sick pay for a limited number of days at 70% of wages, subject to a daily cap | 26 weeks maternity leave plus 16 weeks unpaid | 2 weeks paternity leave; parent's leave with state benefit | 1–8 weeks by length of service | PRSI; pension auto-enrolment |
| Singapore | 7 days, rising by 1 day a year to 14 (Employment Act) | 14 days outpatient and 60 days hospitalisation (including the 14) | 16 weeks government-paid maternity leave (eligible mothers) | 4 weeks government-paid paternity leave; 10 weeks shared parental leave for children born from 1 April 2026 | 1 day to 4 weeks by length of service, or as per contract | CPF |
