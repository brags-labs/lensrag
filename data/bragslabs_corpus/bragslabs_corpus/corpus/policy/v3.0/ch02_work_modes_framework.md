## Chapter 2. Work modes framework

Every employee has one recorded work mode in the HRMS. The work mode is set by the role, not by the individual, and is stated in the appointment letter or a later work-mode agreement.

### 2.1 The four work modes

| Work mode | Where work happens | Minimum office presence | Typical roles |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| On-site | A Company office, every working day | 5 days a week | Facilities, IT infrastructure, ODC and secure-bay project teams, front office, lab and hardware roles, early-career trainees |
| Hybrid | A mix of Company office and home workplace | 3 days a week, including team anchor days | Most engineering, product, design, delivery, HR, finance and sales roles |
| Remote | Home workplace or another approved location in the country of employment | No fixed requirement; attend when called in (see 10.7) | Specialist roles hired for location, roles with distributed teams, approved individual cases |
| Off-site | Client premises, field locations, partner sites or overseas assignments | As per the client or assignment | Consultants deployed at client sites, implementation engineers, field support, onsite assignees abroad |

An off-site employee may also be on-site, hybrid or remote for the days they are not at the client or field location. Their home work mode is recorded alongside the off-site deployment.

### 2.2 Principles

1. **Same standards everywhere.** Pay, performance expectations, career growth, conduct rules and benefits do not depend on work mode, except for mode-specific allowances described in this manual.
2. **Business need first.** Work modes are designed around client commitments, security requirements, team collaboration and role content.
3. **Fairness.** Comparable roles in the same team receive the same work mode unless there is a documented reason.
4. **No proximity bias.** Managers must evaluate people on outcomes, not on visibility in the office (Chapter 22).
5. **Compliance.** Work modes must respect data security, client contracts, tax and statutory registrations.

### 2.3 Eligibility for each work mode

| Criterion | On-site | Hybrid | Remote | Off-site |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Role can be performed without physical presence | Not required | Partly | Fully | Not applicable |
| Client contract permits work away from secure premises | Not required | Required | Required | As per client |
| Completed probation | No | No | Yes, unless hired as remote | No |
| Last performance rating | Any | Any | 3 or above | Any |
| Suitable home workplace (Chapter 14) | No | Yes | Yes | No |
| Reliable internet of at least 50 Mbps | No | Yes | Yes, plus backup | As per assignment |
| Manager and HRBP approval | Default | Yes | Yes, plus business unit head | Yes, plus delivery head |

New graduates and trainees work on-site or hybrid for at least their first 6 months, so they can learn from colleagues in person.

### 2.4 How work modes are assigned

- **New hires:** the work mode is decided by the hiring manager and HR when the requisition is raised, advertised in the job description, and confirmed in the offer letter.
- **Existing employees:** the work mode recorded on the policy effective date continues unless changed under 2.5.
- **Project deployment:** a delivery manager may deploy an employee off-site for a project; the employee's home work mode resumes when the deployment ends.

### 2.5 Requesting a change of work mode

An employee may request a change of work mode once in any 6-month period, for example from on-site to hybrid, or from hybrid to remote.

1. The employee submits a work-mode change request in the HRMS (Form A, Chapter 35), stating the reason, proposed arrangement, home workplace details and proposed start date.
2. The manager discusses the request with the employee within 7 working days, assessing role suitability, team impact, client requirements and performance.
3. The HRBP checks policy eligibility and any tax, statutory or location implications.
4. Approvers decide within 15 working days of the request. Approval may be for a trial period of up to 3 months.
5. The decision and reasons are recorded in the HRMS. Approved changes are documented in a work-mode agreement signed by the employee.
6. If the request is declined, the employee may ask the skip-level manager to review it once.

Requests based on disability, pregnancy, serious illness, or caregiving for a family member with serious illness or disability receive priority and are treated as requests for reasonable accommodation (Chapter 3).

### 2.6 Company-initiated changes

The Company may change an employee's work mode where business needs change, for example when a client requires on-site presence, a project moves to a secure facility, or a team is restructured. The Company will:

- give at least **30 days' written notice** for a change that requires relocation or daily office attendance for an employee who was remote;
- give at least **15 days' notice** for other changes, such as a change in hybrid office days;
- consider individual hardship and offer alternatives where reasonable;
- support relocation as per Chapter 20 where a change requires the employee to move cities.

Shorter notice may apply in emergencies, during client escalations, or when an employee's conduct or performance requires closer supervision.

### 2.7 Temporary work-from-home

On-site and hybrid employees may work from home temporarily, outside their normal arrangement, for:

- personal reasons such as home repairs, deliveries or a family member's illness: up to 4 days a month with manager approval;
- medical recovery, late pregnancy or a temporary disability: up to 3 months with HR approval and a medical certificate;
- city-wide disruptions such as floods, transport strikes, public-health advisories or severe pollution: as announced by HR or the Crisis Management Team.

Temporary work-from-home is recorded in the HRMS. It is not available to employees in secure ODC or client-mandated on-site roles unless the client approves.

### 2.8 Ending or revoking a flexible arrangement

A hybrid or remote arrangement may be reviewed or revoked, with 30 days' notice, if:

- the employee's performance falls below rating 3 or a PIP is started;
- the employee repeatedly breaches availability, attendance or security rules;
- the home workplace no longer meets the requirements of Chapter 14;
- the role or client requirements change.

Revocation for a serious security or conduct breach may take effect immediately.

### 2.9 Records

HR maintains a work-mode register listing each employee's work mode, base location, home workplace address (for remote and hybrid), off-site deployment details, and approval history. Employees must update their home address and contact details in the HRMS within 7 days of any change.
