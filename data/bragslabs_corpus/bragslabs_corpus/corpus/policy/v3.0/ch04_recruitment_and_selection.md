## Chapter 4. Recruitment and selection

The Company hires the best available candidate for each role through a fair, documented and timely process, whether interviews happen in person or online.

### 4.1 Workforce planning and requisitions

1. Each business unit submits an annual headcount plan by role, level, location and work mode, approved by the CEO and CFO.
2. To fill an approved position, the hiring manager raises a requisition in the HRMS with the job description, level, budgeted CTC range, work mode and base location.
3. Positions outside the approved plan need additional approval from the business unit head, CHRO and CFO.
4. Replacement hiring for resigning employees may begin as soon as the resignation is accepted.

### 4.2 Job descriptions

Every job description states the role purpose, key responsibilities, required and preferred qualifications, level, work mode, base location, travel expectations and any shift requirements. Job descriptions must not include requirements unrelated to the job, such as age limits, gender preferences or "local candidates only" unless the role is genuinely location-bound.

### 4.3 Sourcing channels

| Channel | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| Internal job postings | All roles up to L6 are posted internally for at least 7 days before or alongside external advertising (Chapter 23) |
| Employee referrals | See 4.4 |
| Company careers page and job portals | Standard channel for lateral hiring |
| Campus hiring | Through approved institutions; see 4.6 |
| Recruitment agencies | Only empanelled agencies with signed contracts; fees as per contract |
| Professional networks and social media | Recruiters may use them; candidate data is handled as per Chapter 31 |
| Rehires | See 4.7 |
| Diversity hiring programmes | Returnships, disability hiring and similar programmes, approved by the CHRO |

### 4.4 Employee referral programme

- Employees may refer candidates for open roles through the HRMS referral portal.
- If the referred candidate joins and completes **90 days**, the referrer receives a bonus of ₹25,000 to ₹60,000 by level of the role, paid with the next payroll.
- Not eligible: HR team members, the hiring manager and anyone on the interview panel for that role, and senior leaders at L8 and above.
- Referrals are evaluated through the same process as other candidates; a referral does not guarantee an interview.
- If two employees refer the same candidate, the first referral recorded in the portal counts.

### 4.5 Selection process

1. **Screening:** recruiter reviews the application and conducts a 20–30 minute screening call covering experience, expectations, notice period and work mode.
2. **Assessment:** a technical test, coding exercise, case study or work sample relevant to the role. Take-home tasks are limited to 4 hours of effort.
3. **Interviews:** at least two interview rounds for roles up to L4 and three for higher roles, including one with the hiring manager. Each interviewer submits structured written feedback in the HRMS within 24 hours.
4. **HR discussion:** covers culture, policies, compensation expectations and joining date.
5. **Hiring decision:** the hiring manager and HR agree on the selected candidate, based on documented feedback. Leadership roles require approval from the business unit head and CHRO.

### 4.6 Virtual and in-person interviews

- Interviews for hybrid and remote roles may be held entirely online using the Company's approved video platform.
- For on-site and hybrid roles, at least one interview round is in person where practical, unless the candidate is in another city.
- Candidates are informed in advance of the format, duration, panel and any tools needed.
- For online coding assessments, candidates may be asked to keep their camera on and share their screen. Candidates must not use unauthorised help, including AI tools, unless the assessment explicitly allows it.
- Interviews must not be recorded without the candidate's prior written consent.
- Candidates with disabilities may request accommodations, such as extra time, a sign language interpreter or an accessible venue.

### 4.7 Campus hiring

- Campus hires join as Graduate Engineer Trainees or equivalent, on-site or hybrid for their first 6 months.
- Offers are made at a standard CTC for each campus tier, approved annually by the CHRO and CFO.
- The Company honours campus offers. If a joining date must be deferred by more than 3 months, the candidate receives a stipend of ₹15,000 per month during the deferral, or may withdraw without penalty.

### 4.8 Rehires

Former employees who left in good standing and are marked "eligible for rehire" may be rehired. Rehired employees within 12 months of exit may have their previous service counted for leave accrual and seniority, subject to CHRO approval. Gratuity is computed according to law. Employees dismissed for misconduct are not eligible.

### 4.9 Candidate experience and timelines

| Stage | Target time |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledge application | 2 working days |
| Screening decision | 7 working days |
| Feedback after each interview round | 3 working days |
| Offer or regret after final round | 5 working days |

Candidates who are not selected after an interview receive a written regret. Recruiters must not make verbal promises on compensation or joining before the written offer.

### 4.10 Prohibited practices

- Asking candidates about marriage plans, pregnancy, religion, caste or family background.
- Requesting a security deposit, original certificates to be held by the Company, or any fee from a candidate.
- Approaching employees of a client to recruit them where the client contract prohibits it.
- Using unverified or false information about a candidate.

### 4.11 Recruitment fraud

The Company never asks candidates for money. Fake job offers using the Company's name should be reported to **careers-security@bragslabs.com**. Employees must not share interview questions or confidential hiring information externally.

### 4.12 Records

Application data, interview feedback and assessment results are kept for 12 months for unsuccessful candidates and for the duration of employment plus 7 years for hired candidates, and are handled as per Chapter 31.
