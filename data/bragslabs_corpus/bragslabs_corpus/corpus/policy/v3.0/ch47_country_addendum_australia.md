## Chapter 47. Country addendum: Australia

This addendum applies to employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd; the Fair Work Act and its National Employment Standards (NES) set minimum terms, and any applicable modern award or enterprise agreement adds to them.

### 47.1 Key laws

Fair Work Act 2009 and the National Employment Standards; applicable modern awards (for example, the Professional Employees Award for some technical roles); Sex Discrimination Act 1984 (including the positive duty to prevent sexual harassment); Disability, Age and Racial Discrimination Acts; state anti-discrimination laws; Work Health and Safety Acts (including psychosocial hazards); Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992; Paid Parental Leave Act 2010; Privacy Act 1988; state long service leave Acts.

### 47.2 Contracts and awards

- Every new employee receives the Fair Work Information Statement (and the Casual Employment Information Statement for casuals).
- The employment contract states whether a modern award covers the role. Award-covered employees receive at least award rates, penalty rates and allowances; salaries may be set under an annualised wage arrangement with an annual reconciliation where permitted.

### 47.3 Working hours

- Maximum of **38 ordinary hours a week**, plus reasonable additional hours. An employee may refuse unreasonable additional hours.
- Standard week of 38 hours, Monday to Friday.
- Award-covered employees receive overtime and penalty rates under their award.

### 47.4 Right to disconnect

Employees have a legal right to refuse to monitor, read or respond to contact from the Company, or from third parties about work, outside working hours, unless the refusal is unreasonable. Chapter 12 applies in Australia in line with this right.

### 47.5 Leave (Company policy)

| Leave | NES / statutory | Company policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual leave | 4 weeks a year (5 weeks for certain shift workers) | 4 weeks plus 3 Company days between Christmas and New Year; 17.5% leave loading where an award requires |
| Personal/carer's leave | 10 days paid a year, accumulating | 12 days paid a year, accumulating |
| Compassionate leave | 2 days per occasion | 5 days per occasion (Chapter 37) |
| Family and domestic violence leave | 10 days paid a year | As statutory |
| Community service and jury duty | As per NES | Jury duty at full pay |
| Public holidays | National and state public holidays | As statutory |
| Long service leave | Under state law (for example, around 8.67 weeks after 10 years in several states) | As statutory, with pro rata access after 7 years |

### 47.6 Parental leave

- **Unpaid parental leave:** up to 12 months for each parent with 12 months' service, with the right to request a further 12 months.
- **Government Paid Parental Leave:** **26 weeks** for children born or adopted from 1 July 2026, paid at the national minimum wage and shared between parents, with weeks reserved for each parent; superannuation is paid on Parental Leave Pay by the government.
- **Company paid parental leave:** 16 weeks at full pay for the birth parent or primary carer, and 4 weeks at full pay for the partner, paid in addition to government Parental Leave Pay, with superannuation contributions continued on the Company-paid portion.
- Return to work: the employee may return to their pre-leave role and may request flexible working arrangements.

### 47.7 Flexible working

Employees with 12 months' service who are parents, carers, have a disability, are 55 or older, are pregnant, or are experiencing family and domestic violence may request flexible working. The Company responds in writing within **21 days**, discusses the request, and may refuse only on reasonable business grounds, with reasons and alternatives. Refusals may be disputed at the Fair Work Commission.

### 47.8 Pay, superannuation and benefits

- Paid monthly; PAYG tax withheld. National minimum wage and award rates always met.
- **Superannuation guarantee:** at least **12%** of ordinary time earnings, paid to the employee's chosen or stapled fund; from 1 July 2026, superannuation is paid at the same time as wages under the payday super rules.
- Salary packaging, EAP, income protection and life cover through the super fund or Company policy, and private health insurance subsidy of AUD 1,000 a year.

### 47.9 Termination

| Continuous service | Minimum notice by employer |
| --- | --- |
| 1 year or less | 1 week |
| Over 1 year to 3 years | 2 weeks |
| Over 3 years to 5 years | 3 weeks |
| Over 5 years | 4 weeks |

Plus 1 week if the employee is over 45 and has at least 2 years' service. Contractual notice may be longer: 4 weeks for L1–L3, 3 months for L4+. Unfair dismissal protection applies after the minimum employment period (6 months, or 12 months for small businesses) where the employee earns below the high-income threshold or is award-covered. Redundancy pay under the NES ranges from 4 to 16 weeks' pay, depending on service. The Company follows a fair process, including a support person at meetings.

### 47.10 Harassment and safety

The Company meets its **positive duty** to take reasonable and proportionate measures to eliminate sexual harassment, sex discrimination and victimisation, and manages psychosocial hazards such as bullying, harassment and excessive workload under work health and safety laws. Workers may apply to the Fair Work Commission for a stop-bullying or stop-sexual-harassment order. Complaints go to the Australia Respect at Work Committee. Whistleblowers are protected under the Corporations Act.

### 47.11 Restraints

Post-employment restraints are enforceable only to the extent reasonable to protect legitimate business interests, and may be limited by changes to federal law on non-compete clauses for employees earning below the high-income threshold. The Australia HR lead confirms the current position before using any restraint.
