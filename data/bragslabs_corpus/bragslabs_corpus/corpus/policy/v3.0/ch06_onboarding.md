## Chapter 6. Onboarding

Every new joiner, in any work mode, should be equipped, connected and productive within their first 30 days, following a structured 90-day onboarding plan.

### 6.1 Pre-joining (offer acceptance to day 0)

| When | What happens | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer acceptance | Welcome email, HRMS pre-joining portal access, document checklist | HR |
| 10 days before joining | BGV initiated, laptop and accessories allocated, email ID created | HR, IT |
| 7 days before joining | Manager sends a welcome note and first-week schedule; buddy assigned | Manager |
| 5 days before joining | Remote and hybrid joiners: laptop and welcome kit shipped to home address with tracking | IT, Admin |
| 2 days before joining | Day-one logistics confirmed: office address and reporting time, or video link for remote joiners | HR |

### 6.2 Day one by work mode

**On-site and hybrid joiners**

- Report to the designated office at 9:30 am (or as informed) for an in-person induction.
- Complete joining formalities, collect the access card and laptop, and receive a desk.
- Meet the manager and buddy; lunch with the team where possible.

**Remote joiners**

- Join the virtual induction on the Company's video platform.
- Laptop and welcome kit are delivered before day one; IT runs a live setup session in the first hour.
- Identity is verified by video with the original ID document, and documents are signed electronically.
- The manager holds a video welcome call on day one.
- Remote joiners are invited to an in-person onboarding week at their base location within the first 90 days, with travel paid by the Company (Chapter 20).

**Off-site (client-deployed) joiners**

- Complete Company induction (in person or virtual) before reporting to the client site.
- Complete the client's own induction, security clearance and access formalities as directed by the delivery manager.
- Receive a client deployment briefing covering client contacts, conduct rules, escalation paths and safety arrangements (Chapter 11).

### 6.3 Mandatory induction modules

The following must be completed within **15 days** of joining:

1. Company overview, values and organisation structure
2. This HR policy manual and the work-mode framework
3. Code of conduct and ethics
4. Prevention of sexual harassment (POSH)
5. Information security and acceptable use
6. Data protection and privacy
7. Health, safety and emergency procedures, including home workplace safety for remote and hybrid employees
8. Anti-bribery and conflict of interest

Completion is tracked in the learning platform. Access to client systems may be withheld until security and data protection modules are complete.

### 6.4 The 30-60-90 day plan

| Period | Focus | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–30 | Learn: role, team, tools, processes, key stakeholders | Accounts and tools set up; first small deliverable completed; goals agreed |
| Days 31–60 | Contribute: take ownership of defined tasks with guidance | Regular contributions; feedback from manager and buddy |
| Days 61–90 | Perform: work independently on core responsibilities | 90-day review with manager; development needs identified |

The manager records the 30-day and 90-day check-ins in the HRMS. HR conducts a new-joiner survey at 30 and 90 days.

### 6.5 Buddy programme

Every new joiner gets a buddy: a peer from the same or a related team, ideally with the same work mode, who has at least 12 months' service. The buddy:

- meets the joiner at least twice in the first week and weekly for the first month;
- explains informal norms, tools and team practices;
- introduces the joiner to colleagues and communities;
- escalates any onboarding issues to the manager or HR.

Buddies receive recognition points under Chapter 25.

### 6.6 Remote onboarding standards

For remote and hybrid joiners, managers must also:

- schedule daily 15-minute check-ins in the first two weeks;
- set up introductions through video calls with at least five key colleagues in the first two weeks;
- share written documentation for the team's processes, tools and decision-making;
- add the joiner to all relevant chat channels, distribution lists and recurring meetings before day one;
- ensure the joiner meets the team in person at least once in the first 90 days, where feasible.

### 6.7 Assets issued at joining

| Item | On-site | Hybrid | Remote | Off-site |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Laptop with security software | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes, or client device as required |
| Access card | Yes | Yes | On request for office visits | Base office card plus client card |
| Headset with microphone | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| External monitor, keyboard and mouse | At desk | At desk; home set as per Chapter 14 | Yes | As per assignment |
| Ergonomic chair | At desk | Allowance as per Chapter 14 | Allowance as per Chapter 14 | No |
| Mobile phone or SIM | Role-based | Role-based | Role-based | Role-based |

Employees sign an asset acknowledgement in the HRMS and are responsible for the care of assets issued to them.

### 6.8 Statutory registration

HR completes PF (UAN linking), ESI where applicable, group insurance enrolment and professional tax registration within the time limits under law. Remote employees are registered according to their base location, and professional tax or labour welfare fund contributions follow the rules of the State where the law requires (Chapter 18).
