## Chapter 8. On-site working policy

On-site employees work from a Company office every working day; this chapter also sets the office rules that apply to hybrid and visiting remote employees whenever they are in an office.

### 8.1 Who works on-site

- Roles that need physical presence: facilities, IT infrastructure and network operations, hardware labs, front office, security and administration.
- Teams in secure Offshore Development Centres (ODCs) or client-mandated secure bays.
- Graduate trainees during their initial period (Chapter 2).
- Employees whose hybrid or remote arrangement has been revoked under 2.8.

### 8.2 Office hours and presence

On-site employees work the shift assigned to them (Chapter 12) at their base office, five days a week. Absence from the office during working hours for personal reasons needs prior manager approval and is recorded as leave or short leave. Visiting another Company office for work is recorded as on-duty in the HRMS.

### 8.3 Access and identification

- Every person in the office must wear a visible Company ID card at all times.
- Access cards are personal and must never be lent, shared, or used to let in another person (no tailgating).
- Lost cards must be reported to Security within 2 hours; a replacement fee of ₹300 applies after the first loss.
- Employees forgetting their card receive a temporary card for the day after identity verification; three instances in a month are reported to the manager.
- Restricted areas (server rooms, ODCs, labs, HR and finance record rooms) are accessible only to authorised staff.

### 8.4 Visitors

1. The host registers the visitor in the visitor management system at least 1 working day in advance.
2. Visitors show photo ID at reception, receive a visitor badge and are escorted at all times.
3. Visitors may not enter ODCs, server rooms or secure areas without written approval from the area owner.
4. Visitors' laptops and storage devices are declared at reception; photography is not permitted.
5. Family visits are allowed only in the reception or cafeteria area, outside secure zones.

### 8.5 Workspace

| Arrangement | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Assigned desks | For on-site employees and roles needing special equipment |
| Hot desks | Bookable desks for hybrid and visiting employees (Chapter 9) |
| Collaboration zones | Bookable meeting rooms, huddle spaces and project rooms |
| Quiet zones | Phone- and conversation-free areas for focused work |
| Wellness rooms | Rest, nursing mothers and medical needs; bookable for 30-minute slots |

Clean desk rule: at the end of each day, no confidential documents, notes or storage media are left on desks; laptops are locked in drawers or taken home; whiteboards in meeting rooms are wiped.

### 8.6 Facilities

- **Cafeteria:** subsidised meals as per the meal policy; meal cards credited monthly.
- **Transport:** shuttle services on fixed routes for general shifts; door-to-door cabs for late and night shifts (Chapter 12).
- **Parking:** allocated by Admin based on availability, with priority for persons with disabilities, pregnant employees and night-shift staff.
- **Crèche:** a crèche facility is available or tied up with a nearby provider for employees as required by law; details from HR.
- **Medical room:** first-aid and a visiting doctor on scheduled days; tie-up with a nearby hospital for emergencies.
- **Gym and recreation:** where available, governed by facility rules and booking schedules.

### 8.7 Office conduct

- Keep noise low in open work areas; take calls in phone booths or meeting rooms.
- Book meeting rooms only when needed and release unused bookings.
- Food is consumed only in the cafeteria and pantry areas, not at desks in ODCs.
- Personal mobile phones are not permitted in secure ODCs and must be stored in lockers.
- Smoking and vaping only in designated outdoor areas; alcohol is not permitted on Company premises except at events approved by the CHRO.
- Photography and recording inside offices is not allowed without Admin approval.
- Weapons, hazardous materials and pets are not permitted (except assistance animals).

### 8.8 Dress code

| Situation | Expected attire |
| --- | --- |
| Monday to Thursday | Business casual: collared shirts, blouses, kurtas, trousers, chinos, skirts, salwar suits, closed or formal footwear |
| Friday | Smart casual: neat jeans, polo shirts, tops, ethnic wear |
| Client meetings and client visits | Business formal or the client's dress code |
| Festivals and Company events | Traditional or event-specific wear as announced |

Clothing must be clean and not offensive. Religious and cultural attire is always welcome. Employees with medical needs may seek exceptions from HR.

### 8.9 Personal belongings

The Company is not responsible for loss of personal belongings in offices. Lockers are available on request. Security may inspect bags on entry and exit at secure facilities, in the presence of the employee; women employees are inspected only by women security staff.

### 8.10 Office closures

If an office closes because of weather, civil disruption, power or water failure or a public-health order, HR announces it by SMS, email and the HRMS app. On-site employees whose roles can be performed remotely will work from home; others are granted special paid leave for the closure period. Critical operations follow the business continuity plan.
