## Medical insurance

Medical cover is provided by Liffey Health Insurance DAC. It covers the employee from day one, with no waiting period for pre-existing conditions. You can cover your spouse or partner and children; dependants are covered at the Company rate set out in the plan. Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals and clinics. Mental health, maternity, day-care procedures and gender affirmation treatment are covered as per the plan terms. Claims for non-network treatment must be submitted within 30 days of discharge with itemised bills, prescriptions and discharge summaries.

## Dental and vision

Routine dental check-ups and an annual eye test are covered up to the plan limits; spectacles are reimbursed once every two years. Orthodontic treatment for children is covered at 50%.

## Life and accident cover

Life cover of 4× annual base salary is paid to your nominees if you die during employment, from any cause, anywhere in the world. Accident cover pays for accidental death and permanent disability, with weekly compensation for temporary disability. Keep your nominations up to date in the HRMS.

## Retirement

Your employer contributes 6% of eligible pay to the Company retirement plan. Employees not in the Company scheme are enrolled in the State auto-enrolment system.

## Leave

You receive 25 working days of paid annual leave, 10 public holidays, paid sick leave and family leave as set out in the Ireland addendum. Birth parents receive 16 weeks at full pay and partners 2 weeks at full pay, in addition to any statutory scheme.

## Wellbeing

BrightPath Employee Assistance offers up to 8 counselling sessions per issue a year for you and your family, by phone, chat, video or in person. You can also claim a wellbeing allowance for gym, yoga, sports or wellness apps, and join monthly wellbeing sessions. Employees aged 30 and above receive a free annual health check-up.

## Enrolment and changes

You are enrolled automatically on joining. Each year there is an enrolment window in October when you can add or remove dependants, buy voluntary top-up cover and change flexible-benefit choices. Outside the window, changes are allowed only for life events, within 30 days.

## How to make a claim

1. Check whether the provider is in the network. 2. For cashless treatment, show your insurance card and ID. 3. For reimbursement, upload itemised bills, prescriptions and reports in the insurer's app within 30 days. 4. Track the claim in the app; contact the benefits helpdesk if it takes more than 15 working days.

## Leaving BragsLabs

Medical cover ends on your last working day unless local law provides continuation (for example COBRA in the US). Life cover ends on the last working day. Retirement savings remain yours under the plan rules.

## Dependants

You may cover your spouse or partner (including same-sex and unmarried partners), children up to age 25, and, where the plan allows, parents or parents-in-law. Add dependants within 30 days of a life event; proof of relationship may be requested.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I add my newborn to the plan?**

Yes. Add the child in the HRMS within 30 days of birth; cover starts from the date of birth.

**Is mental health covered?**

Yes, both inpatient and outpatient mental health treatment are covered as per the plan, and the EAP is free.

**Does cover continue during unpaid leave?**

Medical cover continues during approved unpaid leave only if you pay the premium for that period.

**Are fertility treatments covered?**

Consultations are covered; treatment cover depends on the plan. You may take up to 5 days of paid leave a year for appointments.

**Who do I contact about benefits?**

The benefits helpdesk via hr@bragslabs.com, or your HR lead Siobhán Murphy.

## Plan detail 1: newborn cover

Covered for 30 days before and 60 days after hospitalisation, for expenses related to the same condition. Network providers bill the insurer directly.

## Plan detail 2: day-care procedures

Covered from day one with no waiting period; claims need the discharge summary and itemised bills. A deductible applies as set out in the plan document.

## Plan detail 3: alternative treatments

Covered in full up to the plan limit, subject to the treating doctor's advice and the insurer's reasonable and customary charges. Network providers bill the insurer directly.

## Plan detail 4: alternative treatments

Emergency treatment outside the country of employment is covered during business travel; leisure travel needs separate travel insurance. Network providers bill the insurer directly.

## Plan detail 5: second medical opinion

Covered when the procedure is listed in the insurer's schedule; no minimum hospital stay is required. A deductible applies as set out in the plan document.

## Plan detail 6: chronic disease management

Covered when the procedure is listed in the insurer's schedule; no minimum hospital stay is required. A deductible applies as set out in the plan document.
