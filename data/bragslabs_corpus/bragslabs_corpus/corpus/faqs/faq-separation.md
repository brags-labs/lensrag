# Employee FAQ: Discipline, resignation and exit

*Maintained by the HR Policy Office. Last reviewed 20 October 2026. If this FAQ and the Global HR Policy Manual differ, the manual and any later HR circular prevail.*

## Q1. How does principles work at BragsLabs?

- Every employee is presumed innocent until a fair process finds otherwise.
- You is told the allegations in writing and given a reasonable opportunity to respond.
- Decisions are based on evidence and are proportionate to the seriousness of the misconduct, your record and any mitigating factors.
- Similar cases are treated similarly across teams, locations and work modes.
- You may appeal any penalty.
- Managers consult HR before starting any formal step.

*Policy reference: Section 33.1.*

## Q2. Can you explain the rules on categories of misconduct?

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Minor misconduct | Repeated late arrival or late logins; not keeping calendar or status updated; repeated failure to mark attendance; dress code violations; minor misuse of Company resources; unprofessional behaviour in meetings or chats; not completing mandatory training on time |
| Serious misconduct | Unauthorised absence for 3–6 days; repeated unavailability during working hours while remote; failure to meet hybrid attendance requirements after warnings; insubordination; negligence causing loss; breach of security rules without malicious intent; working from an unapproved location; unauthorised recording of meetings; breach of client site rules |
| Gross misconduct | Fraud, theft, embezzlement; falsifying records, timesheets, claims or documents; bribery; sexual harassment (on IC findings); violence or threats; deliberate data leaks or misuse of confidential information; unauthorised moonlighting or working for a competitor; unapproved work from another country; working under the influence; serious breach of client trust; retaliation against a complainant or whistleblower; concealing a security incident; repeated serious misconduct |

This list is not exhaustive.

*Policy reference: Section 33.2.*

## Q3. Can you explain the rules on penalties?

| Penalty | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| Verbal counselling (recorded note) | First instance of minor misconduct |
| Written warning | Repeated minor misconduct or first serious misconduct; valid for 12 months |
| Final written warning | Further misconduct during a warning period or serious misconduct; valid for 12 months |
| Withholding of increment, bonus or promotion | Serious misconduct |
| Revocation of hybrid or remote working | Breaches related to work-mode rules or security |
| Removal from client deployment | Breach of client trust or client site rules |
| Recovery of loss | Where BragsLabs suffers financial loss through your misconduct or negligence, as permitted by law |
| Demotion | Serious misconduct, after inquiry |
| Suspension without pay (as penalty) | Only where permitted by applicable standing orders and law |
| Dismissal | Gross misconduct, after inquiry |

*Policy reference: Section 33.3.*

## Q4. What does the policy say about disciplinary procedure?

1. **Preliminary review:** the manager and HR gather facts. For minor issues, a counselling conversation may resolve the matter.
2. **Show-cause notice:** for serious or gross misconduct, HR issues a written notice describing the allegations, evidence and possible consequences. You replies in writing within **3 working days** (extendable on request).
3. **Decision on inquiry:** if the reply is satisfactory, the matter is closed. If not, and a major penalty may follow, a domestic inquiry is held.
4. **Domestic inquiry:** an impartial inquiry officer (not the complainant or a witness) conducts hearings. You may:
   - see the evidence relied on;
   - present their own evidence and witnesses;
   - cross-examine witnesses;
   - be accompanied by a co-employee.
5. **Inquiry report:** the inquiry officer reports findings on each charge.
6. **Decision:** the disciplinary authority (normally the business unit head with the CHRO) decides the penalty after considering the report and any representation from the employee.
7. **Written decision:** issued with reasons and the right of appeal.

Hearings may be held in person, or by secure video conference for remote and off-site employees, with the same safeguards. Recordings are made only with notice to all participants.

*Policy reference: Section 33.4.*

## Q5. What should I know about suspension pending inquiry?

Where misconduct is serious, or your presence could affect the investigation, client relationships or the safety of others, the employee may be suspended pending inquiry, and their system access may be restricted. During suspension:

- a subsistence allowance is paid as required by law, normally 50% of wages for the first 90 days and 75% thereafter if the delay is not due to the employee;
- the employee remains available for the inquiry and may not access Company systems or premises without permission;
- the inquiry is completed as quickly as possible, normally within 90 days.

If the employee is exonerated, they receive full pay for the suspension period, less the subsistence allowance paid.

*Policy reference: Section 33.5.*

## Q6. What does the policy say about absconding?

If an employee is absent without information for **7 consecutive working days**:

1. HR attempts to contact the employee and their emergency contact by phone, email and message.
2. HR sends a first notice to the registered address and email, asking the employee to report or explain within 7 days.
3. If there is no response, HR sends a second notice giving a further 7 days.
4. If there is still no response, your employment may be terminated after an inquiry if required, and the final settlement is processed less any notice-period recovery permitted by law.

For remote employees, HR may also request a welfare check through the emergency contact before any action.

*Policy reference: Section 33.6.*

## Q7. Can you explain the rules on appeals?

You may appeal in writing to the CHRO (or to the CEO if the CHRO was the disciplinary authority) within **7 working days** of the decision. The appeal authority reviews the process and the penalty and decides within 15 working days. The appeal decision is final within BragsLabs.

*Policy reference: Section 33.7.*

## Q8. What should I know about criminal proceedings?

Where misconduct may also be a criminal offence, BragsLabs may report it to the police and may continue its own disciplinary process independently.

*Policy reference: Section 33.8.*

## Q9. What does the policy say about records?

Warnings expire after 12 months for the purpose of escalation, but records are kept as per Chapter 31.

*Policy reference: Section 33.9.*

BragsLabs may pay basic pay (or wages, as the appointment letter states) in lieu of all or part of its notice. Dismissal for proven gross misconduct is without notice.

*Policy reference: Section 34.2.*

## Q12. What should I know about resignation process?

1. You submits the resignation in the HRMS, stating the reason (optional) and proposed last working day.
2. The manager holds a conversation within 3 working days to understand the reasons and, where appropriate, discuss retention.
3. The manager and HRBP accept the resignation in the HRMS within **5 working days**. The resignation can be withdrawn only with BragsLabs's agreement.
4. The manager and employee agree a handover plan.
5. The last working day is confirmed in writing.

*Policy reference: Section 34.3.*

## Q13. What does the policy say about notice period rules?

- You continues to work normally in their work mode during the notice period; BragsLabs may ask a remote or hybrid employee to work from an office for handover if reasonable.
- Notice periods may be shortened by mutual agreement. Any shortfall not waived may be recovered as basic pay for the shortfall days, or adjusted against earned leave balance with BragsLabs's approval.
- BragsLabs may place the employee on **garden leave** for part of the notice period, with full pay and benefits, during which they must not work for anyone else or access Company systems.
- Client-deployed employees agree their release date with the delivery manager to protect client commitments.

*Policy reference: Section 34.4.*

## Q15. How does retrenchment and redundancy work at BragsLabs?

Where roles must be reduced for business reasons, BragsLabs will:

- consider alternatives such as redeployment, retraining, reduced hiring and voluntary separation;
- select employees on fair, objective and documented criteria, following the "last in, first out" principle within a category where required by law, unless there are recorded reasons to depart from it;
- give the notice, or pay in lieu, and the retrenchment compensation required by the Industrial Relations Code (15 days' average pay for every completed year of continuous service for eligible workers), plus any enhanced severance BragsLabs decides;
- obtain government permission where required by law for the establishment's size;
- contribute to the Worker Re-skilling Fund as required by law;
- offer outplacement support and continuation of group health insurance for 3 months after exit;
- give retrenched employees preference for re-employment if BragsLabs hires for similar roles within one year, as required by law.

The same criteria and benefits apply to employees in every work mode.

*Policy reference: Section 34.6.*

## Q16. How does full and final settlement (F&F) work at BragsLabs?

The F&F is paid within **2 working days** of the last working day, as required by the Code on Wages. It includes:

- salary up to the last working day;
- earned leave encashment;
- statutory bonus and any variable pay due under the plan;
- pending reimbursements and allowances;
- notice pay where BragsLabs ended employment without full notice;
- retrenchment compensation, where applicable;

minus permitted recoveries such as notice shortfall, advances, unreturned assets and excess leave taken.

**Gratuity** is paid within 30 days of becoming payable. **PF** transfer or withdrawal is initiated by the employee online, and BragsLabs approves within the time required by EPFO.

If a dispute delays part of the settlement, the undisputed amount is paid on time.

*Policy reference: Section 34.7.*

## Q17. What does the policy say about documents issued?

- Relieving letter and experience letter (on or with the F&F, after asset clearance).
- Final payslip and F&F statement.
- Form 16 at the end of the financial year.
- Service certificate as required by law.

A relieving letter is not withheld to enforce a notice period beyond what the law and appointment letter allow.

*Policy reference: Section 34.8.*

## Q18. What does the policy say about post-employment obligations?

After leaving, former you must:

- keep Company and client information confidential;
- not use BragsLabs's intellectual property;
- not solicit Company employees or clients for 12 months, to the extent enforceable under local law;
- return any Company property later found in their possession.

Reasonable non-solicitation and confidentiality obligations continue after employment. Restrictions on working for a competitor after employment are generally not enforceable in India and are not imposed there, except as part of a lawful garden-leave period. In other countries, a post-employment non-compete is used only where local law permits it, for limited roles, and only as set out in the employment contract and country addendum.

*Policy reference: Section 34.9.*

## Q19. Can you explain the rules on alumni?

Former employees who left in good standing are invited to BragsLabs alumni network and may be considered for rehire (Chapter 4).

*Policy reference: Section 34.10.*
