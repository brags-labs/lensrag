# Employee FAQ: working at BragsLabs in Netherlands

*For employees of BragsLabs B.V. Last reviewed 18 August 2026. The Netherlands addendum (Chapter 42 of manual version 3.0) prevails if this FAQ differs.*

## Q1. What is my paid annual leave?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs B.V. receive **25** — paid annual leave (working days). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q2. What is my paid sick leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs B.V. receive **260** — paid sick leave at full pay (working days). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q3. What is my birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs B.V. receive **16** — birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs B.V. receive **2** — partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q10. Where can I find guidance on key laws?

Dutch Civil Code (Book 7); Working Hours Act (Arbeidstijdenwet); Work and Care Act (Wet arbeid en zorg); Gatekeeper Improvement Act (Wet verbetering poortwachter); Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act; Equal Treatment Act; Flexible Working Act (Wet flexibel werken); Works Councils Act (WOR); Whistleblowers Protection Act; EU GDPR and the Dutch GDPR Implementation Act; Working Conditions Act (Arbowet).

## Q11. Where can I find guidance on working time?

Standard week of 40 hours. The Working Hours Act limits work to 12 hours in a shift and 60 hours in a week, with an average of 48 hours a week over 16 weeks, and at least 11 hours' rest between shifts (once a week this may be shortened to 8 hours).

## Q12. Can you explain the rules on holidays and holiday allowance (Company policy)?

- **25 days' paid holiday** a year on a 5-day week (statutory minimum 20 days), plus Dutch public holidays: New Year's Day, Easter Monday, King's Day, Liberation Day (every year), Ascension Day, Whit Monday, Christmas Day and Boxing Day.
- Statutory holiday expires 6 months after the end of the year in which it was earned; extra-statutory days expire after 5 years.
- **Holiday allowance (vakantiegeld) of 8% of gross annual salary**, paid in May.

## Q13. What does the policy say about sickness?

- You reports sick to the manager and BragsLabs's occupational health service (arbodienst) on the first day.
- BragsLabs pays at least **70% of salary for up to 104 weeks** (and at least the minimum wage in the first year). **Company policy:** 100% in the first year and 70% in the second year.
- Reintegration follows the Gatekeeper Improvement Act: problem analysis by week 6, a plan of action by week 8, and regular evaluation. Employee and Company both have reintegration obligations.

## Q15. What should I know about pay, pension and benefits?

- Paid monthly; wage tax and national insurance contributions withheld.
- Statutory minimum wage (hourly) always met.
- Pension through BragsLabs's defined-contribution pension scheme, with employer contribution of 10%; transition to the new pension system under the Future of Pensions Act as applicable.
- Highly skilled migrants and employees recruited from abroad may qualify for the 30% ruling (tax-free allowance), subject to conditions; BragsLabs applies it where eligible.
- Home-working allowance and travel allowance as permitted under the work-related costs scheme.

## Q16. Where can I find guidance on flexible and remote work?

Under the Flexible Working Act, employees with 26 weeks' service may request a change in hours, working times or workplace (including home working). BragsLabs decides within 1 month and gives written reasons for a refusal. Home workplaces must meet Working Conditions Act standards; BragsLabs provides equipment and advice.

## Q17. How does termination work at BragsLabs?

- Dismissal requires either a UWV dismissal permit (for economic reasons or long-term illness) or a decision of the subdistrict court (for personal reasons such as performance or conduct), unless both parties agree a settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst), with a 14-day cooling-off period for the employee.
- **Statutory notice by employer:** 1 month (under 5 years' service), 2 months (5–10 years), 3 months (10–15 years), 4 months (15+ years), less one month when a UWV permit is used (minimum 1 month).
- **Transition payment (transitievergoeding)** of one-third of a month's salary per year of service is payable on dismissal initiated by the employer, from day one.
- Dismissal is prohibited during the first 2 years of sickness and during pregnancy and maternity leave, except in limited cases.
- Non-compete clauses in fixed-term contracts are valid only with written reasons for a compelling business interest.
