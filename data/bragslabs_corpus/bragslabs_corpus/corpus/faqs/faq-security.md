# Employee FAQ: IT security and privacy

*Maintained by the HR Policy Office. Last reviewed 20 October 2026. If this FAQ and the Global HR Policy Manual differ, the manual and any later HR circular prevail.*

## Q1. How does information classification work at BragsLabs?

| Class | Examples | Handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Published website content, marketing material | No restrictions |
| Internal | Policies, internal announcements, org charts | Company systems only; do not share externally |
| Confidential | Source code, designs, pricing, contracts, employee data, client project data | Need-to-know access; encrypted storage and transfer; no personal devices |
| Restricted | Client production data, personal data in bulk, credentials, security configurations, M&A information | Strictly limited access; secure environments only; never on home printers or removable media; may need ODC or VDI access |

*Policy reference: Section 30.1.*

## Q2. What should I know about acceptable use of Company systems?

- Use Company devices, accounts, email, chat, cloud and networks for business purposes; limited personal use is allowed if it is lawful, brief and does not affect work or security.
- Do not use Company systems to access, store or share illegal, pornographic, hateful, violent or pirated content.
- Do not install unlicensed or unapproved software, browser extensions or AI tools.
- Do not bypass security controls, disable antivirus or VPN, or use personal VPNs or proxies on Company devices.
- Do not use Company resources for cryptocurrency mining, personal businesses or outside employment.
- Do not share, lend or reveal passwords, tokens or access cards.

*Policy reference: Section 30.2.*

## Q3. How does device security work at BragsLabs?

- Company laptops are managed by IT with full-disk encryption, endpoint protection, automatic updates and remote-wipe capability.
- Screens lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity; lock manually (Windows+L or Ctrl+Cmd+Q) whenever you step away.
- Restart to install updates within **48 hours** of notification.
- USB storage devices are blocked by default; exceptions need Information Security approval.
- Personal devices may access only email, chat and approved apps, through BragsLabs's mobile device management (MDM) app with a passcode and encryption.

*Policy reference: Section 30.3.*

## Q4. What does the policy say about working securely from home (hybrid and remote)?

1. Work from a private space where others cannot see your screen or overhear calls.
2. Use BragsLabs VPN or zero-trust access for all Company systems.
3. Secure home Wi-Fi with WPA2/WPA3 and a strong password; change the router's default admin password.
4. Do not let family members or visitors use Company devices.
5. Do not print confidential or restricted information at home.
6. Store devices securely and out of sight when not in use.
7. Do not connect Company devices to smart TVs or shared home devices.
8. Use headphones for confidential calls, and turn off smart speakers and voice assistants in your workspace.

*Policy reference: Section 30.4.*

## Q5. Where can I find guidance on working securely while travelling and in public places?

- Avoid working on confidential matters in public places; use a privacy screen filter.
- Use only BragsLabs VPN on public or hotel Wi-Fi; prefer a mobile hotspot.
- Never leave devices unattended, in vehicles or checked baggage.
- Disable Bluetooth and automatic Wi-Fi connection when not needed.
- For travel to high-risk countries, use a loaner laptop provided by IT and follow the security briefing.

*Policy reference: Section 30.5.*

## Q6. What should I know about working securely at client sites?

- Follow client security policies, including clean desk, device restrictions, ODC rules and access controls.
- Use client systems only for client work, and Company systems only for Company work, unless the contract says otherwise.
- Never copy client data to Company or personal systems without written client approval.
- Return client devices and access cards at the end of the deployment.

*Policy reference: Section 30.6.*

## Q7. Where can I find guidance on passwords and authentication?

- Use unique passwords of at least **14 characters** or passphrases, stored in BragsLabs password manager.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all Company systems.
- Never approve an unexpected MFA prompt.
- Change a password immediately if you suspect it has been compromised.

*Policy reference: Section 30.7.*

## Q8. Where can I find guidance on email, phishing and social engineering?

- Treat unexpected emails, messages and calls with care, especially those asking for credentials, payments, gift cards or urgent action.
- Verify requests for payments or changes to bank details through a known phone number, never by replying to the email.
- Report suspicious emails using the "Report Phishing" button.
- Take part in phishing simulations and security awareness training every year.

*Policy reference: Section 30.8.*

## Q9. How does software development and source code work at BragsLabs?

- Store code only in Company-approved repositories; never on personal accounts or public repositories.
- Never commit secrets, keys or passwords to code.
- Follow secure coding standards and the review process.
- Use open-source components only as allowed by the open-source policy and licence checks.

*Policy reference: Section 30.9.*

## Q10. Can you explain the rules on monitoring?

To protect its systems and information, and to comply with law and client contracts, BragsLabs monitors the use of Company devices, networks, email, chat and cloud services, including security logs, data transfers, web access and system logins. Monitoring is proportionate, for security, compliance and investigations, and not for routine productivity tracking. Personal communications on Company systems may be seen as part of security monitoring, so employees should keep personal use to a minimum. Monitoring data is accessed only by authorised security staff and investigators, as described in Chapter 31.

*Policy reference: Section 30.10.*

## Q11. What does the policy say about security incidents?

Report immediately, and in any case within **1 hour**, to **security@bragslabs.com** or the IT helpdesk:

- lost or stolen devices, ID cards or tokens;
- suspected malware, phishing clicks or account compromise;
- data sent to the wrong person or uploaded to the wrong place;
- any suspected unauthorised access to Company or client data.

Do not attempt to investigate on your own or delete evidence. Prompt reporting is never penalised; concealing an incident is gross misconduct.

*Policy reference: Section 30.11.*

## Q12. Can you explain the rules on consequences?

Breaches of this chapter may lead to removal of access, revocation of hybrid or remote working, disciplinary action up to dismissal, and legal action where the law is broken.

*Policy reference: Section 30.12.*

## Q13. How does roles work at BragsLabs?

- **Data Principal:** the employee, candidate or former employee whose personal data is processed.
- **Data Fiduciary:** BragsLabs, which decides why and how the data is processed.
- **Data Processors:** vendors that process data for BragsLabs, such as payroll, BGV, insurance, travel and HRMS providers.
- **Grievance Officer / Data Protection Officer:** Ms. Priya Nair, Grievance Officer, India (grievance.officer@bragslabs.com), the contact for data protection queries and complaints.

*Policy reference: Section 31.1.*

| Identity and contact | Name, date of birth, photo, address, phone, email, emergency contacts | Employment, communication, safety |
| Government identifiers | PAN, Aadhaar (masked where possible), passport, UAN | Statutory compliance, tax, visas |
| Employment | CV, education, previous employment, BGV results, appointment terms | Hiring and employment |
| Pay and finance | Salary, bank account, tax declarations, reimbursements | Payroll, tax, benefits |

| Work-mode data | Home workplace address, work-mode agreements, travel and location registrations | Work-mode administration, safety, statutory compliance |

| Family | Spouse, children and parents' details | Insurance, nominations, emergency contacts |

BragsLabs does not collect caste or religion except where required by law or voluntarily provided for a specific purpose, such as an ERG or a holiday choice.

*Policy reference: Section 31.2.*

## Q15. Can you explain the rules on privacy notice and consent?

At the point of collection, candidates and employees receive a privacy notice explaining what data is collected, why, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and their rights. Where processing relies on consent (for example, health check-up results, voluntary diversity data, photographs for marketing, or BGV of certain kinds), consent is specific and may be withdrawn at any time through the HRMS. Processing needed for employment purposes, legal obligations or safety may continue as permitted by the DPDP Act.

*Policy reference: Section 31.3.*

## Q16. What does the policy say about principles?

- **Purpose limitation:** data is used only for the purposes stated.
- **Data minimisation:** only the data needed is collected.
- **Accuracy:** employees keep their data up to date in the HRMS.
- **Storage limitation:** data is kept only as long as needed (31.8).
- **Security:** data is protected by access controls, encryption, logging and vendor contracts.
- **Accountability:** BragsLabs can show how it complies.

*Policy reference: Section 31.4.*

## Q17. What should I know about your rights?

Employees and candidates may:

1. ask for a summary of their personal data processed and the processing activities;
2. ask for correction, completion or updating of inaccurate or incomplete data;
3. ask for erasure of data no longer needed for the purpose, subject to legal retention requirements;
4. withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
5. nominate a person to exercise their rights in case of death or incapacity;
6. raise a grievance with the Grievance Officer, and, if not satisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Requests are made through the HRMS privacy request form or by email to the Grievance Officer, and are answered within **30 days**.

*Policy reference: Section 31.5.*

## Q18. What does the policy say about access within the Company?

| Data | Who may access |
| --- | --- |
| Personal file and pay | You, HR, payroll, and the manager for limited job-related information |
| Performance | You, manager, skip-level manager, HR, calibration panel |
| Health and accommodation | HR and medical staff only; managers are told only what adjustments are needed |
| Disciplinary and IC records | HR, investigators, IC members, Legal, decision-makers |
| Security logs | Information Security and authorised investigators |

Managers must not ask employees for, or store, personal data beyond what they need, and must not keep copies of HR records on personal or unmanaged devices.

*Policy reference: Section 31.6.*

## Q19. How does sharing with third parties work at BragsLabs?

Personal data is shared only with data processors bound by contracts requiring confidentiality and security; with government authorities as required by law; with clients only where needed for a project (for example, name and BGV status for site access) and with employee notice; and with insurers, banks and benefits providers to provide benefits. BragsLabs does not sell employee data. Cross-border transfers, including between Company entities, follow the DPDP Act, the GDPR and UK GDPR (using standard contractual clauses or other approved safeguards) and other applicable laws, under BragsLabs's intra-group data transfer agreement.

*Policy reference: Section 31.7.*

## Q20. Where can I find guidance on retention?

| Record | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Unsuccessful candidate data | 12 months after the hiring decision, unless the candidate consents to longer |
| Employee personnel file | Employment plus 7 years |
| Payroll and statutory records | As required by labour and tax laws (typically 8 years) |
| IC and disciplinary records | Employment plus 7 years, or longer if a legal proceeding is pending |
| CCTV footage | 60 days unless needed for an investigation |
| Security logs | 1 year, or as required by law and client contracts |

*Policy reference: Section 31.8.*

## Q21. How does monitoring and privacy work at BragsLabs?

Monitoring of Company systems (Chapter 30) is limited to security, compliance and investigations. CCTV is used in office common areas and secure zones only, never in washrooms, changing rooms, wellness rooms or crèches. BragsLabs does not monitor employees' home environments, and does not require cameras to be on outside meetings.

*Policy reference: Section 31.9.*

## Q22. Where can I find guidance on personal data breaches?

Any suspected breach of personal data is reported to Information Security within 1 hour. BragsLabs assesses the breach and notifies the Data Protection Board and affected individuals as required by the DPDP Act and its rules.

*Policy reference: Section 31.10.*

## Q23. Where can I find guidance on employee responsibilities?

Employees who handle personal data of colleagues, candidates, clients or customers must complete data protection training, process data only as authorised, keep it secure, never share it via personal accounts, and report any breach immediately.

*Policy reference: Section 31.11.*
