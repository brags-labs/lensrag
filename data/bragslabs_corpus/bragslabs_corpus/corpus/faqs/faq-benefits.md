# Employee FAQ: Insurance and wellbeing

*Maintained by the HR Policy Office. Last reviewed 3 November 2026. If this FAQ and the Global HR Policy Manual differ, the manual and any later HR circular prevail.*

## Q1. How does group health insurance work at BragsLabs?

| Feature | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Sum insured | ₹5,00,000 per family per year (floater) |
| Who is covered | Employee, spouse or partner, up to 2 children (more if born together), and either parents or parents-in-law |
| Waiting period | None; pre-existing conditions and maternity covered from day one |
| Maternity | Up to ₹75,000 for normal delivery and ₹1,00,000 for caesarean |
| Newborn | Covered from day one |
| Room rent | Single private room |
| Day care, AYUSH, mental health | Covered as per policy terms |
| Gender affirmation treatment | Covered as per policy terms |
| Premium | Employee and spouse/children paid by BragsLabs; parents' premium shared 50:50 by BragsLabs and the employee |
| Top-up | You may buy a voluntary top-up of ₹5–20 lakh at group rates through payroll |

Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals across India, including in cities where remote employees live. Claims are supported by an on-site or virtual insurance help desk.

*Policy reference: Section 19.1.*

## Q2. Can you explain the rules on group term life insurance?

Cover of **3 times annual fixed CTC**, minimum ₹10,00,000, for death due to any cause, worldwide, during employment.

*Policy reference: Section 19.2.*

## Q3. What should I know about group personal accident insurance?

Cover of **3 times annual fixed CTC** for accidental death and permanent total disability, with proportionate cover for partial disability and weekly compensation for temporary disability, worldwide, 24 hours a day.

*Policy reference: Section 19.3.*

## Q4. How does travel and assignment insurance work at BragsLabs?

Employees travelling abroad on business, or on international assignment, receive travel or international health insurance covering medical emergencies, evacuation, repatriation, loss of baggage and passport, and trip delays (Chapter 20).

*Policy reference: Section 19.4.*

## Q5. What does the policy say about annual health check-up?

- Employees aged **30 and above** receive a free annual health check-up at empanelled diagnostic centres; spouses are covered at 50% cost.
- Home sample collection is available for remote employees.
- Results are confidential and shared only with the employee.

*Policy reference: Section 19.5.*

## Q6. Can you explain the rules on employee Assistance Programme (EAP)?

- Free, confidential counselling for employees and their families, 24x7, by phone, chat, video or in person.
- Up to 8 counselling sessions per issue per year, covering stress, anxiety, relationships, grief, parenting, addiction, finance and legal questions.
- BragsLabs receives only anonymous usage statistics, never the names of users.

*Policy reference: Section 19.6.*

## Q7. Where can I find guidance on mental health and wellbeing?

- Trained mental health first-aiders in every office and in virtual communities.
- Manager training on recognising distress, having supportive conversations and referring to the EAP.
- Monthly wellbeing sessions online and in offices: yoga, meditation, sleep, nutrition, financial wellness.
- Quarterly company-wide **recharge day** off, when possible, to reduce burnout.
- Wellbeing allowance of ₹12,000 a year for gym, sports, yoga classes, wellness apps or hobby courses, claimed with bills.

*Policy reference: Section 19.7.*

## Q8. Can you explain the rules on wellbeing for hybrid and remote employees?

- Ergonomics support (Chapter 14).
- Virtual social events, interest clubs and team rituals.
- Co-working and meet-up budgets (Chapter 10).
- Clear right-to-disconnect rules (Chapter 12).
- Annual pulse surveys on isolation, workload and wellbeing, with action plans reviewed by leadership.

*Policy reference: Section 19.8.*

## Q9. What does the policy say about flexible benefits plan?

You may structure part of their CTC into tax-efficient components, such as meal cards, telephone and internet, books and periodicals, fuel and vehicle maintenance, and leave travel, subject to the Income-tax rules and your tax regime. Choices are made in the HRMS each April.

*Policy reference: Section 19.9.*

## Q10. How does other benefits work at BragsLabs?

| Benefit | Summary |

| Transport | Shuttle and shift cabs (Chapter 8 and 12) |

*Policy reference: Section 19.10.*

## Q11. Where can I find guidance on death of an employee?

If an employee dies during employment, BragsLabs will:

- support the family through a dedicated HR contact;
- pay all dues (salary, leave encashment, gratuity, insurance claims, PF and EDLI support) to nominees as quickly as possible, normally within 30 days of receiving documents;
- continue group health insurance for the immediate family until the next policy renewal;
- pay an immediate ex-gratia amount of ₹2,00,000 for funeral expenses;
- consider compassionate employment or education support for children, at the CHRO's discretion.

*Policy reference: Section 19.11.*
