# Employee FAQ: working at BragsLabs in India

*For employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. Last reviewed 29 September 2026. The India addendum (Chapter 39 of manual version 3.0) prevails if this FAQ differs.*

## Q1. What is my paid annual leave?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. receive **18** — paid annual leave (working days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q2. What is my paid sick leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. receive **8** — paid sick leave at full pay (working days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q3. What is my birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. receive **26** — birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q4. What is my partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay?

Partners receive **7 working days** of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## Q5. What is my contractual notice period for L4 and above?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. receive **90** — contractual notice period for L4 and above (calendar days). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q6. What is my standard working hours per week?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. receive **40** — standard working hours per week. Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q7. What is my employer retirement contribution?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. receive **12** — employer retirement contribution (% of pay). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q8. What is my paid public holidays per year?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. receive **10** — paid public holidays per year. Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q9. What is my life cover?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. receive **3** — life cover (multiple of annual base salary). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q11. Where can I find guidance on state-specific rules?

Shops and Establishments Acts, professional tax, labour welfare fund, festival and national holiday requirements, and rules on women's night work differ by State. The India HR lead maintains a State compliance sheet for each office location: Karnataka (Bengaluru), Telangana (Hyderabad), Maharashtra (Pune) and Tamil Nadu (Chennai). Where a State law gives more leave or protection than this manual, the State law applies.

## Q12. How does standing orders work at BragsLabs?

Where the Industrial Relations Code requires standing orders for an establishment, BragsLabs adopts standing orders consistent with this manual and the model standing orders, and displays them at the establishment and on the intranet.

## Q13. Can you explain the rules on fixed-term employment?

Fixed-term employees receive the same wages, hours and benefits as permanent employees doing the same or similar work, and gratuity after 1 year of service.

## Q14. How does holidays work at BragsLabs?

Each office follows its published holiday list of 10 days, including Republic Day, Independence Day and Gandhi Jayanti, plus 2 floating holidays.
