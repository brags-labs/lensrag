# Employee FAQ: Learning and recognition

*Maintained by the HR Policy Office. Last reviewed 6 October 2026. If this FAQ and the Global HR Policy Manual differ, the manual and any later HR circular prevail.*

## Q1. What should I know about learning entitlements?

| Level | Annual learning budget | Minimum learning hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1–L3 | ₹25,000 | 40 hours |
| L4–L6 | ₹40,000 | 40 hours |
| L7 and above | ₹75,000 | 30 hours, plus leadership programmes |

The budget may be used for courses, certifications, conferences and books related to your current or next role. Unused budget does not carry forward.

*Policy reference: Section 24.1.*

## Q2. How does types of learning work at BragsLabs?

- **Mandatory compliance training:** code of conduct, POSH, information security, data protection, anti-bribery and health and safety, completed at joining and refreshed annually.
- **Technical training:** cloud, data, AI, security, programming languages, testing, architecture and domain skills.
- **Professional skills:** communication, client management, project management, writing and presentation.
- **Leadership development:** first-time manager programme (mandatory within 6 months of becoming a manager), mid-level and senior leadership programmes.
- **Learning on the job:** stretch projects, rotations, shadowing and mentoring.
- **Self-paced learning:** licences to online learning platforms for all employees.

*Policy reference: Section 24.2.*

## Q3. Where can I find guidance on access across work modes?

- All mandatory and core programmes are available online.
- In-person programmes are offered at multiple locations; remote employees who are nominated receive travel support.
- Live virtual sessions are recorded where possible and scheduled in core hours.
- Learning time is working time: you may block up to **4 hours a week** for learning, agreed with the manager.

*Policy reference: Section 24.3.*

## Q4. What should I know about certifications?

BragsLabs sponsors approved certifications relevant to the role (for example, cloud provider, security, project management and agile certifications):

1. You applies through the learning portal with manager approval.
2. BragsLabs pays the exam fee directly or reimburses it on passing.
3. Each certification may be sponsored for up to two attempts.
4. Paid leave of up to **2 days** is given for each exam.

*Policy reference: Section 24.4.*

## Q5. Where can I find guidance on higher education support?

Confirmed employees with at least 2 years' service and a rating of 3 or above may apply for support for part-time or online degree programmes relevant to their career path:

- up to 50% of fees, to a maximum of ₹5,00,000 over the programme;
- examination leave of up to 5 days a year;
- flexible hours where possible.

*Policy reference: Section 24.5.*

## Q6. Where can I find guidance on training agreements and repayment?

Where BragsLabs spends more than ₹50,000 on a single programme or certification for an employee, the employee signs a training agreement. If they resign within 12 months of completing the programme, they repay a pro rata share of the cost, reducing monthly. The repayment is reasonable and proportionate to the actual cost and is waived if BragsLabs ends the employment for reasons other than misconduct.

*Policy reference: Section 24.6.*

## Q7. Can you explain the rules on mentoring and coaching?

- Every employee may request a mentor from outside their reporting line through the mentoring platform.
- New managers and high-potential employees receive structured mentoring.
- Senior leaders may receive external executive coaching, approved by the CHRO.
- Reverse mentoring pairs senior leaders with early-career employees on technology and culture topics.

*Policy reference: Section 24.7.*

## Q8. What should I know about conferences and knowledge sharing?

You may attend external conferences with approval and present at them with BragsLabs's permission. After an external programme, the employee shares key learnings with the team. Internal communities of practice, tech talks and hackathons are open to all, with hybrid participation.

*Policy reference: Section 24.8.*

## Q9. Can you explain the rules on individual development plan (IDP)?

Each employee agrees an IDP with the manager during goal setting, listing 2–3 development goals, the learning activities to reach them, and target dates. Progress is reviewed at the mid-year and annual reviews.

*Policy reference: Section 24.9.*

## Q10. What does the policy say about responsibilities?

- **Employees** own their development, complete mandatory learning on time, and apply what they learn.
- **Managers** release time for learning, discuss development in one-to-ones, and create on-the-job learning opportunities.
- **The L&D team** designs programmes, manages vendors and budgets, and reports learning hours and effectiveness quarterly.

*Policy reference: Section 24.10.*

## Q11. What should I know about principles?

- Recognise specific behaviours and results, soon after they happen.
- Recognise fairly across teams, levels, locations and work modes.
- Combine public appreciation with meaningful rewards.
- Make it easy for anyone to recognise anyone.

*Policy reference: Section 25.1.*

## Q12. What should I know about recognition programmes?

| Programme | Who nominates | Frequency | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Peer recognition ("Kudos") | Any employee | Anytime | Points redeemable on the rewards platform; each employee has 500 points a quarter to give |
| Spot award | Manager | Anytime | ₹2,500–10,000 in points or vouchers |
| Team award | Manager or skip-level | Quarterly | Team celebration budget of ₹1,500 per person |
| Client hero award | Delivery manager, based on client appreciation | Quarterly | ₹25,000 and certificate |
| Innovation award | Any employee; judged by a panel | Half-yearly | ₹1,00,000; idea fast-tracked for funding |
| Annual excellence awards | Business unit leadership | Annual | Trophy, ₹2,00,000, CEO recognition |
| Long-service awards | Automatic | On milestones | See 25.4 |

*Policy reference: Section 25.2.*

## Q13. Where can I find guidance on delivering recognition in distributed teams?

- Awards are announced in all-hands meetings and on the intranet, never only in office events.
- Physical awards and gifts are shipped to remote and off-site employees.
- Team celebrations offer equivalent options: an in-office lunch, a meal voucher for remote members, or a virtual activity.
- Recognition data is reviewed by work mode each quarter to check fairness.

*Policy reference: Section 25.3.*

## Q14. What should I know about long-service awards?

| Years of service | Award |
| --- | --- |
| 5 years | Certificate, ₹10,000 and one extra day of earned leave |
| 10 years | Certificate, ₹25,000 and two extra days of earned leave |
| 15 years | Certificate, ₹50,000 and a gift |
| 20 years | Certificate, ₹75,000 and a family holiday voucher |
| 25 years | Certificate, ₹1,00,000 and recognition by the CEO |

*Policy reference: Section 25.4.*

## Q15. Where can I find guidance on tax?

Cash awards and vouchers above the tax-exempt limit are taxable as perquisites and are processed through payroll.

*Policy reference: Section 25.5.*

## Q16. What should I know about recognition and performance?

Recognition received during the year is visible to managers during performance reviews as supporting evidence, but recognition counts are not used as a performance metric.

*Policy reference: Section 25.6.*

## Q17. Can you explain the rules on misuse?

Trading recognition points, nominating oneself through others, or recognising in exchange for favours is misconduct. Points obtained through misuse are cancelled.

*Policy reference: Section 25.7.*
