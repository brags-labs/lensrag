# Employee FAQ: working at BragsLabs in Australia

*For employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd. Last reviewed 29 September 2026. The Australia addendum (Chapter 47 of manual version 3.0) prevails if this FAQ differs.*

## Q1. What is my paid annual leave?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd receive **20** — paid annual leave (working days). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q2. What is my paid sick leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd receive **12** — paid sick leave at full pay (working days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q3. What is my birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd receive **16** — birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q4. What is my partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd receive **4** — partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q5. What is my contractual notice period for L4 and above?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd receive **90** — contractual notice period for L4 and above (calendar days). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q6. What is my standard working hours per week?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd receive **38** — standard working hours per week. This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q7. What is my employer retirement contribution?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd receive **12** — employer retirement contribution (% of pay). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q8. What is my paid public holidays per year?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd receive **10** — paid public holidays per year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q9. What is my life cover?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd receive **3** — life cover (multiple of annual base salary). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q10. What should I know about key laws?

Fair Work Act 2009 and the National Employment Standards; applicable modern awards (for example, the Professional Employees Award for some technical roles); Sex Discrimination Act 1984 (including the positive duty to prevent sexual harassment); Disability, Age and Racial Discrimination Acts; state anti-discrimination laws; Work Health and Safety Acts (including psychosocial hazards); Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992; Paid Parental Leave Act 2010; Privacy Act 1988; state long service leave Acts.

## Q11. Can you explain the rules on contracts and awards?

- Every new employee receives the Fair Work Information Statement (and the Casual Employment Information Statement for casuals).
- The employment contract states whether a modern award covers the role. Award-covered employees receive at least award rates, penalty rates and allowances; salaries may be set under an annualised wage arrangement with an annual reconciliation where permitted.

## Q12. What should I know about working hours?

- Maximum of **38 ordinary hours a week**, plus reasonable additional hours. You may refuse unreasonable additional hours.
- Standard week of 38 hours, Monday to Friday.
- Award-covered employees receive overtime and penalty rates under their award.

## Q13. What should I know about right to disconnect?

Employees have a legal right to refuse to monitor, read or respond to contact from BragsLabs, or from third parties about work, outside working hours, unless the refusal is unreasonable. Chapter 12 applies in Australia in line with this right.

## Q15. What should I know about parental leave?

- **Unpaid parental leave:** up to 12 months for each parent with 12 months' service, with the right to request a further 12 months.
- **Government Paid Parental Leave:** **26 weeks** for children born or adopted from 1 July 2026, paid at the national minimum wage and shared between parents, with weeks reserved for each parent; superannuation is paid on Parental Leave Pay by the government.
- **Company paid parental leave:** 16 weeks at full pay for the birth parent or primary carer, and 4 weeks at full pay for the partner, paid in addition to government Parental Leave Pay, with superannuation contributions continued on BragsLabs-paid portion.
- Return to work: the employee may return to their pre-leave role and may request flexible working arrangements.

## Q16. Where can I find guidance on flexible working?

Employees with 12 months' service who are parents, carers, have a disability, are 55 or older, are pregnant, or are experiencing family and domestic violence may request flexible working. BragsLabs responds in writing within **21 days**, discusses the request, and may refuse only on reasonable business grounds, with reasons and alternatives. Refusals may be disputed at the Fair Work Commission.

## Q17. What should I know about pay, superannuation and benefits?

- Paid monthly; PAYG tax withheld. National minimum wage and award rates always met.
- **Superannuation guarantee:** at least **12%** of ordinary time earnings, paid to your chosen or stapled fund; from 1 July 2026, superannuation is paid at the same time as wages under the payday super rules.
- Salary packaging, EAP, income protection and life cover through the super fund or Company policy, and private health insurance subsidy of AUD 1,000 a year.

## Q18. How does termination work at BragsLabs?

| Continuous service | Minimum notice by employer |
| --- | --- |
| 1 year or less | 1 week |
| Over 1 year to 3 years | 2 weeks |
| Over 3 years to 5 years | 3 weeks |
| Over 5 years | 4 weeks |

Plus 1 week if the employee is over 45 and has at least 2 years' service. Contractual notice may be longer: 4 weeks for L1–L3, 3 months for L4+. Unfair dismissal protection applies after the minimum employment period (6 months, or 12 months for small businesses) where the employee earns below the high-income threshold or is award-covered. Redundancy pay under the NES ranges from 4 to 16 weeks' pay, depending on service. BragsLabs follows a fair process, including a support person at meetings.

## Q19. What does the policy say about harassment and safety?

BragsLabs meets its **positive duty** to take reasonable and proportionate measures to eliminate sexual harassment, sex discrimination and victimisation, and manages psychosocial hazards such as bullying, harassment and excessive workload under work health and safety laws. Workers may apply to the Fair Work Commission for a stop-bullying or stop-sexual-harassment order. Complaints go to the Australia Respect at Work Committee. Whistleblowers are protected under the Corporations Act.

## Q20. Where can I find guidance on restraints?

Post-employment restraints are enforceable only to the extent reasonable to protect legitimate business interests, and may be limited by changes to federal law on non-compete clauses for employees earning below the high-income threshold. The Australia HR lead confirms the current position before using any restraint.
