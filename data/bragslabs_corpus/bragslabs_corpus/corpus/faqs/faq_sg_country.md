# Employee FAQ: working at BragsLabs in Singapore

*For employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. Last reviewed 29 September 2026. The Singapore addendum (Chapter 44 of manual version 3.0) prevails if this FAQ differs.*

## Q1. What is my paid annual leave?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. receive **18** — paid annual leave (working days). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q2. What is my paid sick leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. receive **14** — paid sick leave at full pay (working days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q3. What is my birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. receive **16** — birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q4. What is my partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. receive **2** — partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q5. What is my contractual notice period for L4 and above?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. receive **60** — contractual notice period for L4 and above (calendar days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q6. What is my standard working hours per week?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. receive **40** — standard working hours per week. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q7. What is my employer retirement contribution?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. receive **17** — employer retirement contribution (% of pay). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q8. What is my paid public holidays per year?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. receive **11** — paid public holidays per year. This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q9. What is my life cover?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. receive **3** — life cover (multiple of annual base salary). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q10. Where can I find guidance on key laws and guidelines?

Employment Act 1968; Workplace Fairness Act 2025 (as brought into force); Child Development Co-Savings Act; Central Provident Fund Act; Retirement and Re-employment Act; Employment of Foreign Manpower Act; Work Injury Compensation Act 2019; Workplace Safety and Health Act; Protection from Harassment Act; Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA); Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices; Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests; Tripartite Advisory on Managing Excess Manpower and Responsible Retrenchment.

## Q11. Can you explain the rules on employment terms and work passes?

- Key employment terms are given in writing within 14 days of starting; itemised payslips are issued with each salary payment.
- Foreign you must hold a valid work pass (Employment Pass, S Pass or other) before starting work. Employment Pass applications follow the COMPASS framework, and roles are advertised on MyCareersFuture where required.

## Q12. What does the policy say about working hours and overtime?

- Standard week of 40 hours, Monday to Friday.
- Employees covered by Part IV of the Employment Act (workmen earning up to the statutory threshold and non-workmen earning up to the statutory threshold) work no more than 44 hours a week, are paid overtime at 1.5× the hourly basic rate, and may not work more than 72 hours of overtime a month.
- Other employees' hours and overtime follow their contract.

## Q14. How does parental leave work at BragsLabs?

- **Maternity:** 16 weeks of Government-Paid Maternity Leave for eligible mothers of Singapore citizen children (12 weeks under the Employment Act otherwise); BragsLabs tops up to full pay for 16 weeks for all mothers.
- **Paternity:** 4 weeks of Government-Paid Paternity Leave (mandatory for eligible fathers of children born from 1 April 2025); BragsLabs gives at least 2 weeks at full pay to all fathers and partners.
- **Shared Parental Leave:** 10 weeks, government-paid, shared between eligible parents of Singapore citizen children born from 1 April 2026.
- **Adoption:** 12 weeks of Government-Paid Adoption Leave for eligible mothers.

## Q15. Where can I find guidance on flexible work arrangement requests?

In line with the Tripartite Guidelines, employees who have completed probation may make formal written requests for flexible work arrangements (flexi-place, flexi-time or flexi-load). BragsLabs responds in writing within **2 months**, considers the request properly, and gives reasons if it rejects it.

## Q16. Can you explain the rules on pay, CPF and benefits?

- Salary is paid at least monthly, within 7 days after the end of the salary period; overtime pay within 14 days.
- **CPF:** contributions for Singapore citizens and permanent residents at the rates for their age band (for employees aged 55 and below, 17% employer and 20% employee of wages up to the CPF wage ceiling).
- Skills Development Levy paid for all employees.
- Group hospitalisation and surgical insurance, outpatient medical and dental benefits, group term life at 3× annual salary, and EAP.
- Work injury compensation insurance for all employees, as required.

## Q17. What does the policy say about retirement and re-employment?

BragsLabs does not dismiss employees because of age before the statutory retirement age, and offers re-employment to eligible employees up to the statutory re-employment age, as set under the Retirement and Re-employment Act.

## Q18. How does termination and retrenchment work at BragsLabs?

| Service | Statutory minimum notice (if contract is silent) |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 26 weeks | 1 day |
| 26 weeks to less than 2 years | 1 week |
| 2 years to less than 5 years | 2 weeks |
| 5 years or more | 4 weeks |

Contractual notice: 1 month during and after probation for L1–L3; 2 months for L4+. Retrenchment follows the Tripartite Advisory, with retrenchment benefits of 1 month of salary per year of service for employees with 2 or more years' service, and Mandatory Retrenchment Notification to MOM where required. Wrongful dismissal claims may be brought at the Tribunal for Employment Claims.

## Q19. What should I know about fair employment and harassment?

BragsLabs follows the Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices and the Workplace Fairness Act once in force, including a grievance-handling process and protection against retaliation. Harassment complaints are handled by the Singapore Respect at Work Committee; serious harassment may also be an offence under the Protection from Harassment Act.
