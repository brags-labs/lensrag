# Employee FAQ: Client sites, field work and assignments

*Maintained by the HR Policy Office. Last reviewed 6 October 2026. If this FAQ and the Global HR Policy Manual differ, the manual and any later HR circular prevail.*

## Q1. Where can I find guidance on types of off-site work?

| Type | Description | Typical duration | Governing document |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client-site deployment (domestic) | Working at a client's office in the country of employment as part of a project | Weeks to years | Deployment letter |
| Field work | Installations, support visits, audits, training delivery, sales visits | Hours to days | Travel request |
| Short-term business travel abroad | Meetings, workshops, transitions, go-lives | Up to 90 days | Travel request and business visa |
| Onsite assignment abroad | Working at a client or Company location outside the country of employment | 3 months to 3 years | International assignment letter |
| Secondment | Working for a group company or partner under their direction | As agreed | Secondment agreement |

*Policy reference: Section 11.1.*

## Q2. What does the policy say about client-site deployment in the country of employment?

**Deployment letter.** Before reporting, the employee receives a deployment letter stating the client, site address, project, start and expected end dates, reporting contacts at BragsLabs and at the client, working hours, allowances and any special conditions.

**Employee responsibilities**

- Follow the client's site rules, security procedures, working hours and dress code, in addition to this manual. Where the two conflict, the stricter rule applies, unless it would breach law or this manual's POSH and safety standards.
- Use client systems only for the client's work and only as authorised; never transfer client data to Company or personal devices unless the contract permits it.
- Report to BragsLabs delivery manager weekly and record time in BragsLabs timesheet system.
- Represent BragsLabs professionally; do not discuss Company commercial terms, other clients or internal matters with client staff.
- Raise any concern about client behaviour, safety, harassment or unethical requests immediately with the delivery manager and HRBP.

**Company responsibilities**

- Confirm that the client site meets basic safety, hygiene and POSH standards before deployment.
- Make sure the employee knows how to reach BragsLabs's Internal Committee, HR and emergency helpline.
- Pay all salary, benefits and approved allowances; the employee does not receive pay from the client.
- Review the deployment every 6 months and before any extension.

**Client-site allowances.** Where the client site is in your base city, no allowance applies unless the commute is more than 25 km each way, in which case a conveyance allowance of ₹400 per day is paid. Where the client site is in another city, Chapter 20 applies (travel, stay and per diem), or a relocation package for deployments over 6 months.

*Policy reference: Section 11.2.*

## Q3. How does field work work at BragsLabs?

- Field visits are planned and approved in advance through the travel or service ticket system.
- Field employees share their itinerary with their manager and check in at the start and end of each visit using the field app or a message.
- You must not visit unsafe or isolated locations alone after dark; women employees on late field visits receive a cab and a check-in call.
- Local conveyance is reimbursed as per Chapter 21; use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at ₹4 per km for two-wheelers and ₹12 per km for cars.
- Tools, equipment and spares issued for field work are recorded and returned after use.

*Policy reference: Section 11.3.*

## Q4. What does the policy say about short-term business travel abroad?

Short trips abroad follow the travel policy (Chapter 20). You must travel on the correct visa for the activity; working on a visitor visa where a work visa is required is not permitted. Legal and the immigration team confirm visa requirements before travel.

*Policy reference: Section 11.4.*

## Q5. What should I know about international onsite assignments?

**Selection.** Assignments are offered based on project need, skills, performance (rating 3 or above), and willingness. Declining an assignment for genuine personal reasons will not affect performance ratings.

**Assignment letter.** It states the host country and location, duration, role, host-country compensation, allowances, tax arrangements, benefits, leave and holidays, home leave, repatriation and any repayment obligations.

**Typical assignment support**

| Item | Policy |
| --- | --- |
| Visa and work permit | Arranged and paid by BragsLabs, including dependants on long-term assignments where permitted |
| Travel | Economy-class airfare at start and end; dependants' fares for assignments over 12 months |
| Host-country pay | At least the host-country legal minimum and the client contract rate requirements, paid as per local law |
| Home salary | Continues or is adjusted as per assignment letter |
| Accommodation | Company-arranged accommodation or a housing allowance for the first 30 days and as agreed thereafter |
| Insurance | Comprehensive international health and travel insurance as required by the host country |
| Tax | Tax advice and filing support in the home and host countries; tax equalisation where specified |
| Home leave | One trip to the home country per year for assignments over 12 months |
| Social security | As required by host-country law and any social security agreement between the home and host countries |
| Emergency support | 24x7 assistance service, medical evacuation cover and emergency repatriation |

**Compliance.** Assignees must comply with host-country laws, immigration conditions, tax registration and local working-time rules, and must keep their passport and visa valid. Overstaying a visa or working outside the visa conditions is gross misconduct.

**Repayment.** Where the employee resigns during an assignment or within 6 months of return, they may be required to repay a pro rata share of visa, relocation and airfare costs, as stated in the assignment letter and as permitted by law.

**Repatriation.** BragsLabs plans your return role at least 60 days before the assignment ends. You returns to a role at the same or a higher level in the home country.

*Policy reference: Section 11.5.*

## Q6. What should I know about secondments?

During a secondment, the seconded employee works under the day-to-day direction of the host organisation, but BragsLabs remains the employer for pay, benefits and this manual. The secondment agreement covers confidentiality, intellectual property and the process for ending the secondment early.

*Policy reference: Section 11.6.*

## Q7. Can you explain the rules on safety and emergencies off-site?

All off-site you must register their location and travel in BragsLabs's travel and safety system, carry the emergency helpline number, and follow the emergency procedures in Chapter 32. In any emergency involving personal safety, the employee should first get to safety and then inform the manager and BragsLabs emergency helpline.

*Policy reference: Section 11.7.*
