# Employee FAQ: Conduct, gifts and outside work

*Maintained by the HR Policy Office. Last reviewed 20 October 2026. If this FAQ and the Global HR Policy Manual differ, the manual and any later HR circular prevail.*

## Q1. Where can I find guidance on core values in practice?

| Value | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Integrity | Being honest in work, records, timesheets, claims and communication; keeping commitments |
| Respect | Treating everyone with dignity, listening, disagreeing politely, valuing diversity |
| Ownership | Taking responsibility for outcomes, raising problems early, fixing mistakes |
| Client focus | Understanding client needs, protecting client interests and data |
| Collaboration | Sharing knowledge, helping colleagues, including remote team members |
| Excellence | Delivering quality work, learning continuously |

*Policy reference: Section 26.1.*

## Q2. How does general standards work at BragsLabs?

All you must:

1. Comply with all applicable laws, regulations and Company policies.
2. Perform their duties diligently and follow reasonable instructions.
3. Be honest in all records, including attendance, timesheets, expense claims and performance evidence.
4. Protect Company and client assets, information and reputation.
5. Avoid conflicts of interest (Chapter 27).
6. Treat colleagues, clients, vendors and the public with respect.
7. Report breaches of this code through the channels in Chapter 29.

*Policy reference: Section 26.2.*

## Q3. What does the policy say about respectful workplace?

The following are prohibited in person and online:

- bullying: repeated, unreasonable behaviour that intimidates, humiliates or undermines someone;
- abusive, threatening or obscene language or gestures;
- spreading rumours, gossip or false information about colleagues;
- jokes, memes or comments that demean people for any protected characteristic;
- physical violence or threats of violence;
- deliberately excluding a colleague from work communications or meetings to isolate them.

*Policy reference: Section 26.3.*

## Q4. What does the policy say about virtual meeting etiquette?

- Join on time; let the organiser know in advance if you cannot attend.
- Keep the camera on for team meetings, one-to-ones and client calls, unless bandwidth, health or a valid reason prevents it.
- Use a neutral or Company-provided background in client meetings.
- Mute when not speaking; avoid multitasking that makes you unresponsive.
- Dress appropriately for the meeting, as you would in the office.
- Do not record meetings without informing all participants; Company recordings are stored only on approved systems.
- Use the chat respectfully; it is part of the meeting record.
- Make sure no one else in your home can see or hear confidential discussions.

*Policy reference: Section 26.4.*

## Q5. What should I know about written communication and chat?

- Be clear, polite and professional in emails and chats, including informal channels.
- Use public team channels for work discussions so information is shared; use direct messages for personal or sensitive matters.
- Do not send work messages to colleagues' personal accounts or personal phone numbers outside working hours unless urgent (Chapter 12).
- Remember that Company messages are Company records and may be reviewed in investigations.
- Do not forward confidential information to personal email or chat accounts.

*Policy reference: Section 26.5.*

## Q6. What should I know about conduct at client sites and in the field?

- Follow the client's rules, conduct standards and security instructions.
- Never discuss Company pricing, other clients or internal issues with client staff.
- Do not accept employment discussions from the client without informing the delivery manager, where the client contract restricts this.
- Report any unethical, unsafe or harassing behaviour by client staff to the delivery manager and HRBP.

*Policy reference: Section 26.6.*

## Q7. Where can I find guidance on social media and public statements?

- Only authorised spokespersons speak on behalf of BragsLabs to media, analysts or investors.
- You may mention their employment on professional networks but must make clear that personal views are their own.
- Never post confidential information, client names or projects without approval, photos of secure areas, screens or whiteboards, or derogatory content about colleagues, clients or competitors.
- Do not engage in online harassment or hate speech, in a personal or professional capacity, that could harm BragsLabs's reputation or colleagues' wellbeing.

*Policy reference: Section 26.7.*

## Q8. Where can I find guidance on use of AI tools?

- Use only AI tools approved by BragsLabs for work.
- Do not enter confidential, client or personal data into AI tools unless the tool is approved for that data classification.
- Review AI-generated content for accuracy, bias and intellectual property issues before using it; the employee remains responsible for the output.
- Disclose AI use where the client contract, project rules or academic integrity requires it.

*Policy reference: Section 26.8.*

## Q9. How does substance use work at BragsLabs?

Working under the influence of alcohol, drugs or any substance that impairs judgement is prohibited, whether at the office, at home during working hours, at a client site or at a Company event. Possession or sale of illegal drugs is gross misconduct. Employees taking prescription medication that affects safety-critical work should inform their manager confidentially. Support for substance-related problems is available through the EAP.

*Policy reference: Section 26.9.*

## Q10. What should I know about political and religious activity?

You may hold and express personal political and religious views, but must not use Company time, resources, email or premises for campaigning, fundraising or proselytising, and must not pressure colleagues.

*Policy reference: Section 26.10.*

## Q11. What does the policy say about personal relationships at work?

Consensual relationships between you must be disclosed to HR where one person has authority over, or influences decisions about, the other. HR will arrange a change in reporting lines to avoid conflicts. Failure to disclose such a relationship is a breach of this code.

*Policy reference: Section 26.11.*

## Q12. What does the policy say about legal compliance?

You must comply with anti-corruption, competition, export control, sanctions, insider trading (where BragsLabs or clients are listed) and environmental laws. Questions go to the Legal team.

*Policy reference: Section 26.12.*

## Q13. What does the policy say about consequences?

Breaches of this code are handled under Chapter 33. Managers who know of breaches and fail to act may also face disciplinary action.

*Policy reference: Section 26.13.*

## Q14. How does what is a conflict of interest work at BragsLabs?

A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal interests, relationships or activities could influence, or appear to influence, their decisions for BragsLabs. Common examples:

| Situation | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Financial interest | Holding shares in a vendor, client or competitor (beyond small holdings in listed companies under 1%) |
| Family and relationships | A relative working at a vendor the employee selects, or reporting to the employee |
| Outside work | Freelancing for a client or competitor |
| Corporate opportunity | Taking for oneself a business opportunity discovered through work |
| Board positions | Serving as a director of another company |
| Use of position | Using Company information or contacts for personal benefit |

*Policy reference: Section 27.1.*

## Q15. How does disclosure work at BragsLabs?

- Employees declare actual or potential conflicts in the HRMS at joining, annually by **30 April**, and whenever a new conflict arises.
- HR and your manager (and Legal where needed) review each declaration and decide on safeguards, such as removing the employee from a decision.
- Failure to declare a conflict is a breach of the code of conduct, even if no harm results.

*Policy reference: Section 27.2.*

## Q16. What does the policy say about outside employment and moonlighting?

Work modes that give employees more flexibility make clear rules on outside work especially important.

**Not permitted:**

- any paid or unpaid work for a competitor, client, vendor or partner of BragsLabs;
- any outside work during BragsLabs's working hours or using Company equipment, systems, information or intellectual property;
- holding a second full-time or part-time job with another employer, in any work mode;
- outside work that affects your performance, availability or rest.

**Permitted with prior written approval from the manager and HR:**

- occasional paid teaching, writing, speaking or consulting in an unrelated field;
- running a family business that does not compete with BragsLabs;
- open-source contributions in a personal capacity (see 27.4);
- directorships in non-competing or non-profit organisations.

**Permitted without approval:** unpaid social, charitable, religious, community or hobby activities outside working hours that do not conflict with BragsLabs's interests.

Unauthorised moonlighting is gross misconduct and may lead to dismissal.

*Policy reference: Section 27.3.*

## Q17. Can you explain the rules on personal projects and intellectual property?

Personal projects created entirely outside working hours, without Company resources or confidential information, and unrelated to BragsLabs's business, belong to the employee. To avoid disputes, employees should declare significant personal projects or open-source work in the HRMS. Contributions to open source on Company time follow the open-source policy and need CTO approval.

*Policy reference: Section 27.4.*

## Q18. Where can I find guidance on gifts and hospitality?

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Acceptable gifts | Occasional, modest gifts up to ₹2,000 in value, such as festival sweets, diaries or Company-branded items |
| Must be declared | Gifts above ₹2,000 are declined or handed to HR; if refusal would offend, accept and declare within 7 days |
| Never acceptable | Cash, gift cards, vouchers, loans, shares, personal services, travel paid by a vendor, or any gift during a tender, negotiation or audit |
| Hospitality | Business meals and events with a clear business purpose are acceptable if reasonable and not lavish; declare those above ₹5,000 per person |
| Giving gifts | Only Company-approved gifts, within limits, recorded in the gift register; never to public officials without Legal approval |

Gifts may be sent to remote employees at home; the same rules apply wherever a gift is received.

*Policy reference: Section 27.5.*

## Q19. Where can I find guidance on anti-bribery and corruption?

BragsLabs has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption, in line with the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 and other applicable anti-corruption laws, including those of other countries where BragsLabs works.

- Never offer, promise, give, ask for or accept anything of value to improperly influence a business decision or gain an advantage.
- **Facilitation payments** (small payments to speed up routine government actions such as permits or customs clearance) are prohibited.
- **Public officials:** any interaction involving gifts, hospitality or payments to government officials or employees of state-owned enterprises needs prior Legal approval.
- **Third parties:** agents, consultants and vendors acting for BragsLabs are subject to due diligence and anti-bribery clauses.
- **Political contributions** on behalf of BragsLabs are not permitted without Board approval and compliance with law.
- **Charitable donations and CSR** are made only through approved processes and must not be used to disguise a bribe.

If you are asked for a bribe, refuse, report it immediately to Legal or the Ethics Committee, and record the details. If you face a threat to personal safety, comply with the demand to stay safe and report it as soon as possible; you will not face disciplinary action.

*Policy reference: Section 27.6.*

## Q20. What should I know about insider information?

Employees who have access to unpublished price-sensitive information about BragsLabs (if listed) or about a client or partner must not trade in their securities or share the information with anyone, in line with SEBI insider trading regulations.

*Policy reference: Section 27.7.*

## Q21. What should I know about reporting?

Any suspected conflict, bribe or improper gift is reported to the manager, HR, Legal or through the whistleblower channel (Chapter 29).

*Policy reference: Section 27.8.*
