# Employee FAQ: working at BragsLabs in Germany

*For employees of BragsLabs GmbH. Last reviewed 18 August 2026. The Germany addendum (Chapter 41 of manual version 3.0) prevails if this FAQ differs.*

## Q1. What is my paid annual leave?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs GmbH receive **28** — paid annual leave (working days). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q2. What is my paid sick leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs GmbH receive **30** — paid sick leave at full pay (working days). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q3. What is my birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs GmbH receive **16** — birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q4. What is my partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs GmbH receive **2** — partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q5. What is my contractual notice period for L4 and above?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs GmbH receive **90** — contractual notice period for L4 and above (calendar days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q6. What is my standard working hours per week?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs GmbH receive **40** — standard working hours per week. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q7. What is my employer retirement contribution?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs GmbH receive **20** — employer retirement contribution (% of pay). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q8. What is my paid public holidays per year?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs GmbH receive **10** — paid public holidays per year. This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q9. What is my life cover?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs GmbH receive **2** — life cover (multiple of annual base salary). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q10. Where can I find guidance on key laws?

Civil Code (BGB); Working Time Act (Arbeitszeitgesetz); Federal Leave Act (Bundesurlaubsgesetz); Continued Remuneration Act (Entgeltfortzahlungsgesetz); Maternity Protection Act (Mutterschutzgesetz); Federal Parental Allowance and Parental Leave Act (BEEG); Protection Against Dismissal Act (Kündigungsschutzgesetz); Works Constitution Act (Betriebsverfassungsgesetz); General Equal Treatment Act (AGG); Part-Time and Fixed-Term Employment Act; Minimum Wage Act; Pay Transparency Act; EU GDPR and Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG); Whistleblower Protection Act (HinSchG); Evidence Act (Nachweisgesetz).

## Q11. Where can I find guidance on works council?

Where a works council (Betriebsrat) exists, it has co-determination rights over, among other things, working time arrangements, overtime, mobile and remote work rules, technical systems capable of monitoring performance or behaviour, leave scheduling principles and pay structures. Global policies in these areas take effect in Germany only through a works agreement (Betriebsvereinbarung) where required. The works council must be consulted before every dismissal; a dismissal without consultation is invalid.

## Q12. What should I know about working time and time recording?

- Standard week of 40 hours, Monday to Friday.
- Maximum 8 hours a day, extendable to 10 hours if the average over 6 months does not exceed 8 hours a day.
- At least 11 hours' rest between working days; breaks of 30 minutes after 6 hours and 45 minutes after 9 hours.
- Sunday and public holiday work is generally prohibited.
- Working time is recorded for all employees, including remote employees, as required by German case law, using BragsLabs time-recording system.
- Overtime is compensated by time off or pay as set out in the employment contract or works agreement.

## Q13. How does holidays (Company policy) work at BragsLabs?

**28 working days' paid holiday** a year on a 5-day week, above the statutory 20 days, plus the public holidays of the federal state (Bundesland) where the employee works. Holiday should be taken in the calendar year; statutory holiday carried over must be used by 31 March of the following year, and BragsLabs reminds employees in good time so that holiday does not lapse unknowingly.

## Q14. What does the policy say about sickness?

Employees notify their manager on the first day of illness. A medical certificate (electronic eAU retrieved by BragsLabs from the health insurer) is required from the fourth day. BragsLabs continues full pay for up to **6 weeks** per illness; afterwards, statutory health insurance pays sick pay (Krankengeld), and BragsLabs may top up for a further 6 weeks, paying the difference between Krankengeld and net salary.

## Q15. Can you explain the rules on family leave?

- **Maternity protection:** 6 weeks before and 8 weeks after birth (12 weeks after premature or multiple births), with maternity pay from the health insurer and a Company top-up to full net pay. Dismissal protection applies from pregnancy until 4 months after birth.
- **Parental leave (Elternzeit):** up to 3 years per parent per child, with state parental allowance (Elterngeld); dismissal protection during parental leave.
- **Company paid partner leave:** 2 weeks at full pay (Chapter 37).
- **Care leave:** short-term leave of up to 10 days for acute care situations, and longer care leave under the Care Leave and Family Care Leave Acts.

## Q16. Can you explain the rules on pay and benefits?

- Paid monthly in arrears; wage tax, solidarity surcharge where applicable, church tax where applicable, and social insurance contributions (pension, health, long-term care, unemployment) are deducted.
- Statutory minimum wage always met.
- Company pension (betriebliche Altersversorgung) with an employer subsidy of 20% on deferred compensation, or as agreed.
- Benefits such as a public transport ticket subsidy, job bike and meal allowance, as permitted under tax rules.

## Q17. How does remote and mobile work work at BragsLabs?

Mobile work and home office are provided under a works agreement or individual agreement covering working time recording, availability, equipment, costs, data protection and occupational safety. The employer's occupational health and safety duties continue for home-based work, including a risk assessment of the home workplace.

## Q18. Where can I find guidance on termination?

- Dismissals must be in writing with a wet-ink signature; email or electronic signature is not sufficient.
- **Statutory notice by employer:** 4 weeks to the 15th or end of a calendar month, rising with length of service to 7 months after 20 years.
- After 6 months' service, in establishments with more than 10 employees, a dismissal must be socially justified (for conduct, personal or operational reasons) under the Protection Against Dismissal Act. Conduct-related dismissals generally require a prior written warning (Abmahnung).
- Special protection applies to pregnant employees, employees on parental leave, severely disabled employees (approval of the Integration Office needed) and works council members.
- Mass dismissals require notification to the Federal Employment Agency and consultation with the works council.
- Post-employment non-competes are only valid if BragsLabs pays compensation of at least 50% of the last remuneration for the restricted period (maximum 2 years).

## Q19. What should I know about equal treatment and complaints?

Under the General Equal Treatment Act, BragsLabs has a complaints office (Beschwerdestelle) for discrimination and harassment complaints: Katrin Weber, HR Berlin (beschwerdestelle@bragslabs.com). Complaints are investigated and employees protected against victimisation. Whistleblowing reports may be made through the internal reporting channel under the Whistleblower Protection Act, which also allows reports to external government channels.
