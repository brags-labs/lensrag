# Employee FAQ: working at BragsLabs in United Arab Emirates

*For employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC. Last reviewed 18 August 2026. The United Arab Emirates addendum (Chapter 45 of manual version 3.0) prevails if this FAQ differs.*

## Q1. What is my paid annual leave?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC receive **22** — paid annual leave (working days). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q2. What is my paid sick leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC receive **20** — paid sick leave at full pay (working days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q3. What is my birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC receive **16** — birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q4. What is my partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC receive **2** — partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q5. What is my contractual notice period for L4 and above?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC receive **60** — contractual notice period for L4 and above (calendar days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q6. What is my standard working hours per week?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC receive **40** — standard working hours per week. This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q10. What does the policy say about key laws?

Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 on the Regulation of Labour Relations and its Executive Regulations (mainland and most free zones); DIFC Employment Law No. 2 of 2019 (if the entity is in the Dubai International Financial Centre); ADGM Employment Regulations (if in Abu Dhabi Global Market); Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection; unemployment insurance law; GPSSA pension law for UAE and GCC nationals; health insurance laws of each emirate; immigration and residence laws.

## Q11. How does contracts, visas and onboarding work at BragsLabs?

- Employment contracts are fixed-term and registered with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) or the free-zone authority, in Arabic and English. Where there is a conflict, the Arabic version prevails for MOHRE purposes.
- BragsLabs sponsors and pays for your work permit, residence visa, Emirates ID and medical tests. BragsLabs never holds an employee's passport.
- Recruitment costs are never charged to employees.
- Emiratisation targets apply to the entity as required by law; BragsLabs runs a UAE national talent programme.

## Q12. How does probation work at BragsLabs?

Probation is up to **6 months**. During probation, BragsLabs may end employment with 14 days' written notice; an employee who wishes to move to another UAE employer gives 1 month's notice (14 days if leaving the UAE), as set by law.

## Q13. How does working hours work at BragsLabs?

- Standard week of 40 hours, Monday to Friday (with a Friday finishing at 1:00 pm as the local office decides).
- Maximum 8 hours a day and 48 hours a week for overtime-eligible staff.
- **Ramadan:** working hours are reduced by **2 hours a day** for all employees.
- Overtime, where applicable, is paid at basic pay plus at least 25%, or plus 50% for work between 10:00 pm and 4:00 am; overtime is limited to 2 hours a day. Senior and supervisory roles may be excluded as permitted by law.
- At least one paid weekly rest day.

## Q15. How does pay and benefits work at BragsLabs?

- Salaries are paid monthly in AED through the **Wage Protection System (WPS)** on or before the due date.
- No personal income tax applies; no deductions are made except those permitted by law.
- **Health insurance:** mandatory medical insurance for employees (and dependants where BragsLabs sponsors them), as required by the emirate.
- **Unemployment insurance:** employees subscribe to the Involuntary Loss of Employment scheme as required by law; BragsLabs reminds employees and may pay the premium.
- **UAE and GCC nationals:** pension contributions to GPSSA (or the relevant emirate's fund) at the statutory rates.
- **DIFC employees:** employer contributions to the DIFC Employee Workplace Savings (DEWS) scheme instead of gratuity.
- Annual air ticket to the home country, housing and transport allowances, and education allowance for L5 and above, as set out in the offer letter.

## Q16. Can you explain the rules on end-of-service gratuity (mainland and most free zones)?

Employees with at least 1 year of continuous service receive end-of-service gratuity based on basic pay:

| Years of service | Gratuity |
| --- | --- |
| First 5 years | 21 days' basic pay for each year |
| Each year after 5 years | 30 days' basic pay for each year |
| Maximum | 2 years' total pay |

Partial years are paid pro rata. Final dues, including gratuity, are paid within **14 days** of the end of employment. Unpaid leave days are excluded from service. UAE nationals covered by GPSSA receive pension benefits instead.

## Q17. How does termination work at BragsLabs?

- Notice of **30 to 90 days**, as set in the contract (30 days for L1–L3, 60 days for L4+), applies to both sides.
- Termination must be for a legitimate reason under the Labour Law; summary dismissal without notice is permitted only for the grounds listed in the law, after a written investigation.
- Arbitrary dismissal, such as dismissal because an employee filed a genuine complaint, entitles the employee to compensation.
- Post-employment non-compete clauses are limited in time (no more than 2 years), place and type of work, as permitted by law.
- BragsLabs cancels the work permit and residence visa after exit and gives the employee the statutory grace period information.

## Q18. How does equality and harassment work at BragsLabs?

The Labour Law prohibits discrimination based on race, colour, sex, religion, national or social origin or disability, and prohibits sexual harassment, bullying and verbal, physical or psychological violence by employers, managers or colleagues. Women receive equal pay for work of equal value. Complaints go to the UAE Respect at Work Committee; you may also complain to MOHRE or the free-zone authority.
