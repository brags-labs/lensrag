# Employee FAQ: Travel and relocation

*Maintained by the HR Policy Office. Last reviewed 6 October 2026. If this FAQ and the Global HR Policy Manual differ, the manual and any later HR circular prevail.*

## Q1. Where can I find guidance on what counts as business travel?

- Travel to a client site, partner, conference or event outside your base city.
- Travel by remote employees to an office or meeting called by BragsLabs (Chapter 10).
- Travel by hybrid employees to an office other than their base office, at BragsLabs's request.
- Travel to training programmes nominated by BragsLabs.

Travel between home and the base office is commuting, not business travel. A remote employee who chooses to visit an office for personal convenience pays their own travel.

*Policy reference: Section 20.1.*

## Q2. Can you explain the rules on approval and booking?

1. Raise a travel request in the travel portal with purpose, dates, destination, project code and estimated cost.
2. Approvals: manager for domestic travel; manager and business unit head for international travel; client approval where the cost is billable.
3. Book through the travel desk at least **7 days** ahead for domestic and **21 days** ahead for international travel. Late bookings need justification.
4. Choose the lowest logical fare within 2 hours of the preferred time.
5. Register the trip in the travel safety system (automatic for travel desk bookings).

*Policy reference: Section 20.2.*

## Q3. What should I know about domestic travel entitlements?

| Grade | Air | Rail | Hotel per night (Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3 city) | Per diem (meals and incidentals) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1–L3 | Economy | AC 3-tier | ₹4,000 / ₹3,000 / ₹2,500 | ₹800 |
| L4–L6 | Economy | AC 2-tier | ₹6,500 / ₹5,000 / ₹4,000 | ₹1,200 |
| L7–L8 | Economy; premium economy over 3 hours | AC 1st class | ₹10,000 / ₹8,000 / ₹6,000 | ₹1,800 |
| L9 and above | Business | AC 1st class | Actuals at approved hotels | Actuals |

- Rail or bus may be used for distances under 500 km where practical.
- Staying with friends or relatives instead of a hotel earns a lodging allowance of 30% of the hotel limit.
- Local travel at the destination: app-based cabs or hotel transport; self-drive rentals with approval.

*Policy reference: Section 20.3.*

## Q4. What does the policy say about international travel entitlements?

| Item | Policy |
| --- | --- |
| Air | Economy; business class for flights over 8 hours for L7 and above |
| Hotel | As per the country rate card, in Company-approved hotels |
| Per diem | As per the country rate card, paid in local currency or through a forex card |
| Visa and insurance | Arranged and paid by BragsLabs |
| Forex | Forex card issued by the travel desk; settled within 15 days of return |
| Mobile | International roaming pack or local SIM reimbursed |

*Policy reference: Section 20.4.*

## Q5. What should I know about women and solo travellers?

- Women you may choose hotels from BragsLabs's verified safe-hotel list and request rooms on lower floors near lifts.
- Late-night arrivals are met by Company-arranged pre-paid cabs from the airport or station.
- Travellers to high-risk destinations receive a pre-travel security briefing.

*Policy reference: Section 20.5.*

## Q6. What does the policy say about travel time?

Travel during working hours counts as working time. Employees who travel overnight or arrive after midnight may start the next working day at noon. Travel on weekends or holidays at BragsLabs's request earns compensatory off (Chapter 12).

*Policy reference: Section 20.6.*

## Q7. How does long-term client-site postings (domestic) work at BragsLabs?

Where a client-site deployment in another city lasts more than **60 days**, the employee may choose between:

- **Option A:** serviced apartment or hotel paid by BragsLabs, plus 50% of per diem, and one trip home every 4 weeks; or
- **Option B:** a monthly deputation allowance of ₹60,000 covering stay and meals, plus one trip home every 4 weeks.

Postings over 6 months may be converted to a relocation with your consent.

*Policy reference: Section 20.7.*

## Q8. What does the policy say about relocation?

When BragsLabs asks an employee to move cities permanently (new base location, Company-initiated work-mode change, transfer), BragsLabs provides:

| Support | Policy |
| --- | --- |
| Travel | One-way airfare or train fare for employee and dependants |
| Household goods | Packing and moving through an approved vendor, up to ₹1,50,000 (L1–L3), ₹2,50,000 (L4–L6) or ₹4,00,000 (L7 and above), set by level |
| Temporary accommodation | Up to 15 days for the employee and family |
| Relocation allowance | One month's fixed salary, up to ₹3,00,000 |
| Home search and school support | Through a relocation partner |
| Leave | 2 days of special leave for the move |

Employees who resign within 12 months of a Company-paid relocation may be asked to repay a pro rata share of relocation costs. Employees who move at their own request (for example, a remote employee changing city) receive no relocation support.

*Policy reference: Section 20.8.*

## Q9. Where can I find guidance on emergency travel?

If an employee on travel or assignment faces a medical emergency, natural disaster, civil unrest or a family emergency at home, BragsLabs arranges and pays for emergency travel back home or to safety, and the 24x7 assistance provider coordinates support.

*Policy reference: Section 20.9.*

## Q10. Can you explain the rules on loyalty points and personal travel?

You may keep frequent-flyer and hotel loyalty points earned on business travel, provided they never choose a costlier option to earn points. Adding personal days to a business trip is allowed if it does not increase BragsLabs's cost, and the extra days are taken as leave and paid for by the employee.

*Policy reference: Section 20.10.*
