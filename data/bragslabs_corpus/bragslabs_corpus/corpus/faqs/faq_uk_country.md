# Employee FAQ: working at BragsLabs in United Kingdom

*For employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd. Last reviewed 18 August 2026. The United Kingdom addendum (Chapter 40 of manual version 3.0) prevails if this FAQ differs.*

## Q1. What is my paid annual leave?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd receive **25** — paid annual leave (working days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q2. What is my paid sick leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd receive **10** — paid sick leave at full pay (working days). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q3. What is my birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd receive **16** — birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q4. What is my partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd receive **2** — partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q5. What is my contractual notice period for L4 and above?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd receive **90** — contractual notice period for L4 and above (calendar days). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q6. What is my standard working hours per week?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd receive **37.5** — standard working hours per week. This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q7. What is my employer retirement contribution?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd receive **5** — employer retirement contribution (% of pay). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q8. What is my paid public holidays per year?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd receive **8** — paid public holidays per year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q9. What is my life cover?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd receive **4** — life cover (multiple of annual base salary). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q10. Where can I find guidance on key laws?

Employment Rights Act 1996 and Employment Rights Act 2025; Equality Act 2010 (including the duty to take reasonable steps to prevent sexual harassment); Working Time Regulations 1998; National Minimum Wage Act 1998; Maternity and Parental Leave Regulations; Shared Parental Leave Regulations; Pensions Act 2008 (auto-enrolment); UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018; Health and Safety at Work Act 1974; Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998; Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 (right-to-work checks).

## Q11. What does the policy say about contracts and right to work?

- Every employee receives a written statement of particulars on or before the first day of work.
- Right-to-work checks are completed before employment starts and repeated for time-limited permissions.
- Sponsored workers' employment follows Home Office sponsor duties.

## Q12. What should I know about working time?

- Standard week of 37.5 hours, Monday to Friday.
- The average working week must not exceed 48 hours unless the employee signs a voluntary opt-out, which they may cancel with notice.
- Daily rest of 11 consecutive hours, weekly rest of 24 hours, and a 20-minute break for shifts over 6 hours.
- Night workers receive free health assessments.

## Q13. What does the policy say about holidays (Company policy)?

**25 days' paid annual leave plus the 8 UK bank holidays** (pro rata for part-time), above the statutory 5.6 weeks. Up to 5 days may be carried over to the next holiday year. Holiday pay includes regular overtime, commission and allowances as required by law.

## Q14. What should I know about sickness?

- Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) is payable from the first day of sickness absence and without a lower earnings limit, from 6 April 2026.
- **Company sick pay:** 10 days at full pay in the first year of service, rising to 20 days after 2 years (inclusive of SSP).
- Self-certification for up to 7 calendar days; a fit note is needed after that.

## Q15. What does the policy say about family leave (Company policy)?

## Q16. Can you explain the rules on flexible working?

You may request flexible working from their first day of employment, up to two requests in 12 months. BragsLabs responds within 2 months, consults the employee, and refuses only for one of the statutory business reasons.

## Q17. Where can I find guidance on pay, pension and benefits?

- Paid monthly through PAYE, with income tax and National Insurance deducted.
- National Minimum Wage and National Living Wage always met; BragsLabs aims to pay at least the voluntary Real Living Wage.
- **Pension:** auto-enrolment into BragsLabs scheme, employer contribution of 5% of qualifying earnings, employee 3%, with salary sacrifice available.
- Private medical insurance, life assurance at 4× salary, income protection, EAP and cycle-to-work scheme.

## Q18. Can you explain the rules on notice and termination?

| Situation | Notice |
| --- | --- |
| Statutory minimum by employer | 1 week after 1 month's service, then 1 week per complete year, up to 12 weeks |
| Company contractual notice | 1 month during probation; 3 months after probation for L4+, 1 month for L1–L3 |
| By employee | As stated in the contract |

Dismissals follow a fair procedure in line with the Acas Code of Practice on disciplinary and grievance procedures. Redundancy follows consultation rules, with statutory redundancy pay for employees with 2 years' service and collective consultation where 20 or more redundancies are proposed. Protection from unfair dismissal is changing under the Employment Rights Act 2025; the UK HR lead confirms the current qualifying period before any dismissal.

## Q19. Where can I find guidance on harassment and grievances?

BragsLabs takes reasonable steps to prevent sexual harassment, including by third parties such as clients, through risk assessment, training and clear reporting routes. Complaints go to the UK Respect at Work Committee and are handled under the Acas Code. You may be accompanied by a colleague or trade union representative at formal grievance and disciplinary hearings. Whistleblowing is protected under the Public Interest Disclosure Act, including disclosures about sexual harassment.

## Q20. What should I know about restrictive covenants?

Post-employment restrictions are limited to what is reasonable to protect legitimate business interests, typically non-solicitation and non-dealing for 6 months, and non-competes only for senior roles where justified.
