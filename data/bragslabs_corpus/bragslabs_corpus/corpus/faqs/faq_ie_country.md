# Employee FAQ: working at BragsLabs in Ireland

*For employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd. Last reviewed 29 September 2026. The Ireland addendum (Chapter 43 of manual version 3.0) prevails if this FAQ differs.*

## Q1. What is my paid annual leave?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd receive **25** — paid annual leave (working days). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q2. What is my paid sick leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd receive **10** — paid sick leave at full pay (working days). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q3. What is my birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd receive **16** — birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q4. What is my partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd receive **2** — partner / non-birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q5. What is my contractual notice period for L4 and above?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd receive **90** — contractual notice period for L4 and above (calendar days). This is a Company entitlement and may be more generous than local law.

## Q6. What is my standard working hours per week?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd receive **39** — standard working hours per week. Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q7. What is my employer retirement contribution?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd receive **6** — employer retirement contribution (% of pay). Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q8. What is my paid public holidays per year?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd receive **10** — paid public holidays per year. Where local law gives more, local law applies.

## Q9. What is my life cover?

Under manual version 3.0, employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd receive **4** — life cover (multiple of annual base salary). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

## Q10. Where can I find guidance on key laws and codes?

Organisation of Working Time Act 1997; Terms of Employment (Information) Acts; Payment of Wages Act 1991; Sick Leave Act 2022; Maternity Protection Acts; Paternity Leave and Benefit Act 2016; Parental Leave Acts; Parent's Leave and Benefit Act 2019; Adoptive Leave Acts; Work Life Balance and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2023; Employment Equality Acts 1998–2015; Unfair Dismissals Acts; Minimum Notice and Terms of Employment Act 1973; Redundancy Payments Acts; Protected Disclosures Acts 2014–2022; Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005; EU GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018; Automatic Enrolment Retirement Savings System Act 2024. Codes of Practice on the Right to Disconnect, on requesting flexible and remote work, and on the prevention and resolution of bullying and sexual harassment.

## Q11. Where can I find guidance on terms and working time?

- Five core terms are given in writing within 5 days of starting, and the full statement within 1 month.
- Standard week of 39 hours. Average working week no more than 48 hours over a reference period; 11 hours' daily rest; 24 hours' weekly rest; a 15-minute break after 4.5 hours and 30 minutes after 6 hours.
- Working hours are recorded, including for remote employees.

## Q12. How does holidays (Company policy) work at BragsLabs?

**25 days' annual leave** a year (statutory minimum 4 working weeks), plus **10 public holidays**: New Year's Day, St Brigid's Day, St Patrick's Day, Easter Monday, the first Mondays of May, June and August, the last Monday of October, Christmas Day and St Stephen's Day.

## Q13. Can you explain the rules on sickness?

- Statutory sick leave is paid by BragsLabs at 70% of normal pay, subject to the daily cap, for the number of days set by the Minister (currently 5 days a year), for employees with 13 weeks' service and a medical certificate.
- **Company sick pay:** full pay for 10 days a year from the start of employment, inclusive of statutory sick pay, then Illness Benefit from the Department of Social Protection.

## Q15. How does flexible working, remote working and right to disconnect work at BragsLabs?

- All you may request remote working from their first day; BragsLabs decides within 4 weeks, considering the Code of Practice, and must give reasons for a refusal.
- Parents of children under 12 (or 16 if the child has a disability) and carers may request flexible working after 6 months' service.
- In line with the Code of Practice on the Right to Disconnect, employees are not expected to work routinely outside normal hours and will not be penalised for not responding (Chapter 12).

## Q16. How does pay, pension and benefits work at BragsLabs?

- Paid monthly; PAYE income tax, USC and PRSI deducted.
- National minimum wage always met.
- **Pension:** BragsLabs operates its own occupational pension or PRSA with an employer contribution of 6%. Employees not in a qualifying Company scheme are enrolled in the State automatic enrolment retirement savings system as required.
- Private health insurance (employee and family), life cover at 4× salary, income protection, EAP and cycle-to-work scheme.

## Q17. How does termination and redundancy work at BragsLabs?

| Service | Statutory minimum notice by employer |
| --- | --- |
| 13 weeks to 2 years | 1 week |
| 2 to 5 years | 2 weeks |
| 5 to 10 years | 4 weeks |
| 10 to 15 years | 6 weeks |
| 15 years or more | 8 weeks |

Contractual notice is usually longer: 1 month for L1–L3 and 3 months for L4+. Unfair dismissal protection generally applies after 12 months' service, and from day one for certain grounds such as pregnancy or making a protected disclosure. Dismissals follow fair procedures (S.I. 146/2000). Statutory redundancy pay is 2 weeks' pay per year of service plus a bonus week, subject to a weekly cap, for employees with 2 years' service.

## Q18. Can you explain the rules on equality, harassment and whistleblowing?

The Employment Equality Acts prohibit discrimination on 9 grounds: gender, civil status, family status, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, race and membership of the Traveller community. Complaints are handled under the Code of Practice on bullying and harassment through the Ireland Respect at Work Committee. Protected disclosures may be made internally or to prescribed persons and the Protected Disclosures Commissioner.
