# Grievance case register — Ireland, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IE-2025Q4-001 — Discrimination
**Filed:** 11 October 2025 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-IE-89787 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 27
**Themes:** gender, disability accommodation

**Summary.** The employee says their request for an ergonomic accommodation was ignored for 5 weeks. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
