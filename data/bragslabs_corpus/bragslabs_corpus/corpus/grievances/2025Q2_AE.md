# Grievance case register — United Arab Emirates, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AE-2025Q2-001 — Sexual harassment (referred to Internal Committee / Respect at Work Committee)
**Filed:** 04 April 2025 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-AE-62845 · **Handled at:** Respect at Work Committee · **Days to resolve:** 51
**Themes:** unwelcome comments, inappropriate messages

**Summary.** A complaint about inappropriate messages from a colleague was referred to the committee; the case summary is confidential. Two other members of the Client Delivery team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "Details are confidential and held by the committee."

**Investigation.** The committee followed the confidential inquiry procedure; only the outcome category is recorded here. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: pay correction processed. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-AE-2025Q2-002 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 05 April 2025 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-AE-83934 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** public criticism, micro-management

**Summary.** The employee reports that the manager asks for hourly status updates on chat, which they find excessive. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "I am open to a discussion with my manager."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: transport route reviewed with the vendor. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
