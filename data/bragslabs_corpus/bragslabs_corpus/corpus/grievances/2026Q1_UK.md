# Grievance case register — United Kingdom, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-UK-2026Q1-001 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 03 January 2026 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Sales
**Complainant:** BL-UK-26093 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 15
**Themes:** office-day mandate, caregiving conflict

**Summary.** The employee complains that the team's anchor days were changed without consultation and desks are not available on those days. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: rotation introduced for evening calls. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
