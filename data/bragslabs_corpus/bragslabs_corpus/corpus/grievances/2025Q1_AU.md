# Grievance case register — Australia, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AU-2025Q1-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 18 February 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Sales
**Complainant:** BL-AU-95624 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 17
**Themes:** incorrect increment, overtime not paid

**Summary.** The employee reports that overtime approved by the manager was not processed in payroll for 6 weeks. Two other members of the Sales team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: transport route reviewed with the vendor. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.

### Case GRV-AU-2025Q1-002 — Retaliation
**Filed:** 14 March 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-AU-20169 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 18
**Themes:** rating dropped after complaint, removed from project after complaint

**Summary.** The employee believes they were removed from a project after acting as a witness in an earlier inquiry. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I am open to a discussion with my manager."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: rating reviewed by skip-level manager. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-AU-2025Q1-003 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 29 March 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Sales
**Complainant:** BL-AU-11894 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 14
**Themes:** understaffing, weekend work

**Summary.** The employee states that the team has carried two vacant positions for 9 months and that they have regularly worked past 9 pm to meet client deadlines. The employee says the matter has continued for about 7 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 45 days.
