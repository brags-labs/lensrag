# Grievance case register — United Arab Emirates, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AE-2025Q4-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 21 October 2025 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Design
**Complainant:** BL-AE-75309 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 11
**Themes:** unrealistic deadlines, after-hours messages

**Summary.** The complaint describes repeated weekend deployments over the last quarter without compensatory off being approved. The employee says the matter has continued for about 4 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: accommodation granted. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
