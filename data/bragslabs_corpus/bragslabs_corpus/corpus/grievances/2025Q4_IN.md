# Grievance case register — India, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IN-2025Q4-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 01 October 2025 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Chennai · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-IN-66801 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** proximity bias, skipped promotion

**Summary.** The employee believes their promotion case was not presented at calibration although they met the criteria. The employee has been in the Client Delivery team for 4 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
