# Grievance case register — Australia, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AU-2024Q4-001 — Bullying and disrespect
**Filed:** 16 November 2024 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-AU-52922 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 24
**Themes:** hostile chat messages, exclusion from meetings

**Summary.** The employee reports being repeatedly left out of project meetings and chat channels by two colleagues. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-AU-2024Q4-002 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 25 November 2024 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L7 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** anonymous · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 16
**Themes:** understaffing, after-hours messages

**Summary.** The complaint describes repeated weekend deployments over the last quarter without compensatory off being approved. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. HR will review the situation again after 90 days.

### Case GRV-AU-2024Q4-003 — Leave refusal
**Filed:** 29 December 2024 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-AU-31463 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** school holiday clash, planned leave refused

**Summary.** The employee's planned leave during school holidays was refused at short notice citing a client release. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The parties agreed a practical solution at the first meeting.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: pay correction processed. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
