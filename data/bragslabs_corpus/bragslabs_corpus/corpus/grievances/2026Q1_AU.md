# Grievance case register — Australia, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AU-2026Q1-001 — Bullying and disrespect
**Filed:** 03 January 2026 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-AU-95455 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 9
**Themes:** hostile chat messages, exclusion from meetings

**Summary.** The employee describes demeaning remarks about their accent made by a senior colleague during stand-ups. The employee has been in the Quality Engineering team for 10 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: pay correction processed. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
