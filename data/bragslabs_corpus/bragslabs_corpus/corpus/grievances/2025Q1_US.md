# Grievance case register — United States, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Inc. between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-US-2025Q1-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 03 January 2025 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Austin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** BL-US-14180 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 15
**Themes:** understaffing, after-hours messages

**Summary.** The employee reports being asked to join calls with the German client every evening, outside agreed core hours, without a rotation. The employee has been in the Cloud Operations team for 10 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-US-2025Q1-002 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 06 January 2025 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-US-17379 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 14
**Themes:** commute cost, desk shortage
