# Grievance case register — Germany, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs GmbH between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-DE-2025Q2-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 02 April 2025 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Analytics Consulting
**Complainant:** BL-DE-36420 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** unrealistic deadlines, after-hours messages

**Summary.** The employee reports being asked to join calls with the US East Coast client every evening, outside agreed core hours, without a rotation. Two other members of the Analytics Consulting team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: pay correction processed. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-DE-2025Q2-003 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
