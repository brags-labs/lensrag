# Grievance case register — India, Q3 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. between 01 July 2026 and 30 September 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IN-2026Q3-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 02 July 2026 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Bengaluru · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L7 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-IN-36865 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 15
**Themes:** incorrect increment, missing allowance

**Summary.** The employee reports that overtime approved by the manager was not processed in payroll for 4 weeks. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I am open to a discussion with my manager."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: mediation session held. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 60 days.
