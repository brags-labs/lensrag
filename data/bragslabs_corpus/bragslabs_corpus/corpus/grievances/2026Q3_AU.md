# Grievance case register — Australia, Q3 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd between 01 July 2026 and 30 September 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AU-2026Q3-001 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 03 July 2026 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-AU-71299 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 19
**Themes:** desk shortage, caregiving conflict

**Summary.** The employee complains that the team's anchor days were changed without consultation and desks are not available on those days. Two other members of the Quality Engineering team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The parties agreed a practical solution at the first meeting.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: mediation session held. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
