# Grievance case register — Netherlands, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs B.V. between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-NL-2025Q2-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 05 April 2025 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-NL-18119 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 18
**Themes:** rating downgrade, skipped promotion

**Summary.** The employee, who works remotely, believes office-based colleagues receive more visible assignments and better ratings. The employee has been in the Product Management team for 12 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: manager coaching arranged. HR will review the situation again after 60 days.
