# Grievance case register — Netherlands, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs B.V. between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-NL-2026Q1-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 02 January 2026 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** BL-NL-52383 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 17
**Themes:** understaffing, weekend work

**Summary.** The employee states that the team has carried two vacant positions for 5 months and that they have regularly worked past 10 pm to meet client deadlines. Two other members of the Machine Learning team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The complaint was withdrawn before the hearing.

**Outcome.** Withdrawn. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
