# Grievance case register — Canada, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-CA-2025Q2-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 05 April 2025 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-CA-35634 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** after-hours messages, understaffing

**Summary.** The complaint describes repeated weekend deployments over the last quarter without compensatory off being approved. The employee says the matter has continued for about 3 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: workload rebalanced with an additional contractor. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 45 days.
