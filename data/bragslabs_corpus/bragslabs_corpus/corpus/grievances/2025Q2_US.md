# Grievance case register — United States, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Inc. between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-US-2025Q2-001 — Bullying and disrespect
**Filed:** 03 April 2025 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** anonymous · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 21
**Themes:** hostile chat messages, demeaning remarks

**Summary.** The employee reports being repeatedly left out of project meetings and chat channels by two colleagues. The employee says the matter has continued for about 12 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: facilities repair completed. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-US-2025Q2-002 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 05 April 2025 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** BL-US-59777 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 13
