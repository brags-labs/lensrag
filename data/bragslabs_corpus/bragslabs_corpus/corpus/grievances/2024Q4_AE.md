# Grievance case register — United Arab Emirates, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AE-2024Q4-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 15 October 2024 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** off-site · **Level:** L1 · **Department:** Design
**Complainant:** BL-AE-23064 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 34
**Themes:** incorrect increment, missing allowance

**Summary.** The employee disputes the increment applied in April, which was lower than the rating band in the compensation guide. The employee says the matter has continued for about 3 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I am open to a discussion with my manager."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The parties agreed a practical solution at the first meeting.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: team working agreement revised. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 60 days.

### Case GRV-AE-2024Q4-002 — Leave refusal
**Filed:** 11 November 2024 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Design
**Complainant:** BL-AE-27590 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 15
**Themes:** short-notice cancellation, planned leave refused

**Summary.** The employee says approved leave was cancelled two days before travel. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The employee withdrew the complaint after an informal discussion.

**Outcome.** Withdrawn. Action: no action required. HR will review the situation again after 90 days.

### Case GRV-AE-2024Q4-003 — Discrimination
**Filed:** 28 December 2024 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** off-site · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** BL-AE-94504 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** age, religion

**Summary.** The employee says their request for an ergonomic accommodation was ignored for 4 weeks. The employee says the matter has continued for about 9 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: work-mode exception approved for three months. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
