# Grievance case register — United States, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Inc. between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-US-2025Q4-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 04 October 2025 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Analytics Consulting
**Complainant:** BL-US-69760 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 28
**Themes:** delayed arrears, incorrect increment

**Summary.** The employee reports that overtime approved by the manager was not processed in payroll for 2 weeks. The employee has been in the Analytics Consulting team for 14 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: accommodation granted. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
