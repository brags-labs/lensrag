# Grievance case register — United Kingdom, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-UK-2025Q4-001 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 05 October 2025 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-UK-55771 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 10
**Themes:** office-day mandate, commute cost

**Summary.** The employee complains that the team's anchor days were changed without consultation and desks are not available on those days. The employee has been in the Quality Engineering team for 12 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 90 days.
