# Grievance case register — Germany, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs GmbH between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-DE-2025Q3-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 07 July 2025 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** People & HR
**Complainant:** BL-DE-20938 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 23
**Themes:** rating downgrade, skipped promotion

**Summary.** The employee disputes a rating downgrade at calibration that was not explained to them. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The employee withdrew the complaint after an informal discussion.

**Outcome.** Withdrawn. Action: no action required. HR will review the situation again after 45 days.

### Case GRV-DE-2025Q3-002 — Discrimination
**Filed:** 08 July 2025 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Design
**Complainant:** BL-DE-19099 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** disability accommodation, religion

**Summary.** The employee says their request for an ergonomic accommodation was ignored for 7 weeks. The employee has been in the Design team for 12 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: written warning issued to respondent. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
