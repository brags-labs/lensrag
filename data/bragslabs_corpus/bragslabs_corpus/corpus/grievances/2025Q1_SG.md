# Grievance case register — Singapore, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-SG-2025Q1-001 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 14 January 2025 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L1 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-SG-92459 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** caregiving conflict, desk shortage

**Summary.** The employee complains that the team's anchor days were changed without consultation and desks are not available on those days. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: facilities repair completed. HR will review the situation again after 60 days.

### Case GRV-SG-2025Q1-002 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 15 January 2025 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-SG-41089 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 9
