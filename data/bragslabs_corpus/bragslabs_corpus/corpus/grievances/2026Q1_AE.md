# Grievance case register — United Arab Emirates, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AE-2026Q1-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 29 January 2026 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** BL-AE-19000 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 4
**Themes:** overtime not paid, incorrect increment

**Summary.** The employee disputes the increment applied in April, which was lower than the rating band in the compensation guide. Two other members of the Machine Learning team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: accommodation granted. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
