# Grievance case register — Ireland, Q2 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd between 01 April 2026 and 30 June 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IE-2026Q2-001 — Bullying and disrespect
**Filed:** 05 April 2026 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L1 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-IE-54705 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** exclusion from meetings, hostile chat messages

**Summary.** The employee describes demeaning remarks about their accent made by a senior colleague during stand-ups. Two other members of the Client Delivery team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: pay correction processed. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-IE-2026Q2-002 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 05 May 2026 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Design
**Complainant:** BL-IE-27802 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 23
**Themes:** commute cost, office-day mandate

**Summary.** Following the move to three office days a week, the employee says the commute of 31 km each way makes the requirement unreasonable and asks for an exception. The employee has been in the Design team for 12 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
