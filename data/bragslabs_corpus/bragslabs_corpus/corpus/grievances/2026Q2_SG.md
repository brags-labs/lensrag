# Grievance case register — Singapore, Q2 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. between 01 April 2026 and 30 June 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-SG-2026Q2-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 04 April 2026 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-SG-96672 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 31
**Themes:** weekend work, unrealistic deadlines

**Summary.** The employee states that the team has carried two vacant positions for 5 months and that they have regularly worked past 9 pm to meet client deadlines. The employee says the matter has continued for about 2 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: transport route reviewed with the vendor. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
