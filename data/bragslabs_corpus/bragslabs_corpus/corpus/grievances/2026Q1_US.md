# Grievance case register — United States, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Inc. between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-US-2026Q1-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 04 January 2026 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** BL-US-82393 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** missing allowance, delayed arrears

**Summary.** The employee reports that overtime approved by the manager was not processed in payroll for 3 weeks. The employee says the matter has continued for about 2 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: transport route reviewed with the vendor. HR will review the situation again after 30 days.
