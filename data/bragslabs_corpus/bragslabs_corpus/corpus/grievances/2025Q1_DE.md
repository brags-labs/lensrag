# Grievance case register — Germany, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs GmbH between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-DE-2025Q1-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 17 January 2025 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L7 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-DE-22876 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** public criticism, favouritism

**Summary.** The employee feels that work allocation favours a small group close to the manager, leaving them with routine tasks only. The employee says the matter has continued for about 6 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-DE-2025Q1-002 — Manager behaviour
