# Grievance case register — United Kingdom, Q2 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd between 01 April 2026 and 30 June 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-UK-2026Q2-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 01 April 2026 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-UK-33925 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 9
**Themes:** weekend work, after-hours messages

**Summary.** The complaint describes repeated weekend deployments over the last quarter without compensatory off being approved. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "I want clarity on how the policy applies to me."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
