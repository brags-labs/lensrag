# Grievance case register — Ireland, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IE-2024Q4-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 06 October 2024 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-IE-30472 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 23
**Themes:** public criticism, lack of one-to-ones

**Summary.** The employee alleges that their manager criticises them in team meetings in front of the client and does not hold regular one-to-ones. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: rating reviewed by skip-level manager. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
