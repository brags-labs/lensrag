# Grievance case register — United States, Q2 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Inc. between 01 April 2026 and 30 June 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-US-2026Q2-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 03 April 2026 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Austin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-US-64208 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** public criticism, lack of one-to-ones

**Summary.** The employee feels that work allocation favours a small group close to the manager, leaving them with routine tasks only. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: manager coaching arranged. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-US-2026Q2-002 — Workload and working hours
