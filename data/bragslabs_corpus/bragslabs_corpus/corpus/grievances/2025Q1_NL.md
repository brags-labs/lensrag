# Grievance case register — Netherlands, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs B.V. between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-NL-2025Q1-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 03 January 2025 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-NL-51198 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** after-hours messages, unrealistic deadlines

**Summary.** The complaint describes repeated weekend deployments over the last quarter without compensatory off being approved. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I want clarity on how the policy applies to me."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: mediation session held. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 90 days.

### Case GRV-NL-2025Q1-002 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 12 January 2025 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Design
**Complainant:** BL-NL-70636 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 1
**Themes:** favouritism, micro-management

**Summary.** The employee alleges that their manager criticises them in team meetings in front of the client and does not hold regular one-to-ones. The employee has been in the Design team for 12 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. HR will review the situation again after 60 days.

### Case GRV-NL-2025Q1-004 — Pay and allowances

**Complainant:** BL-NL-74553 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 22
**Themes:** missing allowance, incorrect increment
