# Grievance case register — Australia, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AU-2025Q3-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 06 July 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-AU-35756 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 4
**Themes:** overtime not paid, delayed arrears

**Summary.** The employee disputes the increment applied in April, which was lower than the rating band in the compensation guide. Two other members of the Client Delivery team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: written warning issued to respondent. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-AU-2025Q3-002 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 16 July 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-AU-23694 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 13
**Themes:** lack of one-to-ones, favouritism

**Summary.** The employee alleges that their manager criticises them in team meetings in front of the client and does not hold regular one-to-ones. The employee has been in the Platform Engineering team for 4 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: team working agreement revised. HR will review the situation again after 30 days.
