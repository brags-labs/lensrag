# Grievance case register — Ireland, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IE-2026Q1-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 03 January 2026 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-IE-81106 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 3
**Themes:** delayed arrears, missing allowance

**Summary.** The employee disputes the increment applied in April, which was lower than the rating band in the compensation guide. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "I want clarity on how the policy applies to me."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
