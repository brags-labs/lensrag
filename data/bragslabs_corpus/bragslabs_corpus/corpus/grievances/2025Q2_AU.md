# Grievance case register — Australia, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AU-2025Q2-001 — Retaliation
**Filed:** 03 April 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-AU-46257 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 4
**Themes:** removed from project after complaint, rating dropped after complaint

**Summary.** The employee believes they were removed from a project after acting as a witness in an earlier inquiry. The employee has been in the Data Engineering team for 6 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-AU-2025Q2-002 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 14 April 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L1 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** BL-AU-90788 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** commute cost, caregiving conflict

**Summary.** The employee complains that the team's anchor days were changed without consultation and desks are not available on those days. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: manager coaching arranged. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
