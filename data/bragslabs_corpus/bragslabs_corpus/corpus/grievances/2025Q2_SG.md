# Grievance case register — Singapore, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-SG-2025Q2-001 — Sexual harassment (referred to Internal Committee / Respect at Work Committee)
**Filed:** 06 April 2025 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-SG-83013 · **Handled at:** Respect at Work Committee · **Days to resolve:** 62
**Themes:** unwelcome comments, inappropriate messages

**Summary.** A complaint involving unwelcome comments of a sexual nature was received and referred to the committee under the harassment policy; details are held by the committee. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "Details are confidential and held by the committee."

**Investigation.** The committee followed the confidential inquiry procedure; only the outcome category is recorded here. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: facilities repair completed. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.

### Case GRV-SG-2025Q2-002 — Manager behaviour
