# Grievance case register — Canada, Q2 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. between 01 April 2026 and 30 June 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-CA-2026Q2-001 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 02 April 2026 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** anonymous · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 15
**Themes:** caregiving conflict, commute cost

**Summary.** The employee, a primary caregiver, says the new office-day requirement conflicts with care arrangements and their accommodation request was declined. The employee says the matter has continued for about 4 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I am open to a discussion with my manager."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: manager coaching arranged. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
