# Grievance case register — United Arab Emirates, Q2 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC between 01 April 2026 and 30 June 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AE-2026Q2-001 — Discrimination
**Filed:** 05 April 2026 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** People & HR
**Complainant:** BL-AE-46280 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** religion, gender

**Summary.** The employee says their request for an ergonomic accommodation was ignored for 5 weeks. The employee has been in the People & HR team for 12 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. HR will review the situation again after 45 days.
