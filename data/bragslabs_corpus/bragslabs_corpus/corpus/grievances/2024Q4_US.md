# Grievance case register — United States, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Inc. between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-US-2024Q4-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 01 October 2024 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Analytics Consulting
**Complainant:** BL-US-72713 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 3
**Themes:** proximity bias, skipped promotion

**Summary.** The employee, who works remotely, believes office-based colleagues receive more visible assignments and better ratings. The employee has been in the Analytics Consulting team for 10 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I am open to a discussion with my manager."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: facilities repair completed. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.

### Case GRV-US-2024Q4-002 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 01 October 2024 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** BL-US-31481 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 12
**Themes:** after-hours messages, understaffing

**Summary.** The employee states that the team has carried two vacant positions for 4 months and that they have regularly worked past 10 pm to meet client deadlines. Two other members of the Cloud Operations team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.
