# Grievance case register — United Arab Emirates, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AE-2025Q3-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 06 July 2025 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Finance
**Complainant:** BL-AE-27008 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** after-hours messages, weekend work

**Summary.** The complaint describes repeated weekend deployments over the last quarter without compensatory off being approved. The employee says the matter has continued for about 8 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: rating reviewed by skip-level manager. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.

### Case GRV-AE-2025Q3-002 — Bullying and disrespect

**Complainant:** BL-AE-71779 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
