# Grievance case register — Germany, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs GmbH between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-DE-2026Q1-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 02 January 2026 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L7 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** BL-DE-29631 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** rating downgrade, calibration opacity

**Summary.** The employee disputes a rating downgrade at calibration that was not explained to them. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "I want clarity on how the policy applies to me."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. HR will review the situation again after 45 days.
