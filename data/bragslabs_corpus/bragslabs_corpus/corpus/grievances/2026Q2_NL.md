# Grievance case register — Netherlands, Q2 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs B.V. between 01 April 2026 and 30 June 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-NL-2026Q2-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 11 April 2026 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L1 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** BL-NL-34141 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 8
**Themes:** micro-management, lack of one-to-ones

**Summary.** The employee alleges that their manager criticises them in team meetings in front of the client and does not hold regular one-to-ones. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: no action required. HR will review the situation again after 30 days.
