# Grievance case register — Singapore, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-SG-2025Q4-001 — Leave refusal
**Filed:** 09 October 2025 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** BL-SG-83427 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** short-notice cancellation, school holiday clash

**Summary.** The employee says approved leave was cancelled two days before travel. The employee has been in the Cloud Operations team for 11 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: team working agreement revised. HR will review the situation again after 90 days.
