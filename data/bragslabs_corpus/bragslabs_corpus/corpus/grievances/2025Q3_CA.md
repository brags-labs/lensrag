# Grievance case register — Canada, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-CA-2025Q3-001 — Leave refusal
**Filed:** 03 July 2025 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Finance
**Complainant:** BL-CA-92466 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 18
**Themes:** school holiday clash, short-notice cancellation

**Summary.** The employee says approved leave was cancelled two days before travel. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: team working agreement revised. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-CA-2025Q3-002 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 10 July 2025 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-CA-42595 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 4
**Themes:** incorrect increment, missing allowance

**Summary.** The employee reports that overtime approved by the manager was not processed in payroll for 6 weeks. The employee has been in the Data Engineering team for 10 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I want clarity on how the policy applies to me."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: accommodation granted. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 45 days.

### Case GRV-CA-2025Q3-003 — Discrimination
