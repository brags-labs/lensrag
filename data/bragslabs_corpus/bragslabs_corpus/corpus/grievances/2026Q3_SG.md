# Grievance case register — Singapore, Q3 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. between 01 July 2026 and 30 September 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-SG-2026Q3-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 06 July 2026 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** anonymous · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 24
**Themes:** calibration opacity, skipped promotion

**Summary.** The employee believes their promotion case was not presented at calibration although they met the criteria. The employee says the matter has continued for about 6 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: workload rebalanced with an additional contractor. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
