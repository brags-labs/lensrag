# Grievance case register — India, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IN-2024Q4-001 — Retaliation
**Filed:** 01 October 2024 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Bengaluru · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-IN-88533 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 18
**Themes:** removed from project after complaint, rating dropped after complaint

**Summary.** The employee says their rating dropped after they raised a grievance last year. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "I am open to a discussion with my manager."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: transport route reviewed with the vendor. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
