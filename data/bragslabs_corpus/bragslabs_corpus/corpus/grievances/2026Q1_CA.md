# Grievance case register — Canada, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-CA-2026Q1-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 11 January 2026 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Design
**Complainant:** BL-CA-79776 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** weekend work, understaffing

**Summary.** The employee reports being asked to join calls with the US East Coast client every evening, outside agreed core hours, without a rotation. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
