# Grievance case register — United Kingdom, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-UK-2025Q2-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 06 April 2025 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Finance
**Complainant:** BL-UK-86067 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 1
**Themes:** rating downgrade, proximity bias

**Summary.** The employee disputes a rating downgrade at calibration that was not explained to them. Two other members of the Finance team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: work-mode exception approved for three months. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 30 days.

### Case GRV-UK-2025Q2-002 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 08 April 2025 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-UK-27724 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 15
**Themes:** public criticism, favouritism

**Summary.** The employee alleges that their manager criticises them in team meetings in front of the client and does not hold regular one-to-ones. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The employee withdrew the complaint after an informal discussion.

**Outcome.** Withdrawn. Action: no action required. HR will review the situation again after 30 days.

### Case GRV-UK-2025Q2-004 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 14 April 2025 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** BL-UK-50460 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 14
**Themes:** unrealistic deadlines, weekend work

**Summary.** The employee reports being asked to join calls with the US East Coast client every evening, outside agreed core hours, without a rotation. The employee has been in the Machine Learning team for 4 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I want clarity on how the policy applies to me."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: transport route reviewed with the vendor. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
