# Grievance case register — Canada, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-CA-2024Q4-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 09 October 2024 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-CA-12776 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 8
**Themes:** missing allowance, overtime not paid

**Summary.** The employee says their conveyance allowance for August was not paid despite approved timesheets. The employee has been in the Quality Engineering team for 5 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The employee withdrew the complaint after an informal discussion.

**Outcome.** Withdrawn. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-CA-2024Q4-002 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 15 October 2024 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-CA-64530 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 23
**Themes:** favouritism, public criticism

**Summary.** The employee feels that work allocation favours a small group close to the manager, leaving them with routine tasks only. Two other members of the Quality Engineering team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** The employee withdrew the complaint after an informal discussion.

**Outcome.** Withdrawn. Action: no action required. HR will review the situation again after 45 days.
