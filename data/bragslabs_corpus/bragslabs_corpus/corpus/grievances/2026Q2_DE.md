# Grievance case register — Germany, Q2 2026

**Filed:** 09 April 2026 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Design
**Complainant:** BL-DE-26681 · **Handled at:** Respect at Work Committee · **Days to resolve:** 57
**Themes:** unwelcome comments, inappropriate messages

**Summary.** A complaint about inappropriate messages from a colleague was referred to the committee; the case summary is confidential. The employee has been in the Design team for 4 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "Details are confidential and held by the committee."

**Investigation.** The committee followed the confidential inquiry procedure; only the outcome category is recorded here. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
