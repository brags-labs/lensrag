# Grievance case register — Singapore, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-SG-2026Q1-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 07 January 2026 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-SG-99208 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 13
**Themes:** skipped promotion, proximity bias

**Summary.** The employee disputes a rating downgrade at calibration that was not explained to them. The employee says the matter has continued for about 12 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I want clarity on how the policy applies to me."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: pay correction processed. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
