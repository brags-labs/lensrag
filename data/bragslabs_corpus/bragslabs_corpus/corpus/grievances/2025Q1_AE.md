# Grievance case register — United Arab Emirates, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AE-2025Q1-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 10 January 2025 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-AE-29416 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** weekend work, understaffing

**Summary.** The employee states that the team has carried two vacant positions for 3 months and that they have regularly worked past 11 pm to meet client deadlines. The employee has been in the Product Management team for 9 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: workload rebalanced with an additional contractor. HR will review the situation again after 60 days.

### Case GRV-AE-2025Q1-002 — Bullying and disrespect
**Filed:** 10 January 2025 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-AE-36634 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 8
**Themes:** demeaning remarks, hostile chat messages

**Summary.** The employee reports being repeatedly left out of project meetings and chat channels by two colleagues. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The complaint was withdrawn before the hearing.

**Outcome.** Withdrawn. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-AE-2025Q1-003 — Leave refusal
**Filed:** 29 March 2025 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Sales
**Complainant:** BL-AE-27693 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** school holiday clash, short-notice cancellation

**Summary.** The employee's planned leave during school holidays was refused at short notice citing a client release. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The employee withdrew the complaint after an informal discussion.

**Outcome.** Withdrawn. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
