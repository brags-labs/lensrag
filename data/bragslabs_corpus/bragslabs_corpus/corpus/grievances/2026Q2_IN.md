# Grievance case register — India, Q2 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. between 01 April 2026 and 30 June 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IN-2026Q2-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 03 April 2026 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Bengaluru · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Design
**Complainant:** BL-IN-23458 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 21
**Themes:** rating downgrade, calibration opacity

**Summary.** The employee disputes a rating downgrade at calibration that was not explained to them. The employee has been in the Design team for 13 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
