# Grievance case register — Ireland, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IE-2025Q2-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 03 April 2025 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Analytics Consulting
**Complainant:** BL-IE-82737 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** public criticism, favouritism

**Summary.** The employee reports that the manager asks for hourly status updates on chat, which they find excessive. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** The parties agreed a practical solution at the first meeting.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: transport route reviewed with the vendor. HR will review the situation again after 90 days.
