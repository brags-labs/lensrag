# Grievance case register — United States, Q3 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Inc. between 01 July 2026 and 30 September 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-US-2026Q3-001 — Office facilities
**Filed:** 01 July 2026 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-US-66405 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 8
**Themes:** cafeteria quality, parking

**Summary.** The employee raises concerns about noise in the open area near the collaboration zone and the lack of phone booths. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-US-2026Q3-002 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 04 July 2026 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L7 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-US-71273 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 27
**Themes:** skipped promotion, calibration opacity

**Summary.** The employee, who works remotely, believes office-based colleagues receive more visible assignments and better ratings. Two other members of the Platform Engineering team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I want clarity on how the policy applies to me."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: rotation introduced for evening calls. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
