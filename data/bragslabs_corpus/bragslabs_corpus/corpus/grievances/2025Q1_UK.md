# Grievance case register — United Kingdom, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-UK-2025Q1-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 02 January 2025 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** off-site · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** anonymous · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 8
**Themes:** unrealistic deadlines, understaffing

**Summary.** The complaint describes repeated weekend deployments over the last quarter without compensatory off being approved. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: rotation introduced for evening calls. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 30 days.
