# Grievance case register — India, Q1 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. between 01 January 2026 and 31 March 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IN-2026Q1-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 01 January 2026 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Hyderabad · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-IN-91686 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 11
**Themes:** missing allowance, overtime not paid

**Summary.** The employee says their conveyance allowance for August was not paid despite approved timesheets. Two other members of the Data Engineering team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I want clarity on how the policy applies to me."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-IN-2026Q1-002 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 01 January 2026 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Bengaluru · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Sales
