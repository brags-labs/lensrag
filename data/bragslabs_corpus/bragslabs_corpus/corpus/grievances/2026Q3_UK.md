# Grievance case register — United Kingdom, Q3 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd between 01 July 2026 and 30 September 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-UK-2026Q3-001 — Discrimination
**Filed:** 01 July 2026 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Finance
**Complainant:** BL-UK-25497 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** age, disability accommodation

**Summary.** The employee says their request for an ergonomic accommodation was ignored for 7 weeks. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: rotation introduced for evening calls. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
