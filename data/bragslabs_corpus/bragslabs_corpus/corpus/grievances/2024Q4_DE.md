# Grievance case register — Germany, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs GmbH between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-DE-2024Q4-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 01 October 2024 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L7 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** anonymous · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** public criticism, lack of one-to-ones

**Summary.** The employee alleges that their manager criticises them in team meetings in front of the client and does not hold regular one-to-ones. The employee has been in the Data Engineering team for 12 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: manager coaching arranged. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 60 days.
