# Grievance case register — Australia, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AU-2025Q4-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 07 October 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** off-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-AU-22519 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** weekend work, unrealistic deadlines

**Summary.** The employee reports being asked to join calls with the US East Coast client every evening, outside agreed core hours, without a rotation. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.

### Case GRV-AU-2025Q4-002 — Office facilities

**Complainant:** BL-AU-29408 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 9
