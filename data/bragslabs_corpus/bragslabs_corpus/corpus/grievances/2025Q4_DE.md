# Grievance case register — Germany, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs GmbH between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-DE-2025Q4-001 — Leave refusal
**Filed:** 14 October 2025 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-DE-87362 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 2
**Themes:** school holiday clash, planned leave refused

**Summary.** The employee's planned leave during school holidays was refused at short notice citing a client release. The employee had a rating of 3 in the last cycle and no prior grievances on record.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The complaint was withdrawn before the hearing.

**Outcome.** Withdrawn. Action: no action required. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-DE-2025Q4-002 — Office facilities
**Filed:** 16 October 2025 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-DE-10201 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** cafeteria quality, parking

**Summary.** The employee raises concerns about noise in the open area near the collaboration zone and the lack of phone booths. The employee says the matter has continued for about 5 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: workload rebalanced with an additional contractor. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 60 days.
