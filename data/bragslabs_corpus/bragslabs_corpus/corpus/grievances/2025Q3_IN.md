# Grievance case register — India, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IN-2025Q3-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 01 July 2025 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Hyderabad · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-IN-69073 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 16
**Themes:** lack of one-to-ones, micro-management

**Summary.** The employee alleges that their manager criticises them in team meetings in front of the client and does not hold regular one-to-ones. Two other members of the Product Management team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-IN-2025Q3-002 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 02 July 2025 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Pune · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L7 · **Department:** Sales
**Complainant:** BL-IN-83635 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 29
**Themes:** overtime not paid, missing allowance

**Summary.** The employee says their on-call allowance for January was not paid despite approved timesheets. The employee has been in the Sales team for 3 months and says the issue started after a change of project.
