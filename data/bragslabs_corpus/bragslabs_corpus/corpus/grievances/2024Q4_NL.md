# Grievance case register — Netherlands, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs B.V. between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-NL-2024Q4-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 05 October 2024 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-NL-59024 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 13
**Themes:** overtime not paid, delayed arrears

**Summary.** The employee reports that overtime approved by the manager was not processed in payroll for 5 weeks. The employee has been in the Data Engineering team for 13 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: rating reviewed by skip-level manager. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
