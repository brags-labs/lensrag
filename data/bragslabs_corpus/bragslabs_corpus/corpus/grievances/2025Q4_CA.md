# Grievance case register — Canada, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-CA-2025Q4-001 — Discrimination
**Filed:** 04 October 2025 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L7 · **Department:** Analytics Consulting
**Complainant:** BL-CA-58945 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** age, disability accommodation

**Summary.** The employee says their request for an ergonomic accommodation was ignored for 8 weeks. The employee has been in the Analytics Consulting team for 4 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. Both parties were given the chance to respond in writing within 3 working days.

**Findings.** The parties agreed a practical solution at the first meeting.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: pay correction processed. HR will review the situation again after 45 days.

### Case GRV-CA-2025Q4-002 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 10 October 2025 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** People & HR
**Complainant:** BL-CA-31727 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 38
**Themes:** caregiving conflict, desk shortage

**Summary.** The employee, a primary caregiver, says the new office-day requirement conflicts with care arrangements and their accommodation request was declined. Two other members of the People & HR team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
