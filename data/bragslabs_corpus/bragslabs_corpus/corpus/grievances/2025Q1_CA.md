# Grievance case register — Canada, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-CA-2025Q1-001 — Bullying and disrespect
**Filed:** 01 January 2025 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** anonymous · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 4
**Themes:** exclusion from meetings, demeaning remarks

**Summary.** The employee describes demeaning remarks about their accent made by a senior colleague during stand-ups. Two other members of the Cloud Operations team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: workload rebalanced with an additional contractor. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-CA-2025Q1-002 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 07 January 2025 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** BL-CA-56739 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 18
**Themes:** proximity bias, skipped promotion

**Summary.** The employee, who works remotely, believes office-based colleagues receive more visible assignments and better ratings. Two other members of the Machine Learning team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: workload rebalanced with an additional contractor. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
