# Grievance case register — Ireland, Q3 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd between 01 July 2026 and 30 September 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IE-2026Q3-001 — Retaliation
**Filed:** 04 July 2026 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-IE-22529 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 9
**Themes:** removed from project after complaint, rating dropped after complaint

**Summary.** The employee says their rating dropped after they raised a grievance last year. The employee has been in the Product Management team for 3 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
