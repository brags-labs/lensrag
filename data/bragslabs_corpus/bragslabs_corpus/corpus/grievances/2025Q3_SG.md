# Grievance case register — Singapore, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-SG-2025Q3-001 — Bullying and disrespect
**Filed:** 03 July 2025 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** BL-SG-74263 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 20
**Themes:** hostile chat messages, demeaning remarks

**Summary.** The employee describes demeaning remarks about their accent made by a senior colleague during stand-ups. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: manager coaching arranged. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.
