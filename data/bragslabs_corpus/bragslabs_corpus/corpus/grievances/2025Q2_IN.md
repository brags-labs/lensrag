# Grievance case register — India, Q2 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. between 01 April 2025 and 30 June 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IN-2025Q2-001 — Office facilities
**Filed:** 01 April 2025 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Bengaluru · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-IN-56314 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** cafeteria quality, parking

**Summary.** The employee raises concerns about noise in the open area near the collaboration zone and the lack of phone booths. The employee says the matter has continued for about 4 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.

### Case GRV-IN-2025Q2-002 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 02 April 2025 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Bengaluru · **Work mode:** off-site · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-IN-40946 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 4
**Themes:** incorrect increment, overtime not paid

### Case GRV-IN-2025Q2-003 — Transport and safety
