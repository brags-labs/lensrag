# Grievance case register — Netherlands, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs B.V. between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-NL-2025Q3-001 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 09 July 2025 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** off-site · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-NL-69237 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 12
**Themes:** incorrect increment, delayed arrears

**Summary.** The employee reports that overtime approved by the manager was not processed in payroll for 2 weeks. Two other members of the Client Delivery team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
