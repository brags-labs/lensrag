# Grievance case register — United Arab Emirates, Q3 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC between 01 July 2026 and 30 September 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AE-2026Q3-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 01 July 2026 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** BL-AE-45958 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 14
**Themes:** public criticism, favouritism

**Summary.** The employee feels that work allocation favours a small group close to the manager, leaving them with routine tasks only. Two other members of the Machine Learning team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The evidence did not support the complaint; the decision was within policy and properly documented.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
