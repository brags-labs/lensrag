# Grievance case register — Ireland, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IE-2025Q3-001 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 03 July 2025 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** Finance
**Complainant:** anonymous · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 30
**Themes:** office-day mandate, desk shortage

**Summary.** The employee, a primary caregiver, says the new office-day requirement conflicts with care arrangements and their accommodation request was declined. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: rating reviewed by skip-level manager. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
