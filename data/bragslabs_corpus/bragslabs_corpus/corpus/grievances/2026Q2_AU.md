# Grievance case register — Australia, Q2 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd between 01 April 2026 and 30 June 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-AU-2026Q2-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 04 April 2026 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** BL-AU-72985 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** calibration opacity, skipped promotion

**Summary.** The employee, who works remotely, believes office-based colleagues receive more visible assignments and better ratings. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: team working agreement revised. HR will review the situation again after 45 days.

### Case GRV-AU-2026Q2-002 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 07 April 2026 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** off-site · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Analytics Consulting
**Complainant:** BL-AU-33720 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 5
**Themes:** desk shortage, office-day mandate

**Summary.** The employee, a primary caregiver, says the new office-day requirement conflicts with care arrangements and their accommodation request was declined. The employee has been in the Analytics Consulting team for 11 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The parties agreed a practical solution at the first meeting.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: manager coaching arranged. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
