# Grievance case register — Netherlands, Q4 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs B.V. between 01 October 2025 and 31 December 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-NL-2025Q4-001 — Retaliation
**Filed:** 03 October 2025 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Product Management
**Complainant:** BL-NL-67653 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 16
**Themes:** removed from project after complaint, rating dropped after complaint

**Summary.** The employee believes they were removed from a project after acting as a witness in an earlier inquiry. The employee says the matter has continued for about 2 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: pay correction processed. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 30 days.

### Case GRV-NL-2025Q4-002 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 03 October 2025 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Finance
**Complainant:** BL-NL-92474 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 15
**Themes:** overtime not paid, missing allowance

**Summary.** The employee disputes the increment applied in April, which was lower than the rating band in the compensation guide. The issue arose during a critical client release when the team was working extended hours.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: transport route reviewed with the vendor. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
