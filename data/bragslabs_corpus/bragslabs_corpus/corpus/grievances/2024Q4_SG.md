# Grievance case register — Singapore, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-SG-2024Q4-001 — Promotion and rating fairness
**Filed:** 05 October 2024 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** Finance
**Complainant:** BL-SG-58360 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 8
**Themes:** calibration opacity, proximity bias

**Summary.** The employee disputes a rating downgrade at calibration that was not explained to them. The employee has been in the Finance team for 11 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I am open to a discussion with my manager."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: pay correction processed. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 30 days.

### Case GRV-SG-2024Q4-002 — Pay and allowances
**Filed:** 30 October 2024 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** Platform Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-SG-56790 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 16
**Themes:** delayed arrears, incorrect increment

**Summary.** The employee says their home office allowance for March was not paid despite approved timesheets. The employee says the matter has continued for about 14 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** The parties agreed a practical solution at the first meeting.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: written warning issued to respondent. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.

### Case GRV-SG-2024Q4-003 — Office facilities
**Filed:** 09 November 2024 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-SG-22688 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 15
**Themes:** air conditioning, parking

**Summary.** The employee raises concerns about noise in the open area near the collaboration zone and the lack of phone booths. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

### Case GRV-SG-2024Q4-004 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 12 November 2024 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Machine Learning
**Complainant:** BL-SG-36639 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 18
