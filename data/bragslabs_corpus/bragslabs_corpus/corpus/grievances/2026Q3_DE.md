# Grievance case register — Germany, Q3 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs GmbH between 01 July 2026 and 30 September 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-DE-2026Q3-001 — Bullying and disrespect
**Filed:** 04 July 2026 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L5 · **Department:** Client Delivery
**Complainant:** BL-DE-60204 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 22
**Themes:** exclusion from meetings, hostile chat messages

**Summary.** The employee describes demeaning remarks about their accent made by a senior colleague during stand-ups. The employee says the matter has continued for about 7 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The complaint was substantiated on the main points.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: rotation introduced for evening calls. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
