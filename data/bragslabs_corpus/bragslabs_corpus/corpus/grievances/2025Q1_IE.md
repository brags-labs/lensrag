# Grievance case register — Ireland, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IE-2025Q1-001 — Transport and safety
**Filed:** 05 January 2025 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Finance
**Complainant:** BL-IE-19998 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** driver conduct, unsafe drop point

**Summary.** Since the new transport vendor started, the employee says route changes have doubled the commute time. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I would like a fair review of the facts."

**Investigation.** The committee reviewed documents, interviewed two witnesses and heard both parties. The skip-level manager was consulted before the decision.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: transport route reviewed with the vendor. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.

### Case GRV-IE-2025Q1-002 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 07 January 2025 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** People & HR
**Complainant:** BL-IE-15553 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 20
**Themes:** unrealistic deadlines, weekend work

**Summary.** The employee states that the team has carried two vacant positions for 8 months and that they have regularly worked past 11 pm to meet client deadlines. The employee has been in the People & HR team for 7 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The concern about process was valid, but the decision itself was within policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: mediation session held. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
