# Grievance case register — United States, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs Inc. between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-US-2025Q3-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 02 July 2025 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Austin · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L2 · **Department:** People & HR
**Complainant:** BL-US-98944 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** lack of one-to-ones, public criticism

**Summary.** The employee feels that work allocation favours a small group close to the manager, leaving them with routine tasks only. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** HR reviewed payroll records and the approved timesheets for the period. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** No breach of policy was found, although communication could have been clearer.

**Outcome.** Not upheld. Action: no action required. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.

### Case GRV-US-2025Q3-002 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 02 July 2025 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-US-25439 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 29
**Themes:** after-hours messages, weekend work

**Summary.** The employee states that the team has carried two vacant positions for 6 months and that they have regularly worked past 9 pm to meet client deadlines. The employee says the matter has continued for about 14 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: accommodation granted. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 30 days.
