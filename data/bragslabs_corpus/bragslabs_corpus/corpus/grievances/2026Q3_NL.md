# Grievance case register — Netherlands, Q3 2026

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs B.V. between 01 July 2026 and 30 September 2026, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-NL-2026Q3-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 11 July 2026 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L6 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-NL-49557 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 19
**Themes:** after-hours messages, unrealistic deadlines

**Summary.** The employee reports being asked to join calls with the US East Coast client every evening, outside agreed core hours, without a rotation. The employee asked for the matter to be handled confidentially and without involving the client.

**Employee statement.** "I am considering leaving if this continues."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: manager coaching arranged. The employee was told how to escalate if the issue recurs.

### Case GRV-NL-2026Q3-002 — Hybrid and work-mode decisions
**Filed:** 14 July 2026 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Data Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-NL-67961 · **Handled at:** Stage 3 (Grievance Redressal Committee) · **Days to resolve:** 26
**Themes:** desk shortage, commute cost

**Summary.** Following the move to three office days a week, the employee says the commute of 31 km each way makes the requirement unreasonable and asks for an exception. Two other members of the Data Engineering team have raised similar concerns informally, according to the HRBP.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The hearing was held by secure video because the employee works remotely.

**Findings.** Part of the complaint was supported by evidence; the rest reflected a misunderstanding of the policy.

**Outcome.** Partially upheld. Action: team working agreement revised. The employee was informed of the right to appeal to the CHRO within 7 working days.
