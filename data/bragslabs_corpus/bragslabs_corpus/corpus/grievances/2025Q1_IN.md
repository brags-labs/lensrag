# Grievance case register — India, Q1 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. between 01 January 2025 and 31 March 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-IN-2025Q1-001 — Workload and working hours
**Filed:** 01 January 2025 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Chennai · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L4 · **Department:** Sales
**Complainant:** BL-IN-90235 · **Handled at:** Stage 2 (HRBP) · **Days to resolve:** 13
**Themes:** unrealistic deadlines, weekend work

**Summary.** The complaint describes repeated weekend deployments over the last quarter without compensatory off being approved. The employee says the matter has continued for about 2 weeks and was first raised informally with the manager.

**Employee statement.** "It feels like my concerns do not matter."

**Investigation.** The HRBP met the employee and the manager separately and reviewed chat records and timesheets. HR checked the relevant policy chapter and the decision records in the HRMS.

**Findings.** The evidence supports the employee's account and the policy was not applied correctly.

**Outcome.** Upheld. Action: workload rebalanced with an additional contractor. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.

### Case GRV-IN-2025Q1-002 — Promotion and rating fairness
