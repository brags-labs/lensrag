# Grievance case register — United Kingdom, Q3 2025

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd between 01 July 2025 and 30 September 2025, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-UK-2025Q3-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 02 July 2025 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** remote · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Cloud Operations
**Complainant:** BL-UK-99952 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 6
**Themes:** micro-management, public criticism

**Summary.** The employee feels that work allocation favours a small group close to the manager, leaving them with routine tasks only. The employee has been in the Cloud Operations team for 3 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "This has affected my health and motivation."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. A support colleague attended the hearing with the employee.

**Findings.** The parties agreed a practical solution at the first meeting.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: accommodation granted. A follow-up check-in is scheduled in 45 days.
