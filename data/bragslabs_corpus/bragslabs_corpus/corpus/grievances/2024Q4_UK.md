# Grievance case register — United Kingdom, Q4 2024

Confidential register of grievances filed by employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd between 01 October 2024 and 31 December 2024, prepared by HR for the Grievance Redressal Committee. Harassment complaints are recorded only by outcome category; details are held by the relevant committee.

### Case GRV-UK-2024Q4-001 — Manager behaviour
**Filed:** 22 October 2024 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** on-site · **Level:** L3 · **Department:** Quality Engineering
**Complainant:** BL-UK-44149 · **Handled at:** Stage 1 (manager) · **Days to resolve:** 7
**Themes:** favouritism, public criticism

**Summary.** The employee feels that work allocation favours a small group close to the manager, leaving them with routine tasks only. The employee has been in the Quality Engineering team for 8 months and says the issue started after a change of project.

**Employee statement.** "I have raised this informally twice and nothing changed."

**Investigation.** The manager met the employee within 7 working days and documented the discussion. The respondent manager was interviewed and accepted part of the account.

**Findings.** The manager and employee agreed changes without a formal hearing.

**Outcome.** Resolved informally. Action: written warning issued to respondent. The case was closed and the employee confirmed they were satisfied with the outcome.
