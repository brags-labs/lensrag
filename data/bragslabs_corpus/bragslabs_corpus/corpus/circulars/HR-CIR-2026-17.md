## 1. Summary
This circular informs employees of all BragsLabs entities worldwide about: Launch of Global HR Policy Manual v3.0. It takes effect on 1 November 2026.

## 2. Background
The change follows the annual benchmarking of our benefits against the global market and feedback from the 2025 employee survey. The change also reflects updates in local law and in the guidance issued by the relevant authorities.

Version 3.0 covers 10 countries: United States, India, United Kingdom, Germany, Netherlands, Ireland, Singapore, United Arab Emirates, Canada, Australia. The main changes are:
- Hybrid office days: 2 office days a week → 3 office days a week
- Work from abroad days: 10 working days a year → 20 working days a year
- Remote setup allowance: ₹20,000 → ₹25,000
- Hybrid setup allowance: ₹8,000 → ₹10,000
- Remote internet allowance: ₹1,200 → ₹1,500
- Hybrid internet allowance: ₹800 → ₹1,000
- Night shift allowance: ₹500 per night → ₹600 per night
- Late shift allowance: ₹300 per night → ₹400 per night
- Afternoon shift allowance: ₹200 per night → ₹250 per night
- Learning budget l1 l3: ₹20,000 → ₹25,000
- Learning budget l4 l6: ₹30,000 → ₹40,000
- Learning budget l7: ₹60,000 → ₹75,000
- Wellbeing allowance: ₹8,000 → ₹12,000
- Health sum insured: ₹4,00,000 → ₹5,00,000
- Remote cap pct: 15% → 20%
- India paternity working days: 7 working days → 10 working days
- Referral bonus min: ₹20,000 → ₹25,000
- Referral bonus max: ₹50,000 → ₹60,000
- Coworking budget: ₹20,000 → ₹30,000
- Global min annual leave days: 15 days → 18 days
- Global min sick days: 8 days → 10 days
- Global min birth parent weeks: 14 weeks → 16 weeks
- New country addendum: United Arab Emirates (BragsLabs FZ-LLC)
- New country addendum: Australia (BragsLabs Pty Ltd)
- United States: paid annual leave (working days) 18 → 20
- Germany: paid annual leave (working days) 27 → 28
- Ireland: paid annual leave (working days) 23 → 25
- Singapore: paid annual leave (working days) 16 → 18
- United States: paid sick leave at full pay (working days) 8 → 10
- Ireland: paid sick leave at full pay (working days) 5 → 10
- Canada: paid sick leave at full pay (working days) 7 → 10
- United States: birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks) 14 → 16
- United Kingdom: birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks) 14 → 16
- Germany: birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks) 14 → 16
- Ireland: birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks) 14 → 16
- Canada: birth-parent leave at full pay (weeks) 14 → 16

## 5. Transition rules

- Managers should not change approved plans because of this circular without discussing them with the employee and the HRBP.
- Requests already approved before the effective date are honoured on their original terms.
- Any arrears arising from this change are paid in the first payroll run after the effective date.

## 6. What you need to do

**Employees**

- Update your plans with your manager where the change affects you.
- Raise any question through the HR helpdesk, quoting the circular number.
- Check your balances or allowances in the HRMS after the effective date.

**Managers**

- Consult your HRBP before declining a request made under this circular.
- Apply the new rule consistently and record any exception in the HRMS.
- Brief your team within two weeks of this circular.

**HR and Payroll**

- The policy team incorporates the change into the next version of the Global HR Policy Manual.
- HRBPs hold drop-in sessions for questions.
- Country HR leads report adoption and issues in the monthly HR review.

## Impact on related policies

- Related allowances, balances and approvals in the HRMS are updated automatically; no new form is needed.
- Country addenda that state a more favourable local rule continue to apply.
- The change does not alter notice periods, probation terms or performance ratings.

## Communication plan

- HRBPs will hold two 30-minute drop-in sessions in each region.
- An intranet article and a short video explainer will be published within a week.
- The HR helpdesk has been briefed and will tag related tickets with this circular number.

## Monitoring and review

- HR will review adoption, helpdesk volumes and feedback after three months.
- Any unintended effect on particular groups (for example caregivers or employees with disabilities) will be reported to the CHRO.
- Results will be shared in the next Pulse survey read-out.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Does this change apply to fixed-term employees and probationers?**

Yes. It applies to all employees of the entities listed above, pro rata for fixed-term and part-time employees, unless the circular says otherwise.

**What if my appointment letter gives me a better benefit?**

Your appointment letter prevails where it is more favourable (Chapter 1.4).

**Can my manager refuse to apply this change?**

No. Managers apply Company policy consistently; exceptions need HR approval and are recorded in the HRMS.

**Where can I find the updated policy text?**

On the HR intranet and in the HRMS policy library. The next version of the manual will include this change.

**Will this affect my performance rating?**

No. Using an entitlement under Company policy never affects performance ratings.

**Who do I contact with questions?**

Your HRBP, or the HR helpdesk at hr@bragslabs.com, quoting the circular number.

## 8. Relationship to the Global HR Policy Manual

This circular amends manual version 2.0 from the effective date and will be incorporated into version 3.0. Where this circular and the manual differ, this circular prevails from its effective date.
