# HR-CIR-2024-02: Wellbeing allowance raised to ₹10,000

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Circular number | HR-CIR-2024-02 |
| Issued | 4 March 2024 |
| Effective from | 1 April 2024 |
| Applies to | BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. |
| Issued by | Rohit Sharma, HR Lead, India |
| Status | In force from the effective date |
| Policy reference | Chapter 19.7 |

## 1. Summary
This circular changes the annual wellbeing allowance for employees in India. From 1 April 2024, it changes from **₹8,000** to **₹10,000**.

## 2. Background
The change follows the annual benchmarking of our benefits against the India market and feedback from the 2023 employee survey. Leadership reviewed the cost and impact of this change with Finance and the country HR leads before approving it.

## 3. What changes

| Item | Before | From 1 April 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| India — Wellbeing allowance | ₹8,000 | ₹10,000 |

## 4. Worked examples
- An eligible employee receives ₹10,000 instead of ₹8,000 for claims or payments falling on or after the effective date.
- Claims or shifts before 1 April 2024 are paid at ₹8,000.

## 5. Transition rules

- Managers should not change approved plans because of this circular without discussing them with the employee and the HRBP.
- Employees on notice, on long leave or on international assignment are covered from the effective date in the same way as other employees.
- Where an entitlement accrues monthly, the new rate applies from the first full month after the effective date and is pro-rated for the year.

## 6. What you need to do

**Employees**

- Read this circular and the updated policy chapter on the intranet.
- Check your balances or allowances in the HRMS after the effective date.
- Update your plans with your manager where the change affects you.

**Managers**

- Apply the new rule consistently and record any exception in the HRMS.
- Consult your HRBP before declining a request made under this circular.
- Brief your team within two weeks of this circular.

**HR and Payroll**

- Payroll and HR Operations configure the HRMS before the effective date.
- The policy team incorporates the change into the next version of the Global HR Policy Manual.
- HRBPs hold drop-in sessions for questions.

## Impact on related policies

- The change does not alter notice periods, probation terms or performance ratings.
- Related allowances, balances and approvals in the HRMS are updated automatically; no new form is needed.
- Payroll will show the change as a separate line on the first payslip after the effective date.

## Communication plan

- Country HR leads will brief people managers in the next monthly managers' call.
- The HR helpdesk has been briefed and will tag related tickets with this circular number.
- HRBPs will hold two 30-minute drop-in sessions in each region.

## Monitoring and review

- Any unintended effect on particular groups (for example caregivers or employees with disabilities) will be reported to the CHRO.
- Results will be shared in the next Pulse survey read-out.
- HR will review adoption, helpdesk volumes and feedback after three months.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Does this change apply to fixed-term employees and probationers?**

Yes. It applies to all employees of the entities listed above, pro rata for fixed-term and part-time employees, unless the circular says otherwise.

**What if my appointment letter gives me a better benefit?**

Your appointment letter prevails where it is more favourable (Chapter 1.4).

**Will this affect my performance rating?**

No. Using an entitlement under Company policy never affects performance ratings.

**Who do I contact with questions?**

Your HRBP, or the HR helpdesk at hr@bragslabs.com, quoting the circular number.

**Where can I find the updated policy text?**

On the HR intranet and in the HRMS policy library. The next version of the manual will include this change.

**Can my manager refuse to apply this change?**

No. Managers apply Company policy consistently; exceptions need HR approval and are recorded in the HRMS.

## 8. Relationship to the Global HR Policy Manual

This circular amends manual version 2.0 from the effective date and will be incorporated into version 3.0. Where this circular and the manual differ, this circular prevails from its effective date.
