## 1. Summary
This circular informs employees of all BragsLabs entities worldwide about: Public holiday calendars for 2026. It takes effect on 1 January 2026.

## 2. Background
Several employees raised this topic through the HR helpdesk and the Pulse survey; this circular responds to that feedback. The change follows the annual benchmarking of our benefits against the local market and feedback from the 2024 employee survey.

The 2026 public holiday calendars are below. Remote employees follow the calendar of their base location.
- **United States** (11 days): New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, Floating holiday
- **India** (10 days): Republic Day, Holi, Ugadi / Gudi Padwa, May Day, Independence Day, Ganesh Chaturthi, Gandhi Jayanti, Dussehra, Diwali, Christmas
- **United Kingdom** (8 days): New Year's Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Early May bank holiday, Spring bank holiday, Summer bank holiday, Christmas Day, Boxing Day
- **Germany** (10 days): Neujahr, Internationaler Frauentag (Berlin), Karfreitag, Ostermontag, Tag der Arbeit, Christi Himmelfahrt, Pfingstmontag, Tag der Deutschen Einheit, 1. Weihnachtstag, 2. Weihnachtstag
- **Netherlands** (8 days): New Year's Day, Easter Monday, King's Day, Liberation Day, Ascension Day, Whit Monday, Christmas Day, Boxing Day
- **Ireland** (10 days): New Year's Day, St Brigid's Day, St Patrick's Day, Easter Monday, May bank holiday, June bank holiday, August bank holiday, October bank holiday, Christmas Day, St Stephen's Day
- **Singapore** (10 days): New Year's Day, Chinese New Year (2 days), Hari Raya Puasa, Good Friday, Labour Day, Vesak Day, Hari Raya Haji, National Day, Deepavali, Christmas Day
- **United Arab Emirates** (8 days): New Year's Day, Eid al-Fitr (3 days), Arafat Day, Eid al-Adha (3 days), Islamic New Year, Prophet's Birthday, Commemoration Day, National Day (2 days)
- **Canada** (11 days): New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Civic Holiday, Labour Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, Boxing Day
- **Australia** (10 days): New Year's Day, Australia Day, Good Friday, Easter Saturday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, King's Birthday, Labour Day (NSW), Christmas Day, Boxing Day

## 5. Transition rules

- Requests already approved before the effective date are honoured on their original terms.
- Employees on notice, on long leave or on international assignment are covered from the effective date in the same way as other employees.
- Any arrears arising from this change are paid in the first payroll run after the effective date.

## 6. What you need to do

**Employees**

- Raise any question through the HR helpdesk, quoting the circular number.
- Read this circular and the updated policy chapter on the intranet.
- Check your balances or allowances in the HRMS after the effective date.

**Managers**

- Consult your HRBP before declining a request made under this circular.
- Watch for workload or wellbeing effects and raise them early.
- Apply the new rule consistently and record any exception in the HRMS.

**HR and Payroll**

- Country HR leads report adoption and issues in the monthly HR review.
- HRBPs hold drop-in sessions for questions.
- The policy team incorporates the change into the next version of the Global HR Policy Manual.

## Impact on related policies

- Payroll will show the change as a separate line on the first payslip after the effective date.
- Country addenda that state a more favourable local rule continue to apply.
- Related allowances, balances and approvals in the HRMS are updated automatically; no new form is needed.

## Communication plan

- The HR helpdesk has been briefed and will tag related tickets with this circular number.
- HRBPs will hold two 30-minute drop-in sessions in each region.
- An intranet article and a short video explainer will be published within a week.

## Monitoring and review

- Any unintended effect on particular groups (for example caregivers or employees with disabilities) will be reported to the CHRO.
- HR will review adoption, helpdesk volumes and feedback after three months.
- Results will be shared in the next Pulse survey read-out.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Does this change apply to fixed-term employees and probationers?**

Yes. It applies to all employees of the entities listed above, pro rata for fixed-term and part-time employees, unless the circular says otherwise.

**Can my manager refuse to apply this change?**

No. Managers apply Company policy consistently; exceptions need HR approval and are recorded in the HRMS.

**What if my appointment letter gives me a better benefit?**

Your appointment letter prevails where it is more favourable (Chapter 1.4).

**Where can I find the updated policy text?**

On the HR intranet and in the HRMS policy library. The next version of the manual will include this change.

**Will this affect my performance rating?**

No. Using an entitlement under Company policy never affects performance ratings.

**Who do I contact with questions?**

Your HRBP, or the HR helpdesk at hr@bragslabs.com, quoting the circular number.

## 8. Relationship to the Global HR Policy Manual

This circular amends manual version 2.0 from the effective date and will be incorporated into version 3.0. Where this circular and the manual differ, this circular prevails from its effective date.
