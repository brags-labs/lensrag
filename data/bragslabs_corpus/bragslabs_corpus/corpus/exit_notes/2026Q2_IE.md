# Exit interview notes — Ireland, Q2 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd whose last working day fell in Q2 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-IE-2026Q2-001
**Last working day:** 12 April 2026 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** People & HR · **Tenure:** 3.9 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Career growth and promotion · **Secondary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They want to move into a lead role and no suitable internal opening was available. The employee received an offer about 28% higher than current fixed pay and felt the April increment did not reflect market rates. They said they valued good onboarding, the inclusive culture. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A clear promotion timeline or an internal move to a lead role.

**Notice and handover.** They were placed on garden leave for the last two weeks.

**In their words.** "It was a practical decision for me at this stage."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Offer more flexibility on office days for long-distance commuters. They felt exit formalities were smooth.

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
