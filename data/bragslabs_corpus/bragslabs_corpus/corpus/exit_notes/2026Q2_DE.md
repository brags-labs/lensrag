# Exit interview notes — Germany, Q2 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs GmbH whose last working day fell in Q2 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-DE-2026Q2-001
**Last working day:** 08 April 2026 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** remote
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** Finance · **Tenure:** 4.6 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Personal relocation · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They felt their pay had fallen behind peers in the market after two cycles of modest increments. They are moving to Bali for family reasons and no Company office is available there. They said they valued supportive colleagues, the buddy programme. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A retention offer was discussed but came too late.

**Notice and handover.** They were placed on garden leave for the last two weeks.

**In their words.** "It is mostly about the next step in my career."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Review compensation bands against the market more often. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
