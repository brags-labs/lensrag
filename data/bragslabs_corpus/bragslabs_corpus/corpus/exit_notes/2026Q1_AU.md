# Exit interview notes — Australia, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-AU-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 10 January 2026 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L1 · **Department:** Finance · **Tenure:** 0.6 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Family reasons · **Secondary reason:** Fully remote offer elsewhere · **Would rejoin:** Yes

**Interviewer notes.** They need to care for an elderly parent and are taking a break from work. They accepted a fully remote role with another employer. They said they valued the wellbeing sessions, flexible hours. The employee was warm and positive throughout the conversation.

**What might have changed the decision.** A longer unpaid break or sabbatical, which they were not yet eligible for.

**Notice and handover.** Part of the notice period was waived by the manager after handover was complete.

**In their words.** "I've learned a lot here and would happily come back."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Review compensation bands against the market more often. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Positive exit; the employee is likely to return in future. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
