# Exit interview notes — Canada, Q2 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. whose last working day fell in Q2 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-CA-2026Q2-001
**Last working day:** 07 April 2026 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** on-site
**Level:** L2 · **Department:** People & HR · **Tenure:** 8.6 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Secondary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They joined when the team worked two office days and felt the move to three days changed the deal they had signed up for. The employee received an offer about 20% higher than current fixed pay and felt the April increment did not reflect market rates. They said they valued the health insurance cover, the inclusive culture. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** Keeping two office days, or a commute-based exception, would have changed the decision.

**Notice and handover.** They served the full notice period and completed a documented handover.

**In their words.** "Honestly, the last few months have been draining."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Keep the learning budget; it is valuable. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
