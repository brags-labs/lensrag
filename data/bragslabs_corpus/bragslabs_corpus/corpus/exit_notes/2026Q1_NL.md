# Exit interview notes — Netherlands, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs B.V. whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-NL-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 13 January 2026 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L5 · **Department:** Design · **Tenure:** 3.6 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Relationship with manager · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They felt their pay had fallen behind peers in the market after two cycles of modest increments. They described frequent changes in priorities and a lack of regular one-to-ones with their manager. They said they valued the learning budget and certifications, the wellbeing sessions. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A retention offer was discussed but came too late.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "Overall it was a fair place to work."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Keep the learning budget; it is valuable. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
