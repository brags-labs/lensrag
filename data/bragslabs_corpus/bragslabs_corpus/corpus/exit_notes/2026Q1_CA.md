# Exit interview notes — Canada, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-CA-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 15 January 2026 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Machine Learning · **Tenure:** 8.6 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Fully remote offer elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They accepted a fully remote role with another employer. They felt the team was understaffed for most of the year. They said they valued supportive colleagues, flexible hours. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A fully remote arrangement, which the role was not eligible for.

**Notice and handover.** The notice shortfall was adjusted against earned leave with HR approval.

**In their words.** "It was a practical decision for me at this stage."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Offer more flexibility on office days for long-distance commuters. 

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
