# Exit interview notes — United Arab Emirates, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-AE-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 02 January 2026 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L6 · **Department:** Quality Engineering · **Tenure:** 1.2 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Higher studies · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They felt their pay had fallen behind peers in the market after two cycles of modest increments. They have been admitted to a full-time master's programme starting next term. They said they valued flexible hours, the wellbeing sessions. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A market correction of their salary during the year might have kept them.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "Overall it was a fair place to work."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hold regular one-to-ones in every team. They felt exit formalities were smooth.

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
