# Exit interview notes — United States, Q2 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Inc. whose last working day fell in Q2 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-US-2026Q2-001
**Last working day:** 01 April 2026 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Boston · **Work mode:** on-site
**Level:** L7 · **Department:** Data Engineering · **Tenure:** 5.5 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Secondary reason:** Career growth and promotion · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** The change to three office days a week from April added 4 hours of commuting a week and they could not make it work with their family routine. They were passed over for promotion in the last cycle and could not see a path to the next level within a year. They said they valued the inclusive culture, supportive colleagues. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**Notice and handover.** Part of the notice period was waived by the manager after handover was complete.

**In their words.** "Honestly, the last few months have been draining."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Keep the learning budget; it is valuable. They mentioned the new expense module as a pain point.

**HRBP assessment.** Avoidable loss; the underlying issue is shared by others in the team. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
