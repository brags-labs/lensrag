# Exit interview notes — United Kingdom, Q3 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd whose last working day fell in Q3 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-UK-2026Q3-001
**Last working day:** 08 July 2026 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Platform Engineering · **Tenure:** 9.2 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Fully remote offer elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Career growth and promotion · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They accepted a fully remote role with another employer. They were passed over for promotion in the last cycle and could not see a path to the next level within a year. They said they valued flexible hours, supportive colleagues. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** Fewer office days.

**Notice and handover.** They were placed on garden leave for the last two weeks.

**In their words.** "I felt the company changed the deal on us."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Offer more flexibility on office days for long-distance commuters. They would recommend BragsLabs to friends for early-career roles.

**HRBP assessment.** Avoidable loss; the underlying issue is shared by others in the team. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
