# Exit interview notes — India, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-IN-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 01 January 2026 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Bengaluru · **Work mode:** remote
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Quality Engineering · **Tenure:** 3.6 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Personal relocation · **Secondary reason:** Family reasons · **Would rejoin:** Yes

**Interviewer notes.** Their spouse has been transferred and they are relocating with the family. They need to care for an elderly parent and are taking a break from work. They said they valued the inclusive culture, the wellbeing sessions. The employee was warm and positive throughout the conversation.

**In their words.** "Great people, I'll miss the team."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Review compensation bands against the market more often. They suggested better recognition for remote employees.

**HRBP assessment.** Unavoidable loss; good relationship maintained and a strong alumni candidate. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
