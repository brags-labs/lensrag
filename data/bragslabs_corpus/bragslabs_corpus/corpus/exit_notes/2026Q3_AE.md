# Exit interview notes — United Arab Emirates, Q3 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC whose last working day fell in Q3 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-AE-2026Q3-001
**Last working day:** 01 July 2026 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** on-site
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Finance · **Tenure:** 6.4 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Secondary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They joined when the team worked two office days and felt the move to three days changed the deal they had signed up for. The employee received an offer about 15% higher than current fixed pay and felt the April increment did not reflect market rates. They said they valued good onboarding, the hackathons. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A remote or flexible arrangement for long-distance commuters.

**Notice and handover.** They were placed on garden leave for the last two weeks.

**In their words.** "Honestly, the last few months have been draining."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Keep the learning budget; it is valuable. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
