# Exit interview notes — Netherlands, Q2 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs B.V. whose last working day fell in Q2 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-NL-2026Q2-001
**Last working day:** 17 April 2026 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** on-site
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Design · **Tenure:** 5.8 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Role scope and interesting work · **Secondary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They wanted to work on more advanced machine learning problems than their current project allows. They spoke about sustained late evenings on a client programme and feeling burnt out. They said they valued the inclusive culture, the wellbeing sessions. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A move to a project with more advanced work.

**Notice and handover.** They served the full notice period and completed a documented handover.

**In their words.** "The new rules made it hard to stay."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Make promotion criteria more transparent. They mentioned the new expense module as a pain point.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.

### Exit interview EXIT-NL-2026Q2-002
**Last working day:** 19 April 2026 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** off-site
**Level:** L2 · **Department:** Data Engineering · **Tenure:** 3.7 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes

**In their words.** "Honestly, the last few months have been draining."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hold regular one-to-ones in every team. They mentioned the new expense module as a pain point.

**HRBP assessment.** Avoidable loss; the underlying issue is shared by others in the team. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
