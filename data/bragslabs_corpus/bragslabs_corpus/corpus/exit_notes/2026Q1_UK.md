# Exit interview notes — United Kingdom, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-UK-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 06 January 2026 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** Data Engineering · **Tenure:** 6.6 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Higher studies · **Secondary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They have been admitted to a full-time master's programme starting next term. They felt the team was understaffed for most of the year. They said they valued the inclusive culture, good onboarding. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** Support for part-time study, although the programme is full-time.

**Notice and handover.** They were placed on garden leave for the last two weeks.

**In their words.** "The new rules made it hard to stay."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hold regular one-to-ones in every team. They felt exit formalities were smooth.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
