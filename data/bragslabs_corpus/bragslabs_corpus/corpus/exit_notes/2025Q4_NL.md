# Exit interview notes — Netherlands, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs B.V. whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-NL-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 02 October 2025 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L1 · **Department:** Cloud Operations · **Tenure:** 3.9 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Personal relocation · **Secondary reason:** Higher studies · **Would rejoin:** Yes

**Interviewer notes.** Their spouse has been transferred and they are relocating with the family. They have been admitted to a full-time master's programme starting next term. They said they valued supportive colleagues, the buddy programme. The employee was warm and positive throughout the conversation.

**What might have changed the decision.** Nothing; the move was for family reasons.

**Notice and handover.** Part of the notice period was waived by the manager after handover was complete.

**In their words.** "It was a tough decision; BragsLabs treated me well."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hire to fill vacancies faster so teams are not stretched. They would recommend BragsLabs to friends for early-career roles.

**HRBP assessment.** Positive exit; the employee is likely to return in future. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
