# Exit interview notes — Ireland, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-IE-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 08 October 2025 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** Platform Engineering · **Tenure:** 7.5 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Secondary reason:** Family reasons · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They spoke about sustained late evenings on a client programme and feeling burnt out. They need to care for an elderly parent and are taking a break from work. They said they valued the health insurance cover, the buddy programme. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A rotation for evening client calls.

**Notice and handover.** They served the full notice period and completed a documented handover.

**In their words.** "I felt the company changed the deal on us."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hire to fill vacancies faster so teams are not stretched. They mentioned the new expense module as a pain point.

**HRBP assessment.** Avoidable loss; the underlying issue is shared by others in the team. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
