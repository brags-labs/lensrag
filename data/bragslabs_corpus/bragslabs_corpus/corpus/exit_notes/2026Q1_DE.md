# Exit interview notes — Germany, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs GmbH whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-DE-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 02 January 2026 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** off-site
**Level:** L2 · **Department:** Platform Engineering · **Tenure:** 1.5 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Higher studies · **Secondary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They have been admitted to a full-time master's programme starting next term. They felt the team was understaffed for most of the year. They said they valued interesting client problems, the learning budget and certifications. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** Nothing; they plan to return after the programme.

**In their words.** "It is mostly about the next step in my career."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Improve the new expense tool. They felt exit formalities were smooth.

**HRBP assessment.** Typical career move at this level. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
