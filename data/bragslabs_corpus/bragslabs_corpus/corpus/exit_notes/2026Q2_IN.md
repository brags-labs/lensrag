### Exit interview EXIT-IN-2026Q2-001

**Level:** L5 · **Department:** People & HR · **Tenure:** 7.8 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Higher studies · **Secondary reason:** Family reasons · **Would rejoin:** Yes
