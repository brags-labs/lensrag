# Exit interview notes — Australia, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-AU-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 06 October 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L6 · **Department:** Machine Learning · **Tenure:** 4.6 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Higher studies · **Secondary reason:** Career growth and promotion · **Would rejoin:** Yes

**Interviewer notes.** They have been admitted to a full-time master's programme starting next term. They want to move into a lead role and no suitable internal opening was available. They said they valued the inclusive culture, flexible hours. The employee was warm and positive throughout the conversation.

**What might have changed the decision.** Nothing; they plan to return after the programme.

**Notice and handover.** The notice shortfall was adjusted against earned leave with HR approval.

**In their words.** "Great people, I'll miss the team."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Offer more flexibility on office days for long-distance commuters. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Positive exit; the employee is likely to return in future. Eligible for rehire: Yes.

### Exit interview EXIT-AU-2025Q4-002
**Last working day:** 13 October 2025 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L5 · **Department:** People & HR · **Tenure:** 8.7 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Secondary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They felt the team was understaffed for most of the year. The change to three office days a week from April added 4 hours of commuting a week and they could not make it work with their family routine. They said they valued good onboarding, supportive colleagues. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** Filling the team's vacancies sooner.

**Notice and handover.** They were placed on garden leave for the last two weeks.

**In their words.** "Honestly, the last few months have been draining."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Keep the learning budget; it is valuable. They mentioned the new expense module as a pain point.

**HRBP assessment.** Avoidable loss; the underlying issue is shared by others in the team. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
