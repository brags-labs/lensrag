# Exit interview notes — Australia, Q2 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd whose last working day fell in Q2 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-AU-2026Q2-001
**Last working day:** 12 April 2026 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** Finance · **Tenure:** 9.3 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Career growth and promotion · **Secondary reason:** Relationship with manager · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They want to move into a lead role and no suitable internal opening was available. They described frequent changes in priorities and a lack of regular one-to-ones with their manager. They said they valued the buddy programme, supportive colleagues. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A clear promotion timeline or an internal move to a lead role.

**Notice and handover.** The notice shortfall was adjusted against earned leave with HR approval.

**In their words.** "Overall it was a fair place to work."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hire to fill vacancies faster so teams are not stretched. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
