# Exit interview notes — Canada, Q3 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. whose last working day fell in Q3 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-CA-2026Q3-001
**Last working day:** 06 July 2026 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** remote
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** Analytics Consulting · **Tenure:** 8.6 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Relationship with manager · **Secondary reason:** Career growth and promotion · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They described frequent changes in priorities and a lack of regular one-to-ones with their manager. They want to move into a lead role and no suitable internal opening was available. They said they valued the wellbeing sessions, interesting client problems. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A change of reporting manager earlier in the year.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "The new rules made it hard to stay."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Communicate policy changes earlier and explain the reasons. They mentioned the new expense module as a pain point.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
