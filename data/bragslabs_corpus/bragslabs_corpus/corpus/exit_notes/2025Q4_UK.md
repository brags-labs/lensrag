# Exit interview notes — United Kingdom, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-UK-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 16 October 2025 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** on-site
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** Sales · **Tenure:** 6.1 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Fully remote offer elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Relationship with manager · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** A competitor offered fully remote work, which was the deciding factor. They described frequent changes in priorities and a lack of regular one-to-ones with their manager. They said they valued flexible hours, interesting client problems. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** Fewer office days.

**Notice and handover.** Part of the notice period was waived by the manager after handover was complete.

**In their words.** "I didn't feel heard when I raised concerns."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Review compensation bands against the market more often. They felt exit formalities were smooth.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
