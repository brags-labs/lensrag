# Exit interview notes — United Arab Emirates, Q2 2026

### Exit interview EXIT-AE-2026Q2-001

**Level:** L2 · **Department:** Product Management · **Tenure:** 8.7 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Role scope and interesting work · **Secondary reason:** Higher studies · **Would rejoin:** Maybe
