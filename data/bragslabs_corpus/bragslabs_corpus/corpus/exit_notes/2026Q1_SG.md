# Exit interview notes — Singapore, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-SG-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 06 January 2026 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** off-site
**Level:** L2 · **Department:** Product Management · **Tenure:** 9.5 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Family reasons · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They felt their pay had fallen behind peers in the market after two cycles of modest increments. They need to care for an elderly parent and are taking a break from work. They said they valued flexible hours, supportive colleagues. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A market correction of their salary during the year might have kept them.

**Notice and handover.** Part of the notice period was waived by the manager after handover was complete.

**In their words.** "I felt the company changed the deal on us."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hold regular one-to-ones in every team. 

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
