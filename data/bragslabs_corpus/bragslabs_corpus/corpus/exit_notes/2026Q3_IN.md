# Exit interview notes — India, Q3 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. whose last working day fell in Q3 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-IN-2026Q3-001
**Last working day:** 04 July 2026 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Hyderabad · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L2 · **Department:** Platform Engineering · **Tenure:** 6.0 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Career growth and promotion · **Secondary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They were passed over for promotion in the last cycle and could not see a path to the next level within a year. They joined when the team worked two office days and felt the move to three days changed the deal they had signed up for. They said they valued good onboarding, the learning budget and certifications. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** Earlier visibility of internal openings at the next level.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "I didn't feel heard when I raised concerns."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hire to fill vacancies faster so teams are not stretched. They suggested better recognition for remote employees.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
