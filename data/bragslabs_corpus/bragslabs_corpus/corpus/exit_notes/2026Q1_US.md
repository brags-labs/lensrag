# Exit interview notes — United States, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Inc. whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-US-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 04 January 2026 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Austin · **Work mode:** remote
**Level:** L6 · **Department:** Finance · **Tenure:** 1.9 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Personal relocation · **Secondary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Would rejoin:** Yes

**Interviewer notes.** They are moving to Munich for family reasons and no Company office is available there. The change to three office days a week from April added 4 hours of commuting a week and they could not make it work with their family routine. They said they valued flexible hours, supportive colleagues. The employee was warm and positive throughout the conversation.

**What might have changed the decision.** A remote option in the new city, which the role did not allow.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "Great people, I'll miss the team."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Review compensation bands against the market more often. They would recommend BragsLabs to friends for early-career roles.

**HRBP assessment.** Positive exit; the employee is likely to return in future. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
