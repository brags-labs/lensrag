# Exit interview notes — Ireland, Q1 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd whose last working day fell in Q1 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-IE-2026Q1-001
**Last working day:** 12 January 2026 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L2 · **Department:** Platform Engineering · **Tenure:** 9.2 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** The employee received an offer about 18% higher than current fixed pay and felt the April increment did not reflect market rates. They spoke about sustained late evenings on a client programme and feeling burnt out. They said they valued good onboarding, supportive colleagues. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A market correction of their salary during the year might have kept them.

**Notice and handover.** Part of the notice period was waived by the manager after handover was complete.

**In their words.** "It is mostly about the next step in my career."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hold regular one-to-ones in every team. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
