# Exit interview notes — Netherlands, Q3 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs B.V. whose last working day fell in Q3 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-NL-2026Q3-001
**Last working day:** 11 July 2026 · **Country:** Netherlands · **Office:** Amsterdam · **Work mode:** on-site
**Level:** L1 · **Department:** Data Engineering · **Tenure:** 3.9 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Family reasons · **Secondary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They need to care for an elderly parent and are taking a break from work. They felt the team was understaffed for most of the year. They said they valued the buddy programme, the hackathons. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** Part-time work for a period.

**Notice and handover.** They served the full notice period and completed a documented handover.

**In their words.** "Overall it was a fair place to work."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Keep the learning budget; it is valuable. They would recommend BragsLabs to friends for early-career roles.

**HRBP assessment.** Typical career move at this level. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
