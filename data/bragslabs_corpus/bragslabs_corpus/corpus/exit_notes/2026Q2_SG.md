# Exit interview notes — Singapore, Q2 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. whose last working day fell in Q2 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-SG-2026Q2-001
**Last working day:** 03 April 2026 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Quality Engineering · **Tenure:** 4.6 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Career growth and promotion · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** The employee received an offer about 18% higher than current fixed pay and felt the April increment did not reflect market rates. They were passed over for promotion in the last cycle and could not see a path to the next level within a year. They said they valued flexible hours, the wellbeing sessions. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A retention offer was discussed but came too late.

**Notice and handover.** They were placed on garden leave for the last two weeks.

**In their words.** "Overall it was a fair place to work."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Communicate policy changes earlier and explain the reasons. 

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
