# Exit interview notes — United Arab Emirates, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs FZ-LLC whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-AE-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 21 October 2025 · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Office:** Dubai · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L2 · **Department:** Sales · **Tenure:** 7.9 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Fully remote offer elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Would rejoin:** Yes

**Interviewer notes.** They accepted a fully remote role with another employer. They joined when the team worked two office days and felt the move to three days changed the deal they had signed up for. They said they valued the inclusive culture, the buddy programme. The employee was warm and positive throughout the conversation.

**What might have changed the decision.** Fewer office days.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "I've learned a lot here and would happily come back."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Review compensation bands against the market more often. 

**HRBP assessment.** Positive exit; the employee is likely to return in future. Eligible for rehire: Yes.

### Exit interview EXIT-AE-2025Q4-002

**Level:** L2 · **Department:** Finance · **Tenure:** 7.6 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Secondary reason:** Relationship with manager · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**In their words.** "It is mostly about the next step in my career."

**HRBP assessment.** Typical career move at this level. Eligible for rehire: Yes.

### Exit interview EXIT-AE-2025Q4-003

**Level:** L3 · **Department:** Sales · **Tenure:** 7.1 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They felt their pay had fallen behind peers in the market after two cycles of modest increments. They joined when the team worked two office days and felt the move to three days changed the deal they had signed up for. They said they valued flexible hours, good onboarding. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A market correction of their salary during the year might have kept them.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
