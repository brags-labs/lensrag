# Exit interview notes — Ireland, Q3 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Ireland Ltd whose last working day fell in Q3 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-IE-2026Q3-001
**Last working day:** 02 July 2026 · **Country:** Ireland · **Office:** Dublin · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L6 · **Department:** Analytics Consulting · **Tenure:** 2.3 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** The employee received an offer about 25% higher than current fixed pay and felt the April increment did not reflect market rates. They spoke about sustained late evenings on a client programme and feeling burnt out. They said they valued the wellbeing sessions, the learning budget and certifications. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A retention offer was discussed but came too late.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "I didn't feel heard when I raised concerns."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Communicate policy changes earlier and explain the reasons. They would recommend BragsLabs to friends for early-career roles.

**HRBP assessment.** Avoidable loss; the underlying issue is shared by others in the team. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
