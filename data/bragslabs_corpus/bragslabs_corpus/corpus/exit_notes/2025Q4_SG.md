# Exit interview notes — Singapore, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-SG-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 11 October 2025 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** Cloud Operations · **Tenure:** 7.3 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Relationship with manager · **Secondary reason:** Role scope and interesting work · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They described frequent changes in priorities and a lack of regular one-to-ones with their manager. Their role had become repetitive maintenance work. They said they valued supportive colleagues, flexible hours. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A change of reporting manager earlier in the year.

**Notice and handover.** They were placed on garden leave for the last two weeks.

**In their words.** "I felt the company changed the deal on us."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Offer more flexibility on office days for long-distance commuters. They mentioned the new expense module as a pain point.

**HRBP assessment.** Avoidable loss; the underlying issue is shared by others in the team. Eligible for rehire: Yes.

### Exit interview EXIT-SG-2025Q4-002
**Last working day:** 16 October 2025 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L1 · **Department:** Finance · **Tenure:** 5.4 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** The employee received an offer about 25% higher than current fixed pay and felt the April increment did not reflect market rates. They spoke about sustained late evenings on a client programme and feeling burnt out. They said they valued supportive colleagues, the inclusive culture. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A market correction of their salary during the year might have kept them.

**Notice and handover.** The notice shortfall was adjusted against earned leave with HR approval.

**In their words.** "Overall it was a fair place to work."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Improve the new expense tool. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
