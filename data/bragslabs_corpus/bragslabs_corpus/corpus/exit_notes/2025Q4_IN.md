# Exit interview notes — India, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs India Pvt. Ltd. whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-IN-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 02 October 2025 · **Country:** India · **Office:** Pune · **Work mode:** on-site
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Client Delivery · **Tenure:** 7.2 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Relationship with manager · **Secondary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They felt their contributions were not recognised by their manager. They felt the team was understaffed for most of the year. They said they valued interesting client problems, the health insurance cover. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A change of reporting manager earlier in the year.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "I didn't feel heard when I raised concerns."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Improve the new expense tool. They mentioned the new expense module as a pain point.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
