# Exit interview notes — Germany, Q3 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs GmbH whose last working day fell in Q3 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-DE-2026Q3-001
**Last working day:** 01 July 2026 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L1 · **Department:** Platform Engineering · **Tenure:** 7.3 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Relationship with manager · **Secondary reason:** Role scope and interesting work · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They described frequent changes in priorities and a lack of regular one-to-ones with their manager. Their role had become repetitive maintenance work. They said they valued flexible hours, the wellbeing sessions. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** Regular one-to-ones and clearer feedback.

**Notice and handover.** The notice shortfall was adjusted against earned leave with HR approval.

**In their words.** "I felt the company changed the deal on us."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Review compensation bands against the market more often. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: No.
