# Exit interview notes — United Kingdom, Q2 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs UK Ltd whose last working day fell in Q2 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-UK-2026Q2-001
**Last working day:** 03 April 2026 · **Country:** United Kingdom · **Office:** London · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** Quality Engineering · **Tenure:** 1.4 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Family reasons · **Secondary reason:** Fully remote offer elsewhere · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They need to care for an elderly parent and are taking a break from work. A competitor offered fully remote work, which was the deciding factor. They said they valued flexible hours, the learning budget and certifications. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** Part-time work for a period.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "Overall it was a fair place to work."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Offer more flexibility on office days for long-distance commuters. 

**HRBP assessment.** Partly avoidable; the employee had a clear external opportunity. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
