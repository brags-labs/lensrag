# Exit interview notes — United States, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Inc. whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-US-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 03 October 2025 · **Country:** United States · **Office:** Austin · **Work mode:** on-site
**Level:** L3 · **Department:** People & HR · **Tenure:** 7.8 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They felt their pay had fallen behind peers in the market after two cycles of modest increments. With the new attendance requirement and a long commute, they accepted a role with more flexibility. They said they valued the hackathons, the health insurance cover. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** A retention offer was discussed but came too late.

**Notice and handover.** The notice shortfall was adjusted against earned leave with HR approval.

**In their words.** "The new rules made it hard to stay."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Review compensation bands against the market more often. They would recommend BragsLabs to friends for early-career roles.

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
