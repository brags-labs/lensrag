# Exit interview notes — Germany, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs GmbH whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-DE-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 01 October 2025 · **Country:** Germany · **Office:** Berlin · **Work mode:** off-site
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Finance · **Tenure:** 8.0 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Fully remote offer elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Would rejoin:** Yes

**Interviewer notes.** A competitor offered fully remote work, which was the deciding factor. With the new attendance requirement and a long commute, they accepted a role with more flexibility. They said they valued the hackathons, the learning budget and certifications. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A fully remote arrangement, which the role was not eligible for.

**Notice and handover.** The notice shortfall was adjusted against earned leave with HR approval.

**In their words.** "Overall it was a fair place to work."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Communicate policy changes earlier and explain the reasons. They felt exit formalities were smooth.

**HRBP assessment.** Typical career move at this level. Eligible for rehire: Yes.

### Exit interview EXIT-DE-2025Q4-002

**Level:** L6 · **Department:** Data Engineering · **Tenure:** 2.0 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Fully remote offer elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Career growth and promotion · **Would rejoin:** No
