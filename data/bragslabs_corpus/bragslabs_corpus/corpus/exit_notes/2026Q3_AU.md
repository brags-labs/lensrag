# Exit interview notes — Australia, Q3 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Pty Ltd whose last working day fell in Q3 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-AU-2026Q3-001
**Last working day:** 01 July 2026 · **Country:** Australia · **Office:** Sydney · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Machine Learning · **Tenure:** 3.8 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Relationship with manager · **Secondary reason:** Office attendance requirement · **Would rejoin:** No

**Interviewer notes.** They described frequent changes in priorities and a lack of regular one-to-ones with their manager. With the new attendance requirement and a long commute, they accepted a role with more flexibility. They said they valued supportive colleagues, the hackathons. The conversation was tense at times and the employee was clearly frustrated.

**What might have changed the decision.** Regular one-to-ones and clearer feedback.

**Notice and handover.** Handover was completed early and the remaining notice was spent on documentation.

**In their words.** "Honestly, the last few months have been draining."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Communicate policy changes earlier and explain the reasons. 

**HRBP assessment.** Signals a retention risk in the same team that needs follow-up. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
