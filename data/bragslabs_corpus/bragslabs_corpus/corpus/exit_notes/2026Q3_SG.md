# Exit interview notes — Singapore, Q3 2026

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Pte. Ltd. whose last working day fell in Q3 2026. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-SG-2026Q3-001
**Last working day:** 03 July 2026 · **Country:** Singapore · **Office:** Singapore · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L2 · **Department:** Platform Engineering · **Tenure:** 2.4 years · **Regretted loss:** Yes
**Primary reason:** Workload and burnout · **Secondary reason:** Fully remote offer elsewhere · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** They spoke about sustained late evenings on a client programme and feeling burnt out. They accepted a fully remote role with another employer. They said they valued the buddy programme, the wellbeing sessions. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A rotation for evening client calls.

**Notice and handover.** The notice shortfall was adjusted against earned leave with HR approval.

**In their words.** "It was a practical decision for me at this stage."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Offer more flexibility on office days for long-distance commuters. They suggested better recognition for remote employees.

**HRBP assessment.** Typical career move at this level. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
