# Exit interview notes — Canada, Q4 2025

Notes from exit interviews with employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. whose last working day fell in Q4 2025. Interviews were held by the HR business partner, in person or by video.

### Exit interview EXIT-CA-2025Q4-001
**Last working day:** 05 October 2025 · **Country:** Canada · **Office:** Toronto · **Work mode:** hybrid
**Level:** L4 · **Department:** Machine Learning · **Tenure:** 3.0 years · **Regretted loss:** No
**Primary reason:** Better compensation elsewhere · **Secondary reason:** Personal relocation · **Would rejoin:** Maybe

**Interviewer notes.** The employee received an offer about 30% higher than current fixed pay and felt the April increment did not reflect market rates. They are moving to Vancouver for family reasons and no Company office is available there. They said they valued the learning budget and certifications, supportive colleagues. The conversation was calm and matter-of-fact.

**What might have changed the decision.** A market correction of their salary during the year might have kept them.

**Notice and handover.** They served the full notice period and completed a documented handover.

**In their words.** "It is mostly about the next step in my career."

**Suggestion for BragsLabs.** Hold regular one-to-ones in every team. They also asked for faster settlement of final dues.

**HRBP assessment.** Typical career move at this level. Eligible for rehire: Yes.
