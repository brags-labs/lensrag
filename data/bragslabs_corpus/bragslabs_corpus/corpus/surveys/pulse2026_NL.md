# Pulse survey 2026 — open comments, Netherlands

Verbatim open-text comments from the BragsLabs Pulse Survey run from 8 to 26 June 2026 for employees of BragsLabs B.V. Respondents answered "How likely are you to recommend BragsLabs as a place to work?" (0–10) and could add a comment on a topic of their choice. Comments are unedited apart from removing names.

**PULSE26-NL-0001** · Amsterdam · Design · L1 · on-site · tenure <1 year · likelihood to recommend: 2/10  
*Topic tagged by respondent:* Tools and systems  
The new expense module is painful; claims take twice as long. It makes me less likely to recommend BragsLabs to friends. Several of us feel the same.

**PULSE26-NL-0002** · Amsterdam · Cloud Operations · L3 · hybrid · tenure 3–5 years · likelihood to recommend: 6/10
