# Pulse survey 2026 — open comments, Germany

Verbatim open-text comments from the BragsLabs Pulse Survey run from 8 to 26 June 2026 for employees of BragsLabs GmbH. Respondents answered "How likely are you to recommend BragsLabs as a place to work?" (0–10) and could add a comment on a topic of their choice. Comments are unedited apart from removing names.

**PULSE26-DE-0001** · Berlin · Client Delivery · L1 · hybrid · tenure 1–3 years · likelihood to recommend: 1/10  
*Topic tagged by respondent:* Pay and benefits  
Allowances are paid late and the payroll team is hard to reach. It makes me less likely to recommend BragsLabs to friends. Please fix this.

**PULSE26-DE-0002** · Berlin · Data Engineering · L2 · off-site · tenure 3–5 years · likelihood to recommend: 8/10  
*Topic tagged by respondent:* Wellbeing  
Wellbeing allowance is nice but I rarely think to claim it. I would like clearer communication on what is changing and why. Hope this helps.

**PULSE26-DE-0003** · Berlin · Finance · L7 · hybrid · tenure 5+ years · likelihood to recommend: 10/10  
*Topic tagged by respondent:* Learning and development
