# Pulse survey 2026 — open comments, United States

Verbatim open-text comments from the BragsLabs Pulse Survey run from 8 to 26 June 2026 for employees of BragsLabs Inc. Respondents answered "How likely are you to recommend BragsLabs as a place to work?" (0–10) and could add a comment on a topic of their choice. Comments are unedited apart from removing names.

**PULSE26-US-0001** · Boston · Finance · L4 · off-site · tenure 5+ years · likelihood to recommend: 0/10  
*Topic tagged by respondent:* Tools and systems

**PULSE26-US-0002** · Boston · Platform Engineering · L3 · off-site · tenure <1 year · likelihood to recommend: 0/10
