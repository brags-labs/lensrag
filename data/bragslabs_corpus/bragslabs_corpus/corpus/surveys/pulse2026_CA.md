# Pulse survey 2026 — open comments, Canada

Verbatim open-text comments from the BragsLabs Pulse Survey run from 8 to 26 June 2026 for employees of BragsLabs Canada Inc. Respondents answered "How likely are you to recommend BragsLabs as a place to work?" (0–10) and could add a comment on a topic of their choice. Comments are unedited apart from removing names.
