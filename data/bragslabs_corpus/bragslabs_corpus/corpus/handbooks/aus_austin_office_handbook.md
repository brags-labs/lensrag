# Austin office handbook

*BragsLabs Inc. · 1100 Congress Lane, Floor 9, Austin, TX 78701 · last reviewed 15 October 2026.*

## Welcome

Welcome to the BragsLabs Austin office. The office opened in 2019 and is home to about 310 employees across 3 floors. It serves teams in Platform Engineering, Data Engineering, Product Management, People & HR. This handbook covers practical information for working here; HR policies are in the Global HR Policy Manual.

## Opening hours and access

The office is open from 7:00 am to 11:00 pm on working days; 24x7 support teams have access around the clock. Security is staffed at reception at all times. Wear your ID card visibly, never tailgate, and report a lost card within 2 hours; a replacement fee of USD 10 applies after the first loss.

## Hybrid working in this office

Hybrid employees attend the office 3 days a week, including their team's anchor days. Book desks in the desk-booking app at least one working day ahead; bookings not checked in by 11:00 am are released. Team neighbourhoods are allocated on the east wing. Quiet zones are marked in green; phone booths are available on every floor.

## Leave reminders for this office

Employees based here receive 20 days of paid annual leave under Company policy and 11 public holidays.

## Getting here

The office is a 3-minute walk from the nearest train station. Cycle parking and showers are available. Parking is allocated by Admin, with priority for persons with disabilities, pregnant employees and late-shift staff.

## Food and facilities

The kitchen and café serves breakfast from 8:00 to 10:00 am and lunch from 12:30 to 2:30 pm. Meals are subsidised on office days. Wellness rooms can be booked in 30-minute slots for rest, nursing or medical needs.

The on-site gym is open from 6:00 am to 10:00 pm; book through the facilities app. Parents may claim the childcare allowance described in Chapter 16.9.

## IT support

The IT service desk is on floor 3 and open from 8:30 am to 7:00 pm. Report lost or stolen devices within 2 hours to security@bragslabs.com. Guest Wi-Fi is available; Company devices must use the corporate network or VPN.

## Health, safety and emergencies

There are 6 trained fire wardens and at least one first-aider on every floor. Fire drills take place every 6 months. The assembly point is the front car park. The nearest hospital is Lakeside General, about 7 km away. In an emergency call the local emergency number and then the Company emergency helpline (+1 617 555 0100).

## Visitors and accessibility

Register visitors in the visitor system one working day ahead; visitors are escorted at all times and may not enter secure areas without approval. The office has step-free access, accessible washrooms on every floor and lifts with audio and Braille.

## Community and sustainability

Employee resource groups meet monthly in this office. The office runs a zero single-use plastic programme, and a green team organises volunteering days twice a year.

## Office FAQ

**Can I bring a guest for lunch?** Yes, register them as a visitor; guests are escorted and pay for meals.

**What if I feel unwell at work?** Contact a first-aider or reception; the wellness room is available and reception can arrange transport home.

**Where do I collect deliveries?** Personal deliveries are not accepted at reception; business parcels are logged by the mailroom.

**Can I work late?** Yes; inform security if you will stay after 9:00 pm, and use Company transport where applicable.

**How do I report a facilities issue?** Raise a ticket in the facilities app; urgent issues go to reception.
