## Opening hours and access

The office is open from 7:00 am to 11:00 pm on working days; 24x7 support teams have access around the clock. Security is staffed at reception at all times. Wear your ID card visibly, never tailgate, and report a lost card within 2 hours; a replacement fee of ₹300 applies after the first loss.

## Hybrid working in this office

Hybrid employees attend the office 3 days a week, including their team's anchor days. Book desks in the desk-booking app at least one working day ahead; bookings not checked in by 11:00 am are released. Team neighbourhoods are allocated on the east wing. Quiet zones are marked in green; phone booths are available on every floor.

## Leave reminders for this office

Employees based here receive 18 days of paid annual leave under Company policy and 10 public holidays.

## Shifts and transport

Late and night shifts receive door-to-door cabs booked through the transport app. The night shift allowance is ₹600 per night worked. Women employees on shifts ending after 8:00 pm receive escorted, GPS-tracked cabs and a check-in call after drop-off. Shuttles run on 14 fixed routes for the general shift, leaving the office at 6:45 pm and 7:30 pm.

## Food and facilities

The canteen serves breakfast from 8:00 to 10:00 am and lunch from 12:30 to 2:30 pm. Meals are subsidised on office days. Wellness rooms can be booked in 30-minute slots for rest, nursing or medical needs.

The on-site gym is open from 6:00 am to 10:00 pm; book through the facilities app. The crèche takes children from 6 months to 6 years; places are allocated by date of request.

## IT support

The IT service desk is on floor 3 and open from 8:30 am to 7:00 pm. Report lost or stolen devices within 2 hours to security@bragslabs.com. Guest Wi-Fi is available; Company devices must use the corporate network or VPN.

## Health, safety and emergencies

There are 14 trained fire wardens and at least one first-aider on every floor. Fire drills take place every 6 months. The assembly point is the open ground behind the building. The nearest hospital is St. Brendan's Clinic, about 3 km away. In an emergency call the local emergency number and then the Company emergency helpline (+1 617 555 0100).

## Visitors and accessibility

Register visitors in the visitor system one working day ahead; visitors are escorted at all times and may not enter secure areas without approval. The office has step-free access, accessible washrooms on every floor and lifts with audio and Braille.

## Local holidays

Republic Day, Holi, Ugadi / Gudi Padwa, May Day, Independence Day, Ganesh Chaturthi, Gandhi Jayanti, Dussehra, Diwali, Christmas.

## Community and sustainability

Employee resource groups meet monthly in this office. The office runs a e-waste collection programme, and a green team organises volunteering days twice a year.

## Office FAQ

**Can I bring a guest for lunch?** Yes, register them as a visitor; guests are escorted and pay for meals.

**What if I feel unwell at work?** Contact a first-aider or reception; the wellness room is available and reception can arrange transport home.

**Where do I collect deliveries?** Personal deliveries are not accepted at reception; business parcels are logged by the mailroom.

**Can I work late?** Yes; inform security if you will stay after 9:00 pm, and use Company transport where applicable.

**How do I report a facilities issue?** Raise a ticket in the facilities app; urgent issues go to reception.
